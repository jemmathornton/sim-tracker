# Families from older Sims games, for your Sims 4 save (29 Oct 2026)

Research only. Nothing on the site or in your plan has changed.

This covers every premade household from the older games: The Sims and its expansions, The Sims 2 and its expansions, The Sims 3 with its expansion worlds and its eleven store worlds, the console and handheld versions, and The Sims Stories. That’s **683 households** from 593 Sims Wiki family pages. For each one: who they are, their story, whether the Sims 4 has them, whether they’re already in your plan, and a verdict.

**How it was checked**
- **Lore:** the Sims Wiki (read through its API), in my own words. The Sims 4 is officially its own timeline, so older-game facts only count where you want them to; where the Sims 4’s own family trees disagree (for example, Darren Dreamer is dead in the Sims 4), the Sims 4 wins.
- **Sims 4:** EA’s premades from your game files; Maxis’s own Gallery uploads (creator “Maxis”); and a Gallery search for every household, sorted by downloads. The Gallery’s search leaves out uploads tagged as custom content. A community pick is the most downloaded upload whose Sims have the original first and last names, preferring one that needs nothing you lack. I couldn’t look at faces, so “faithful” means the right people, names and ages, not a likeness check.
- **Packs:** “lacks …” lists the packs and kits an upload uses that you don’t own (Lovestruck, Enchanted by Nature, Vampires, Werewolves, Journey to Batuu, My Wedding Stories and most kits). You can still download it; the game swaps out what you lack (the wiki notes Maxis’s Newbies arrive without their dog if you don’t have Cats & Dogs).
- **Your plan:** every world’s households and residents (the 28 Oct snapshot) and the late relatives in `data/people.json`.
- **Homes:** most free homes are already suggested for someone in `for-jemma/homes-proposal.md`. The worlds below are where each family fits by lore; where that world has no free home, it says so.

**Verdicts:** **Bring in** (strong lore reason and a good Gallery copy), **Maybe** (fits, but optional), **Already in your plan**, **Skip** (no story, a younger copy of Sims you already have, dead in the Sims 4’s trees, or needs packs you don’t own).

## The Sims 2: Strangetown

Your plan already has the Curious brothers (Springscape and Granada Place) and the Smiths (Sultry Springside) in Oasis Springs, and the Sims 4’s StrangerVille is EA’s desert echo of Strangetown. **Grunt**: General Buzz and his sons Tank, Ripp and Buck. **Bring in → StrangerVille.**
- Lore: a military household run like a boot camp. Tank wants to be his father; Ripp wants romance; Buck is left out. Buzz’s wife Lyla divorced him and later died in a mysterious electrocution. Buzz and Tank can’t stand the Curious brothers and the alien Smiths, but Ripp and Buck are friendly with Johnny and Jill Smith.
- Sims 4: Maxis’s “The Grunt Family” (38,653 downloads, nothing you lack) adds Lyla back in. Maxis released it for StrangerVille’s launch. Without Lyla: “The Grunt Family” by strangest_tomato (2,438, nothing you lack).

Second choice: Oasis Springs, where their rivals the Curious brothers and Smiths live (three 2-bed homes are free there, a squeeze for five).
- MCCC links: already listed in the homes proposal.

**Beaker**: Loki, Circe and their test subject, Nervous Subject. **Bring in Loki and Circe (and the twins) → StrangerVille or Oasis Springs.**
- Lore: mad scientists in a hilltop mansion near the alien crash site, keeping Nervous in a basement cell. Circe’s first love was Vidcund Curious, hence the Beaker–Curious rivalry. Loki’s younger sister is Erin (Singles household).

**Specter**: Olive (elder) and her niece Ophelia Nigmos (teen). **Bring in Ophelia → Ravenwood.**
- Lore: Olive has buried several husbands and is about to die; her fortune goes to her lost son Nervous rather than to Ophelia. In the Sims 4 (Life & Death) Olive is younger, engaged to Layne Coffin, and Nyon was never taken away.

- Lore: the Capps are matrilineal (husbands take the wife’s name) and feud with the Montys. Hermia is the youngest; she has crushes on both Mercutio Monty and Puck Summerdream, but wants Puck.
- Sims 4: Tybalt, Juliette and Consort (a ghost) are EA’s; Hermia is missing. “Hermia Capp Sims2” by Spandaria (469, teen, nothing you lack); “Hermia Capp (TS2)” by simgremlinyt (152, young adult, nothing you lack).

**Capp aunts, uncle and cousins** (two family-bin households): Goneril and her husband Albany with Miranda, Hal, Desdemona and Ariel; Regan and her husband Cornwall with her brother Kent. **Maybe → Ondarion; Kent is the best of them.**
