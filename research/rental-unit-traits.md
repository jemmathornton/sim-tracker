# Trait ideas for rental units with none (overnight 6–7 Nov)

**Every idea is a suggestion.** Each fits who lives in that unit in your plan: their aspiration, traits or job. Every trait named is allowed on a rental unit, checked against the game's own rules. Each unit holds up to 3 traits, and 1–2 is fine (your rule).

What the traits used here do (from research/lot-trait-ideas.md):
- **Skills:** Natural Light (painting faster), Home Studio (painting and writing), Science Lair (logic faster), Fast Internet (computer skills), Bracing Breezes (fitness), Great Soil (gardening), Peace and Quiet (reading skill faster; tension fades).
- **Social:** Party Place (friendly chats go better), Convivial (chats and selling).
- **Home:** Homey (Sims feel at home), Good Schools (helps the children who live there), Cat Hangout (local cats visit).

## Oasis Springs: Rio Verde Retirement Home (units 2–6)
- **Who:** the four elders from Maxis's "Everyday Sims 4", one per unit once you've split them up, plus one spare unit.
- **Idea, every unit:** **Peace and Quiet** and **Homey**. That's calm for elders, and Homey matches unit 1 (which has Sunny Aspect and Homey).
- Once you know each elder's hobbies, swap one trait for theirs: Great Soil for a gardener, Home Studio for a painter.
