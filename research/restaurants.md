# Restaurants, world by world (16 Oct)

For the TODO item "Restaurants (missed in the 15 Oct pass)". Write-up only; nothing is changed. Sources: her plan, the game's lot descriptions and the Sims Wiki pages (via the world notes in research/worlds/ and research/ondarion.md).

**Where it stands:** 14 restaurants, against my realistic 18–25 for 25 towns. That range was set before today's talk showed how much else feeds Sims: Community Markets with shared kitchens, pubs that serve food, cafés, Thrift and Boba Stores, open-world food stalls, school cafeterias. Like parks and playgrounds, the restaurant range could reasonably come down, to about 12–18. That's your call.

Already judged fine without a restaurant (15 Oct): **Sulani** (island vibes, The Sand Bar), **Henford-on-Bagley** (The Gnome's Arms serves food) and **Tomarang** (the Night Market, Ro Kaya Rockside's market, The Screaming Gecko's food).

| **Ondarion** | Zanbira Market (shared kitchen, Dambele's soups), Ye Olde Voyager (bar), La Vivace (lounge) | Yes: Giardino Del Grandioso, Sankofa Residence, Fisherman's Hut, The Lair in the Open-Air | **The one real chance.** A fine-dining restaurant at Giardino Del Grandioso, a seafood shack at Fisherman's Hut, or Ye Olde Voyager as a tavern (Restaurant/Bar). See research/ondarion.md |

## In short

- Seven of the eight are fine without one, given their character and what already feeds them.
- **Ondarion** is the only world where a restaurant would both fill a gap and have room. It's best decided in the Ondarion discussion.
- If you lower the range to about 12–18, 14 (or 15 with Ondarion) sits comfortably inside it.
