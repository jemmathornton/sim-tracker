Report only. Nothing in `data/` or her files was changed.

## At a glance

- **How an owner is recorded:** the lot’s `residents` holds the owning household’s name. On a venue lot the site then says “Owned by”. An owner from another world works too (the Newcrest Kirklands own Magnolia Mall), as long as that family has Sims in only one household.
- **The Llama Picturehouse** (Magnolia Promenade): the LeBlancs (Newcrest), Venessa Jeong or Diego Lobo (San Myshuno). Keeping it a plain Bar is still fine. A Bar is a community lot, so an owner can still run a business there later.
- **Plumbob Pictures Museum** (Del Sol Valley): Judith Ward first (no change). Johnny Zest or Holly Alto as bonus-story picks.
- **Canalside Daycare** (Nordhaven): the Koopmans’ daughter (one new Sim), the Munches (moving from Windenburg) or Bruno Hoffman (homeless, with a lore link).
- **Greenwood Hamlet Residence** (Innisgreen): not really a clash. Owners on venue lots are recorded the same way everywhere (Canal Corner, The Portsmouth Promenade). Only the bracket note in `residents` is unusual.
- **Daniel and Mary-Sue Pleasant:** Maxis’s Gallery household (22 Feb 2019; about 65,000 downloads). Both are adults. In it, the twins are teens.
- **Where they could live:** Mossy Lane is the twins’ “old family home” in EA’s own bio, which is the best lore fit. Other options are Spring Steppes unit 3 (next door to the twins), Cordelia’s Secret Cottage or The Olde Mill (Henford), or Bedlington Barn (Brindleton Bay).
- **“Pleasant Rosebush”:** no house by that name in her data, on the Wiki or on the Gallery. Maxis uploaded no house with the family. Their known homes are 215 Sim Lane (Sims 2, Pleasantview) and 1 Ocean Overlook (Sims 3 honeymoon, Sunlit Tides).

## Task A: owners

### How owners work in her data and in the game

- **In her data:**
  - A venue lot’s `residents` = the owner household (Canal Corner: “Tinker”, Portsmouth Promenade: “Greenburg”, Magnolia Mall: “Kirkland”).
  - The app joins the venue to that family’s home in the same world. If they have no home there, it joins the one household of that family elsewhere that has Sims.
  - For a Small Business Residential lot, `residents` = the household that lives there (Fisherman’s Wharf: Johansson).
- **In the game (Businesses & Hobbies, Sims Wiki “Small business”):**
  - A business can run on the home lot or on **any community lot the household owns**. It can’t run on a Residential Rental.
  - Up to 5 activities. The business opens only when its owner opens it.
  - **One open business per household.** So Sims who already run one (the Lodens, Johanssons, Koopmans, Sarpongs, Kirklands, Greenburgs, Tinkers) are left out below.
  - Useful activities:
    - Cinema: **Watch Movies on TV**, **Eat Popcorn**, **Drink Bar Drinks**.
    - Museum: **View Collectibles**, **View Art**, **Acting Routines**, **Attend Classes and Lectures**, **Drink Coffee**.
    - Daycare: **Play with Infant & Toddler Toys**, **Play With Toys**, **Do Homework**, **Use Activity Table**, **Play On Playground Equipment**.
  - The game even has a **Llama** business logo.
  - **Daycare:** there is no official daycare type. Players set the target customers to *supervised* infants, toddlers or children. Parents then drop them off (community guides).
  - **Not confirmed:** whether every owner has to stay on the lot (the Wiki says so only for food). Also whether a household can own a venue in another world. Check both in game.
