# Story ideas: lore-friendly hooks for each world (overnight 17 Oct)

> **Ideas only, for you to pick from.** None of this is your story until you say so. Nothing in `site/`, `data/` or `tools/` is changed.

- **What this is:** 2–4 small story hooks per owned world, built from what your plan already has (empty homes, homeless townies, ownerless businesses, undecided lots, families you moved) and from loose ends in EA’s own lore.
- **Sources:** the Sims Wiki (through its API, 17 Oct; pages linked on each idea), the game’s own lot texts (`research/data/game-lots.json`), `research/ea-family-lore.md`, `townies-and-roles.md`, `undecided-lots.md`, the world notes and TODO.md. EA’s words are summarised, not copied.
- **“Changes”** says how much an idea changes what the game ships:
  - **none:** story only; nothing in the game or your plan moves.
  - **small:** one lot type you’re deciding anyway, one new Sim, a homeless townie given a home, or a household moved within its own world.
  - **big:** an EA Sim or family moved to another world.
- **“Fills”** marks ideas that would also close an open item (an owner to pick, an undecided lot).
- Older-game lore (Sims 2 and 3) is marked as such: the Sims 4 is its own timeline.

## Willow Creek

1. **Cornelia’s grave at Cordelia Church.** Mortimer’s late mother gets a headstone at Cordelia Church, the cemetery next to Ophelia Villa (or among the two graves behind the villa since patch 174), and Agnes comes over from Henford to visit her sister.
   - Touches: the Goths; Cordelia Church (not built); Agnes Crumplebottom. Changes: **none** (a decorative headstone; the game won’t let Cornelia be a ghost).
   - Lore: Cornelia is on the Goth tree since patch 193 (died in a fire); Agnes is her sister. [Cornelia Goth](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Cornelia_Goth), [Agnes Crumplebottom](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Agnes_Crumplebottom)
2. **Ophelia Villa, haunted again.** Your Haunted House choice puts back what every older Goth home had. The ghosts could be “the family”: Lady Ravendancer’s line, which the Goths share with Ravenwood’s Gomeses.
   - Touches: the Goths; Zelmira Gomes (Ravenwood). Changes: **none** (already your choice).
   - Lore: the wiki notes the first Ophelia Villa, unlike every older Goth home, had no family graveyard, and suggests the Haunted House type to make it haunted like them. [Ophelia Villa](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Ophelia_Villa), [Lady Ravendancer Goth](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Lady_Ravendancer_Goth)
3. **The Pancakes’ last try.** Bob and Eliza’s marriage is unhappy (in the game they seem to sleep in separate rooms). Their move from Pique Hearth to Rindle Rose could be a fresh start to save it, perhaps with Iggy, the son Maxis put on the Gallery.
   - Touches: the Pancakes; Rindle Rose; Courtyard Lane Playground. Changes: **none** (**small** if you download Iggy).
   - Lore: [Bob Pancakes](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Bob_Pancakes), [Eliza Pancakes](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Eliza_Pancakes)
4. **The Good Timers move together.** Bjorn Bjergsen leads The Good Timers club and Jacques Villareal is a member, so the two families moving to Brook Bungalow and Umbrage Manor side by side has a reason: the club came too, and could meet at Café Llama.
   - Touches: Bjergsens, Villareals. Changes: **none** (your moves).
   - Lore: [Bjorn Bjergsen](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Bjorn_Bjergsen), [Jacques Villareal](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Jacques_Villareal)
