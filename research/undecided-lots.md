For the open **[Jemma]** lot decisions in TODO.md. Research only; nothing is changed.

## 1. Key Point (Sulani, Mua Pel’am, 40×30, not built)

**Facts:**
- The game calls it a “deserted islet” and “the perfect untouched beach”.
- **The Wiki gives its traits as Oceanic Paradise and Off-the-Grid.** Off-the-Grid is really a lot challenge: most powered and plumbed objects don’t work. Candles, fireplaces, woodburning stoves, fridges, microwaves and a few old-fashioned toilets, sinks and showers still do.
- **Allowed:** every ordinary type, Beach included.
- **Not allowed:** Residential Rental (it isn’t allowed on Sulani’s waterfront lots).
- **Already set aside:** a market, beach or national park (she wants minimal structure); a Custom Venue was not taken (16 Oct).

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Vacation Rental: a castaway hut** | Tiny build; Off-the-Grid becomes the point of the stay; Mua Pel’am is the wild island | Sulani already has Lagoon of Love; check in game that Off-the-Grid stays on a Vacation Rental |
| **Generic: a bare islet** with a dock, a fire pit and a hammock | Least building of all; Off-the-Grid works on Generic | Townies never visit; only her own Sims would go |
| **Tiny Home Residential: a hermit’s beach shack** | Uses the game’s “perfect for a small home”. Sulani’s four homeless locals are mermaids; Kalamainu’u Iona is a Loner, a natural fit | She has kept homeless townies homeless elsewhere; still a build |
| **Leave it an empty lot** | Truest to “untouched”; nothing to build | Stays “To be decided” in spirit; the site shows an empty lot |

**Recommendation: Vacation Rental, a castaway hut.** It’s minimal, it makes the islet usable, and the trait becomes part of the story. If she wants no structure at all, choose **Generic**.

## 2. LaSuli Point (Copperdale, Plumbite Cove, 20×15): Park or Cemetery?

**Facts:**
- The game ships it as a two-bedroom starter home “a stone’s throw away from the Pier”.
- Copperdale has neither a park nor a cemetery now (Totter Park is the Prices’ home).
- Her view: it’s a teen area.
- **What each type needs (game tuning):**
  - **Park:** toilet, sink, 2 benches, chess table, an outdoor table with 4 outdoor seats, and **at least one piece of playground kit**.
  - **Cemetery:** a grave or urn, toilet, 2 benches and a bin. Crypts and gravesites are optional.
- Funerals can be held on a Park as well as a Cemetery.

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Park** | A lakeside hangout by the fairground suits the teen town; brings families and chess players; funerals still possible | Needs one playground item (monkey bars count) even if it’s for teens |
| **Cemetery** | Copperdale would have one; small lots work (only 1 grave needed) | Odd next to the fairground; a family town of teens doesn’t miss one, and Ravenwood has the big one |

**Recommendation: Park.** A small lakeside park for the teens, maybe with a memorial bench or urn wall as decoration if she wants a nod to the cemetery idea.

## 3. Specter Row (Ravenwood, Crow’s Crossing, Moppy Manor, 30×20, not built)

**Facts:**
- **The game’s lot:** a 2-floor house with 1 bedroom and 1 bathroom. The game says the Moppy statue’s eyes glow at night and lights flicker in the empty houses next door.
- **The Specters** are Olive, her fiancé Layne Coffin and her teen son Nyon, so they need 2 bedrooms.
- **EA seems to have meant them to be haunted:** their game home (her Wedding Hall now) has the On Hallowed Grounds trait, and Nyon has a “survived seven nights in a haunted house” milestone.
- **A lot takes one type.**
- **On Hallowed Grounds** works on any lot type: TV static, flickering lights, cold spots, extra ghosts.
- **Haunted houses:** Ravenwood already has 2 (Old Ravenwood Estate, The Cozy Casket House); 4 in her save in all.

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Residential Rental, 2 units, On Hallowed Grounds on both** | Keeps her “Specters’ home + tenants”; the trait keeps it spooky; For Rent’s automatic Maintenance Troubles suits a creaky old house | No Guidry, specters or haunted-house nights |
| **Haunted House Residential, the Specters alone** | The full spooky gameplay; fits EA’s hints | No tenants; a third haunted house in one small world |
| **Haunted House Residential with paying roommates** (Discover University’s roommate service) | Spooky gameplay *and* lodgers | Roommates are random townies, not her chosen households, and won’t show as tenants on the site; check in game that roommates can be had on a haunted house |

**Recommendation: Residential Rental with 2 units:**
- **The Specters’ unit:** 2 bedrooms.
- **A small tenant unit:** 1 bedroom.
- **Traits:** On Hallowed Grounds on both units.

That keeps her story, and Ravenwood already has two haunted houses.

## 4. 1 Cobblebottom Street (Henford-on-Bagley, Finchwick, 20×15, built, not shared)

**Facts:**
- **The game’s house:** 2 floors with 1 bedroom and 1 bathroom, traits Homey and Wild Foxes. The game calls it a “cozy but cluttered home” with an “excess of character”.
- **Finchwick’s only spare home.** Henford has no café, tea room or holiday let.
- **A Café lot needs (game tuning):** espresso bar, espresso machine, grinder, 3 tables, 6 dining chairs, sink, toilet and bin. That’s a lot for a two-floor 20×15 house.

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| **Small Business Residential: a tea room** downstairs, owner upstairs | Fills the gap a posh English village feels most; keeps the bedroom upstairs; “Drink Tea” is a business activity; NPC-run home businesses open (she’s visited Koffieboon) | The ground floor needs adapting; an owner to choose |
| **Café** | Runs by itself with a barista | Biggest change inside; strictly an espresso place |
| **Vacation Rental** | No changes at all; Henford has had no holiday let since 13 Nettle Lane became a home | Doesn’t add anything for villagers |
| **Stays a home** | No work | The village still has no tea room |

**Owner idea for a tea room (her own story):**
- **Who:** Agnes and Agatha Crumplebottom, the elderly cousins who run the garden stall. In the game they’re hidden Sims with no home.
- **Why it fits:** Agnes as a cranky tea-room proprietor fits Henford, and the Crumplebottoms on Cobblebottom Street is a nice echo.
- **To check in game:** whether hidden Sims can be moved into a lot, and whether their stall carries on.
- **Otherwise:** a new Sim of hers.

**Recommendation: Small Business Residential (Tea Room).** If she wants zero changes to the build, choose **Vacation Rental**.
