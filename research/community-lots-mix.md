Caveats: types are matched by words in the plan's lot type, so it's approximate. A lot with two types ("Park/Cemetery", "Restaurant/Bar") counts once for each. ## What stands out

1. **Eating out is short, nightlife isn't.** 34 bars, clubs, lounges and Backrooms against 27 restaurants and cafés. 10 worlds have no restaurant: Selvadorada, StrangerVille, Sulani, Glimmerbrook, Britechester, Evergreen Harbor, Henford-on-Bagley, Copperdale, Nordhaven, Ondarion.
2. **Gyms are scarce.** Only 5 worlds have one; 20 have none.
3. **Karaoke is only in San Myshuno** (Waterside Warble built, Torendi Tunes planned). Tomarang or Britechester would suit one more; a bar with a karaoke machine also does the job.
4. **About right:** libraries, museums, bars, cemeteries, wedding venues, vets and Backrooms.
