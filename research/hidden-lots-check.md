# Four hidden lots: worth adding to the plan?

Researched 14 October 2026 (web sources, the public Gallery and the game's own files). These four lots are in the game files but not in her plan. Mt. Komorebi Peak is already in the plan (added 13 Oct).

## Mt. Komorebi Base Camp (Mt. Komorebi, 20×15)

- The second stop on the Mountain Climb Excursion, which starts at the Sutefani Onsen Bathhouse. You nap in the tent, check and climb the wall, and meet at the trail marker ([simscommunity](https://simscommunity.info/2020/11/16/the-sims-4-snowy-escape-mountain-climb-excursion-guide/)). The Sims Wiki counts it as a hidden lot ([wiki](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Mt._Komorebi)).
- **Can be built on with `bb.enablefreebuild`** ([TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/sims-4-snowy-escape-climbing-mt-komorebi-guide/)). Gallery uploads of it say the same.
- **Rebuilt a lot:** over 24 uploads on the Gallery, and a "Rebuild Mt. Komorebi" speed build by Chrissie's Corner.
- **Watch out:** one source says that saving while you build on these mountain lots ends the climb. Keep the tent and the trail marker.

## The Croft Icefall (Mt. Komorebi, 20×15)

- The third and hardest stop: a bonfire, a wood pile and a bush, with an ice cave that weather sometimes blocks ([simscommunity](https://simscommunity.info/2020/11/16/the-sims-4-snowy-escape-mountain-climb-excursion-guide/), [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/sims-4-snowy-escape-climbing-mt-komorebi-guide/)).
- Same as Base Camp: free build to edit, over 24 Gallery uploads, keep the bonfire, the wood pile and the marker.

## Omiscan Royal Baths (Selvadorada, 40×30)

- Not a temple variant. It's its own secret lot in the jungle: a ruin with a natural pool and dig sites. It's the one jungle area that's always open, and a gateway from it leads to the Omiscan Gardens ([wiki](https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/Jungle_District), [SimsVIP](https://simsvip.com/sims-4-jungle-adventure-guide/)).
- Its lot type in the game is "Omiscan Royal Baths".
- **Can be built on with free build**, going by several Gallery renovations. About 20 uploads, including an unedited copy of the original.
- **Watch out:** keep the gateway to the Gardens and the dig sites. Nobody reports whether they still work after a rebuild.

## Omiscan Temple (Selvadorada, 50×50)

- The secret temple full of puzzles, traps and treasure. It has **five layouts**, and it rearranges itself after each visit ([SimsVIP](https://simsvip.com/sims-4-jungle-adventure-guide/), [snootysims](https://snootysims.com/wiki/sims-4/jungle-adventure-game-pack-sims-4/), [simscommunity](https://simscommunity.info/2018/02/23/the-sims-4-jungle-adventure-important-facts-from-the-latest-temple-livestream/)).
- **No evidence that edits stick.** The layout change would most likely undo them. Gallery "tribute" builds exist (exterior only, or the puzzle pieces without working puzzles), but none works as a temple.

## Suggestion

- **Base Camp and The Croft Icefall:** worth adding as secret lots (Yukimatsu, with the Peak). They're small, can be edited with free build, and are often rebuilt.
- **Omiscan Royal Baths:** worth adding as a secret lot (Belomisia Jungle), keeping the gateway.
- **Omiscan Temple:** not one to rebuild. At most a note, or a tribute build on another lot.
