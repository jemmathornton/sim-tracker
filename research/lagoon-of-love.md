# Lagoon of Love: which lot type? (23 Oct)

> **6 Nov:** two different builds share this lot. The Gallery's “Lagoon of Love” is the beach wedding spot: no house, a bathroom hut, 9 packs. Your Library's “Lagoon Look”, the copy in “Building #2”, is a stilt holiday house: 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, set as a Vacation Rental with Sunny Aspect, Bracing Breezes and Eco Lot, Island Living only. Pictures: for-jemma/lot-compare/lagoon-look.jpg. So “check the bedrooms in game” is answered: the wedding build has none, the house has 3.

Research only. Nothing in data/, the site or her files was changed.

## At a glance

- **Recommendation: Wedding Venue.** It's what the build is (her Gallery text and tags call it a beach wedding spot) and Sulani has no wedding venue.
- **Vacation Rental doesn't fit the build as uploaded:** the upload has 0 beds and 0 bathrooms, so there's nothing to stay in.
- **Park is the runner-up:** it's what the upload is saved as, it can host a Getaway (a Wedding Venue can't), and Sulani has no park lot either.
- **Lot traits:** her three still work on any of the three types; for a wedding spot, Romantic Aura and Party Place (both already in her Gallery tags) would do more.

## The facts

- **The lot:** Lagoon Look, Ohan'ali Town, 30×20. Gallery upload "Lagoon of Love": type Park, 0 beds, 0 baths, §21,408, 7 favourites, 34 downloads. Packs: Get Together, Island Living, Cats & Dogs, City Living, Build Lush Gardens, Backyard Stuff, Jungle Adventure, Dine Out, Outdoor Retreat.
- **Her description:** a stretch of beach and "the perfect wedding spot in Sulani", with a swing set for the children. Tags include #weddingvenue, #beachwedding, #romanticaura, #sunnyaspect, #partyplace.
- **Her plan:** Vacation Rental (settled 14 Oct, TODO.md). Her Building save also had Vacation Rental when last read (plan-vs-game.md).
- **Sulani allows all three** (game's region tuning, research/data/world-lot-types.json). Sulani is a residential world, not a holiday world, but Vacation Rental is on its list.
- **My Wedding Stories: not owned** (Tartosa `owned: false`). The Wedding Venue lot type is free in the base game (patch Feb 2022) and needs no objects; base-game weddings work there since a later patch (research/lot-types-reference.md).
- **Getaways:** a Park can host one; a Wedding Venue and a Vacation Rental can't (the game's own list, research/getaways-check.md).
- **Sulani has no wedding venue, park or vacation rental** apart from this lot. It's an island "not near much else" (her world map), so the nearest wedding venues (Tomarang's ZoSul Taman Botani, Newcrest, Ravenwood, San Sequoia) are all far away.
- **Across her owned worlds:** 5 wedding venues (about right for 25 towns), 10 parks, 14 vacation rentals (research/community-lots-mix.md).

## The options

| Type | What it gives | What it needs | Fits Sulani's mix? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wedding Venue** | A place to pick for a wedding; matches her description and tags | Nothing required (an arch, bar, seating, toilet and sink help). Base game, so no pack needed | Yes: Sulani's only one, and the island is far from the others. Ohan'ali Town is already the social island |
| **Vacation Rental** | A holiday let her Sims can book (1–7 days) | Beds and a bathroom to be any use; the upload has neither. Can't host a Getaway | Weakest: no beds as built. She'd need to add a beach house |
| **Park** | Families and children visit by themselves; can host a Getaway | Playground equipment (the swing set may count), toilet, sink, bin: check in game | Fair: Sulani has no park lot, but two beaches (Ohan'ali Beach is next door) do much of a park's job |
| Beach (other) | Beach visitors, swimming | Allowed in Sulani | Sulani already has two; a third adds little |

**Can weddings happen without the Wedding Venue type?** Yes. With My Wedding Stories a ceremony can be held at the couple's home, a Park, a Beach or a Wedding Venue (Sims Community guide). In the base game a wedding can be held at home or on community lots. Whether one can be held on a Vacation Rental wasn't found in any source: check in game.

## Lot traits

- Now: Sunny Aspect, Bracing Breezes, Eco Lot. All three are general traits and stay allowed on any of the three types.
- **Wedding Venue:** Romantic Aura (romance easier) and Party Place (event goals easier) are the usual picks (research/lot-trait-ideas.md); both are already in her Gallery tags. Sunny Aspect (uplift at sunset) suits an evening ceremony. Three slots, so two of her current ones would go.
- **Vacation Rental:** her three are the usual picks; Romantic Aura suits a honeymoon let.
- **Park:** her three fit; Child's Play suits the swing set.

## If she picks Wedding Venue

- The plan's `type` and her spreadsheet would change, and research/new-save-plan.md (line 86) would say Wedding Venue.
- A re-upload as Wedding Venue would make the Gallery match (Gallery placements keep that type since the 22 Sep 2026 patch).
