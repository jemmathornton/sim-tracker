# Game options for your play save (22 Oct)

Research only, for section 1 of `for-jemma/in-game-checklist.md`. Nothing in the site, data or game was changed.

**Sources.** Best first: the game’s own English text, read from the installed game on 22 Oct 2026 (exact option names and help text). Then EA’s forums (Answers HQ), the Sims Wiki, SimsCommunity and fan guides, dated where the page gives a date. Older research in this folder: `townies-and-roles.md` (§3, §5, from the game’s tuning) and `eco-and-community-spaces.md`.

**Where things are.** Game Options (Esc → Game Options) has a Gameplay tab plus one tab per pack (Eco Lifestyle, Seasons, Werewolves, Royalty & Legacy…). Neighbourhood Stories rules and the heart live in **Manage Worlds → Manage Households**. All of these are saved with each save, so set them in the play save itself.

---

## 1. Neighbourhood Stories

**Where:** the on/off switch is Game Options → Gameplay → **“Enable Neighborhood Stories”**. The rules are in Manage Worlds → Manage Households: the icon top right of the **My Households** or **Other Households** tab sets the whole tab; the left icon under each household tile sets that household alone (“Configure custom Neighborhood Stories for this Household”).

**What it does:** gives Sims you aren’t playing life changes. The rules, each a tick box: Move In, Move Out, Have a Baby, Adopt a Child, Adopt Cats & Dogs, Rescue Horses, Join a Career, Leave a Career, Retire from Career, Die in Accidents, Friendship Changes, Romance Changes. (Marriage and break-ups come under Romance Changes.)
- Move In: “Households may move into unoccupied lots of any Residential world.” It can use a homeless household (homeless premades included) or make a new one. The lot needs enough beds.
- Move Out: “Households in Residential worlds may vacate lots.”

**Default:** on. Every rule on for Other Households; off for My Households. A household follows the rules of the tab it’s in, unless it has its own.

**Caveats:**
- Several players (EA forums, 2023–2025) report households still moving in with it switched off. A fix that worked for one: switch it on, untick every rule in both tabs, then switch it off again. Moderators say switching it off should stop all moves.
- The older weekly top-up in Willow Creek (to about 80% full) and Oasis Springs (60%) is game tuning, not this option. It’s unconfirmed whether switching Neighbourhood Stories off stops it. Your Willow Creek has no empty homes and Oasis Springs is already above 60%, so it shouldn’t matter.
- It doesn’t stop ageing (that’s its own option, §3).
- Homeless premades stay homeless only if Move In is off for the tab they’re in (Other Households).

**What planners do:** “filled with chosen households” saves usually leave it on but untick Move In and Move Out (often babies and deaths too) for Other Households, or switch it off altogether.

**For you:** on, with Move In, Move Out, Have a Baby, Adopt a Child, Adopt Cats & Dogs, Rescue Horses and Die in Accidents unticked for Other Households; keep My Households all off. Careers and romance are your call. If moves still happen, use the off/on fix above.

## 2. Maximum Sim Count, culling and the heart

**Where:** Game Options → Gameplay → **“Maximum Sim Count”**, and the same drop-down at the bottom right of Manage Households (My Households tab), next to the current count.

**What it does:** caps how many Sims can be in **My Households**. It doesn’t cap Other Households. Over the cap, the game makes you move households out before you carry on.

**Choices:** earlier research from the game’s tuning: 80, 150, 200 or Unlimited. Sources disagree (one 2026 fan guide says 50, 100, 200). Check in game. The game warns that Unlimited “will severely impact game speed and load times. It may lead to game crashes.”

**The heart:** it exists. On each household tile in Manage Households, the button players call “the heart” has the game text **“Move into My Households”** (and “Move into Other Households” to undo). There’s no separate “favourite” status for households in your own save; “Favorite Household” in the game’s text is for Gallery households.

**How culling works** (game tuning, earlier research; HappySimming, 6 Jan 2026; EA forum status post, 2019):
- Only **Other Households** are culled. **My Households are never culled.** The whole-save limit is about 800 Sims in recent sources (a 2019 post says 510); the tuning keeps around 170 at the usual level, plus 15%.
- The game keeps Sims best connected to played Sims and seen most recently. Family links (parent, sibling, cousin, spouse, engaged) help; fame helps.
- Sims with no home (homeless premades, townies) are the first to go. Ghosts are culled 90 days after death unless their bond is strengthened.
- A culled role Sim is replaced in the job by a made-up stranger.
- Culled ancestors keep a name and picture on the 2026 family tree but can’t come back.

**Caveats:**
- A household in My Households counts as one of yours, so Neighbourhood Stories uses the My Households rules for it (all off by default). Good for your plan.
- Some job filters skip Sims from played households. It’s untested whether moving an unplayed role Sim into My Households counts as “played”. The light-green plumbob shows “protected, unplayed”, which suggests not, but leave role Sims (Scotts, Chopras, Wolfgang, Fia, Connie…) in Other Households unless they’re at risk.

**What planners do:** heart every household they care about and raise the count to 200 or Unlimited.

**For you:** raise it to Unlimited if the PC copes (otherwise the highest number), then heart all your planned households, Consort Capp and the four Sulani mermaids. Leave job-holding townies unhearted.

## 3. Ageing

**Where:** Game Options → Gameplay → **“Auto Age (Played Households)”**, **“Auto Age (Unplayed Households)”** and **“Sim Lifespan”** (Short, Normal, Long).

**What it does:** Unplayed: “Enables unplayed Sims, Cats, Dogs, and Horses to age over time. If disabled, they will not age up.” Played covers any household with a dark-green plumbob, not just the one you’re playing. Farm animals have their own option (“If disabled, chickens, cows… will no longer grow older”).

**Default:** both on, Normal lifespan.

**Role Sims:** named townies with jobs (Eloise Hiddlestix needs to be a young adult for the Thrift and Boba job) age like any unplayed Sim. With unplayed ageing on, Eloise is replaced in about 28 Sim days at Normal lifespan.

**Caveats** (EA forums, 2022–2024): a few reports of NPCs ageing with it off, usually Sims made outside your worlds. With ageing off and Have a Baby on, unplayed households can fill up with babies who never grow, so untick Have a Baby too (§1).

**What planners do:** unplayed off, played on, so the planned world stays as built while the households they play move on.

**For you:** Auto Age (Unplayed Households) off; Played on or off to taste; lifespan your choice.

## 4. Eco Lifestyle: NPC voting

**Where:** Game Options → **Eco Lifestyle** tab → **“Enable NPC Voting”**. The same tab has “Enable Eco Footprint Gameplay”.

**What it does:** “If disabled, voting at the Community Board is user-directed only.” With it on, each plan on the ballot gets random votes every day while a neighbourhood has fewer than 4 plans, so a random plan usually passes each week.

**Default:** on.

**Caveats:** switching it off doesn’t repeal plans already in force. Check each neighbourhood’s voting board and repeal any you don’t want (players use testing cheats at the mailbox, neighbourhood by neighbourhood).

**For you:** off. Then check each neighbourhood’s current plans once.

## 5. Pets and horses

**Where:** Neighbourhood Stories rules **“Adopt Cats & Dogs”** and **“Rescue Horses”** (Manage Households, per tab or per household).

**What it does:** unplayed households adopt a cat or dog, or rescue a horse (Horse Ranch).

**Default:** on for Other Households.

**Strays:** Brindleton Bay’s strays (Mayor Whiskers, Captain Whitaker) aren’t a game option. They wander and can be adopted by your own Sims. I couldn’t confirm whether Neighbourhood Stories ever adopts a named stray.

**For you:** untick both for Other Households (included in §1).

## 6. Seasons: holidays

**Where:** the Calendar (live mode, next to the clock). Click a day to **Add Holiday**, or a holiday to **Edit Holiday** or delete it. Season length: Game Options → **Seasons** tab → **“Season Length”** (7, 14 or 28 days).

**How it works:** each save keeps its own holidays. A new save has four: **Love Day** (spring), **Harvestfest** (autumn), **Winterfest** and **New Year’s Eve** (winter). Spooky Day isn’t a default (EA forums). Harvestfest and Winterfest are days off work and school. The game warns: “Changing the season length will move the calendar position of holidays and events.”

**For you:** the site’s calendar uses 4-week seasons, so the save needs **Season Length 28 days**. Set that first, then read each holiday off the Calendar: name, season, week (1–4) and day.

## 7. Other options worth setting

Only ones that touch your goals. Names are the game’s own.

- **Royalty & Legacy tab:** “Enable Dynasty Prestige Decay” (prestige drops by itself; off keeps your dynasties where you set them); “Enable Scandals”; “Allow Extortion on Active Sim”; “Enable Noble Career Auto Promotion”. Your call; decay off suits a planned save.
- **Werewolves tab:** “Lunar Cycle Length”, “Disable Lunar Effects”. Taste only.
- **Fame:** “Sims Begin Opted Out of Fame”. On stops townies gaining fame by chance. Fame protects Sims from culling a little, so either is fine.
- **Rentals (For Rent):** “Enable Rental Unit Events” (random tenant events); “If disabled, unoccupied units will no longer be hidden by default when entering Build Mode”. Taste only.
- **Relationships:** “Allow Automatic Breakups” (I didn’t confirm which tab or pack it sits under). Off keeps your planned couples together.
- **Businesses & Hobbies:** “Enable Small Business Dilemmas”. Taste only.
- **New with EA's 27 Oct patch (1.129):** “Allow Weight Changes” and “Allow Muscle Changes” (the game's own settings file, GameplayOptionsData, read 30 Oct). Off keeps every Sim's body as you made it, including your Gallery households. UI Cheats Extension (v1.59.1) hides both, so take it out of Mods while you set them, then put it back (on your list). Your call.
- **Not options:** there is no “fill empty lots” switch any more (Neighbourhood Stories replaced it) and no townie-generation switch.

## Summary

| Option | Set to | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Enable Neighborhood Stories | On | Lets you choose rule by rule |
| Other Households: Move In, Move Out | Off | Nobody fills your empty homes or leaves your lots |
| Other Households: Have a Baby, Adopt a Child, Die in Accidents | Off | Households stay as planned |
| Other Households: Adopt Cats & Dogs, Rescue Horses | Off | No unplanned animals |
| My Households | All off (default) | Hearted households stay as they are |
| Maximum Sim Count | Unlimited (or highest the PC copes with) | Room for every planned household in My Households |
| Heart (“Move into My Households”) | Every planned household, Consort Capp, the four mermaids | My Households are never culled |
| Auto Age (Unplayed Households) | Off | Role Sims like Eloise keep their jobs |
| Enable NPC Voting | Off | No random action plans; repeal any already in force |
| Season Length | 28 days | Matches the site’s 4-week seasons; set before listing holidays |
| Enable Dynasty Prestige Decay | Off (your call) | Dynasties keep their standing |
| Allow Automatic Breakups | Off (your call) | Planned couples stay together |

## Where sources disagree

- **Maximum Sim Count choices:** 80/150/200/Unlimited (tuning, earlier research; EA forums) against 50/100/200/Unlimited (HappySimming, Jan 2026). Check in game.
- **Whole-save culling limit:** about 800 (HappySimming 2026; EA forum 2019) against about 510 (older forum post); the tuning’s target is lower (about 170 + 15%).
- **Neighbourhood Stories off:** EA moderators say it stops all moves; several players say households still moved in. Use the off/on reset if it happens.
- **Ageing off:** works for most; a few reports of NPCs ageing anyway.
