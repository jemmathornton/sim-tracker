# Jemma's lot plan vs the official Sims 4 lot lists

Checked on 13 October 2026 against snootysims (all worlds up to Ciudad Enamorada), the Sims Wiki (fandom) neighbourhood pages, and simscommunity.info, keengamer and destructoid world guides for the newer worlds. Each section lists only the differences. Type differences are ignored where Jemma has deliberately changed a lot type (for example a residential lot turned into a library), because that is part of her plan.

## Headline findings

- **A whole world is missing:** Innisgreen (Enchanted by Nature, July 2025), with 14 lots.
- **Secret and hidden lots missing:** Sylvan Glade (Willow Creek), Forgotten Grotto (Oasis Springs), Hermit's House (Granite Falls), Mt. Komorebi Peak, and the Selvadorada jungle areas and Omiscan Temple.
- **Likely wrong sizes:** Harbor Quarter Gym (Windenburg) and Club Eternity (Ravenwood). The Netherworld Department size is uncertain.
- **Spelling fixes:** Piccola Luce, The Summer Home, Avenida de la Eternidad, Plaza Mariposa, Creek Side Corner, and Champion's Grove (minor).

---

## Willow Creek
- **Missing: Sylvan Glade.** Secret lot, 15×10. It sits with Foundry Cove, by the Sylvan Tree next to Crick Cabana. To reach it, view, water and compliment the tree, then explore the trunk and choose Follow the Sound → Travel Downstream → Enter the Mist → Travel to Glade. Sims with Wellness 7 can also teleport there while meditating once they have visited. It can only be edited with `bb.enablefreebuild`.
- Everything else matches, 21 lots in total (fandom gives 16 residential and 5 community).

## Oasis Springs
- **Missing: Forgotten Grotto.** Secret lot, 15×10. Its entrance is a boarded-up mine in the canyon behind Desert Bloom Park or behind Affluista Mansion. Opening it needs Handiness 10. Then choose Take the Wide Path → Climb the Ladder → Step onto the Ledge. It can only be edited with `bb.enablefreebuild`.
- Everything else matches. Snootysims misspells "Soundtrap Flat", "Rafia Quinta", "Rettlesnake Juice" and "Yuma Hights"; Jemma's spellings are the correct ones.

## Newcrest
Matches: 15 lots, no secret lots.

## Granite Falls
- **Missing: Hermit's House (Deep Woods).** Hidden lot, 30×20, lot type "Hermit's Hut". To reach it, find the bramble or shrub hole at the edge of Granite Falls Forest and choose Explore. Then pick Step Forward → Go Through the Web → Ignore the Object → Travel Towards the Sim. After the first visit a portal lets household Sims go straight there.
- Everything else matches. All of Jemma's sizes agree, including Forest Hideaway 40×30 and Riverside Retreat 30×20.

## Magnolia Promenade (plus the Get to Work secret lots)
Matches. Jemma's "Secret Lots" group covers Police Station 40×30, FutureSim Labs 50×50, Willow Creek Hospital 50×50 and Sixam 20×20. In the game these sit in their own hidden neighbourhoods ("Civic Center" and "Scientist"), but none is missing.

## Windenburg
- **Size mismatch: Harbor Quarter Gym.** The official size is **30×30**; Jemma has 30×20. Snootysims and the Sims Wiki (Lykke Centre page) agree on 30×30.
- **Name: "The Summer House" should be "The Summer Home"** (Windslar, 40×30). Both sources agree.
- Everything else matches, 27 lots including Von Haunt Estate, Ancient Ruins and The Bluffs.

## San Myshuno
Matches. All 21 apartment units (counted individually), both penthouses and the community lots are present. Minor capitalisation: "1313 21 Chic street" should be "1313 21 Chic Street". The Sims Wiki calls the Torendi penthouse "1 Torendi Tower Penthouse".

## Forgotten Hollow
Matches (5 lots, sizes agree).

## Selvadorada
- **Missing: the hidden jungle areas behind the Belomisia Trailhead archway.** They load as separate hidden areas: Area 1 and Area 2, the Plane Crash Site, the Omiscan Royal Baths, the Omiscan Gardens, the Lagoon, the Island and the **Omiscan Temple**. The temple's layout is randomised on each visit. These areas cannot be edited without `bb.enablefreebuild`, and no source gives their sizes. Snootysims lists three of them as "secret lots" (the plane crash site, royal baths and gardens) with no sizes.
- **Name: "Hillview Hideaway"** is correct as the EA name. Jemma's own name, "Hillside Hideaway", is just her choice.
- The 7 normal lots and their sizes all match.

## Brindleton Bay
Matches (16 lots). Sources disagree on one size. For **Sporting Space**, snootysims says 20×20, but the Sims Wiki (Sable Square page) says 30×20, which is what Jemma has. The wiki is the more reliable source here.

## Del Sol Valley
Matches: 11 neighbourhood lots plus the hidden Plumbob Pictures studio (50×50), which is reached only through the Actor career.

## StrangerVille
Matches (11 lots plus the ██████ Lab, 64×64).
- "Riverside Grove" is confirmed by the Sims Wiki; snootysims' "Riverside Groove" is a typo.
- "Plateau Place": snootysims has "Plateau Palace". I couldn't confirm which is right because the Shady Acres wiki page wouldn't load. Jemma's spelling is probably correct.

## Sulani
Matches (14 lots, sizes agree).

## Glimmerbrook
- **Name: "Creekside Corner"** is "Creek Side Corner" in both snootysims and the Sims Wiki.
- Otherwise matches: 5 lots plus the Magic Realm. The Magic Realm is a hidden lot that can only be edited with `bb.enablefreebuild`. No source gives its size, so Jemma's 40×40 can't be verified.

## Britechester
Matches (13 lots, sizes agree).

## Evergreen Harbor
Matches: 15 lots, including Stonestreet Apartments #3 and #4 and Pinecrest Apartments #402 and #404.

## Mt. Komorebi
- **Missing: Mt. Komorebi Peak** (lot type "Mount Komorebi Summit"), **20×20**. You reach it through a Mountain Climbing Expedition, planned by phone from Yukimatsu or the Sutefani Onsen Bathhouse, via Base Camp and The Croft Icefall. With Enchanted by Nature you can also use the Starseed Teleporter in Innisgreen. The Sims Wiki says it can be edited freely, but the lot type can't be changed. Simscommunity describes it as an uneditable "tiny lot".
- The Sims Wiki counts "3 hidden lots" for Mt. Komorebi but doesn't name them. They are probably the Peak plus the Base Camp and Icefall stages, which I believe are rabbit-hole stages rather than buildable lots (unconfirmed).
- The 14 normal lots and their sizes match.

## Henford-on-Bagley
Matches (12 lots). Sources disagree on one size. For **The Gnome's Arms**, snootysims says 20×15, but the Sims Wiki (Finchwick page) says 30×20, which is what Jemma has.

## Tartosa
- **Name: "Piccola Luca" should be "Piccola Luce"** (Porto Luminoso, 20×20).
- Sources disagree on **Via Romanza**. Snootysims says 40×30; the Sims Wiki (Terra Amorosa page) says **30×30**, which is what Jemma has.
- Otherwise matches (9 lots).

## Moonwood Mill
Matches (5 lots). The Grimtooth bunker is part of the bar lot, not a separate lot. The pack territories and the mountain top are special locations, not lots.

## Copperdale
Matches (12 lots). Snootysims misspells "LeaSuli Point" and "ThriftTea"; Jemma's "LaSuli Point" and "ThrifTea" match the Sims Wiki.

## San Sequoia
Matches (12 lots, sizes agree).

## Chestnut Ridge
- Minor name difference: the Sims Wiki has **"Champion's Grove"**; Jemma has "Champions' Grove".
- Sources disagree on **The Cozy Corner House**. Snootysims says 30×20; the Sims Wiki says **20×20**, which is what Jemma has.
- Otherwise matches (13 lots). Canter Cottage is correct; snootysims' "Center Cottage" is a typo.

## Tomarang
Matches: 9 lots, all names and sizes agree across snootysims, keengamer and simscommunity. Some default-type notes:
- **Taka Soi 15** has 2 units (08 and 09) and **Sungai Point** has 3 (11A, 11B, 11C). Jemma's "Residential Rental (2)" on **Isda Riverfront** is her own change; by default that lot is an empty residential lot.
- The Tinsu Temple/Shrine, Sulea (Tiger) Sanctuary, Beard Beard Cove cave, Night Market and Fish Market are rabbit holes or world locations, not lots.

## Ciudad Enamorada
- **Neighbourhood name: "Plaza Maripoza" should be "Plaza Mariposa".**
- **Name: "Avenida de la Eternida" should be "Avenida de la Eternidad".**
- Sources disagree on **La Suite del Flechazo**. Snootysims says 40×20; the Sims Wiki (Nuevo Corazón page) says **20×15**, which is what Jemma has.
- Otherwise matches (13 lots).

## Ravenwood
- **Likely size mismatch: Club Eternity.** The Sims Wiki (Mourningvale page) and simscommunity both say **30×20**. Keengamer says 30×30, which is what Jemma has.
- **Unconfirmed size: Netherworld Department.** Simscommunity gives **20×20**; Jemma has 30×30. I couldn't load a second source.
- Otherwise matches: 13 lots plus the Netherworld Department as the one hidden lot.

## Nordhaven
Matches. All 12 lots and sizes agree with the keengamer guide, and no hidden lots are listed.

## Gibbi Point
Matches. All 13 lots, sizes and neighbourhood placements agree with the keengamer guide and the Sims Wiki. Underlight Bay is a swimming spot, not a lot.

## Ondarion (Royalty & Legacy, February 2026)
Matches. All 18 entries agree with simscommunity's world guide and destructoid's size breakdown: three palaces at 50×50, plus Bright Cliff 17 and 18 and Lakeview 12 and 13 as apartment units. Simscommunity and destructoid place Casa do Corsário and Fisherman's Hut in Verdemar, as Jemma does; thesimstree puts them in Dambele. The hidden room at the top of Verdemar Palace's tower and the treasure island are not separate lots.

## Other worlds not in the plan
- **Batuu (Journey to Batuu game pack).** If Jemma owns it, it has 3 lots that can't be edited, one per area. The Sims Wiki names Black Spire Outpost, the Millennium Falcon area and the Resistance Encampment. All other Batuu locations are rabbit holes. The lots can't be rebuilt without `bb.enablefreebuild`, so this is only for completeness.
- No newer world after Ondarion was found; Wikipedia lists Royalty & Legacy as the latest expansion pack.

---

## Unconfirmed / could not check
- Netherworld Department size: 20×20 from simscommunity only. The Sims Wiki page wouldn't load.
- Club Eternity: 30×20 from two sources against 30×30 from one.
- Plateau Place or Plateau Palace (StrangerVille): the Shady Acres wiki page wouldn't load.
- Magic Realm size: no source gives it, so Jemma's 40×40 can't be verified.
- Sizes of the Selvadorada jungle areas and the Omiscan Temple: none published. Whether each jungle area counts as a "lot" for rebuilding is also unclear.
- The two unnamed extra hidden lots the Sims Wiki counts for Mt. Komorebi.
- Fern & Frond Wading Pond's 64×64 is from keengamer only.
- Batuu lot names: the Sims Wiki's list is vague.
- Teoalida's pages and the Sims Wiki pages for Ravenwood, Nordhaven, Innisgreen and Ondarion failed to load, so I used other guides instead.
