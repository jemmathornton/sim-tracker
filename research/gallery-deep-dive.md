# Gallery deep dive

Run overnight on 14–15 October 2026 from her Gallery data (uploads, the weekly history, comments) and which lot each upload is on. Report only.

Data: `research/data/gallery-deep-dive.json`, made by `tools/research/gallery_deep_dive.py` (rerun after each weekly refresh).

## Headlines

- **226 uploads**: 203 lots, 17 rooms (her apartments) and 6 households. Together they have **19,833 downloads, 2,094 favourites and 50 comments.**
- **The Futures Past is the star.** It has 4,645 downloads and 336 favourites, about a quarter of all her downloads on its own. Next come Oasis Springs Library (1,462), Finchwick Village Hall (879), Bubble World (647), Anchorpoint Rec Center (621) and Izakaya Hanamigawa Koen (613).
- **Fewer packs, more downloads.** Base-game-only builds get a typical 95 downloads each. That drops to 41 with 1–3 packs and 18 with 8 or more.
- **Community lots get downloaded, homes get loved.** Museums, bars, libraries, the thrift and bubble tea stores and small businesses get the most downloads. Homes get fewer downloads but more favourites per download.
- **Her newer builds do better**, even with less time on the Gallery: typically 63–65 downloads for 2022–23 uploads, against 15 for 2017–18.
- **Growth can’t be judged yet.** The history only covers 11–14 October (20 downloads and 1 favourite gained). After a month of Sunday refreshes it will show real trends.

## Comments

50 comments on 26 uploads; 3 are her own replies. Bubble World has the most (8). Nearly all are praise, and two themes come up again and again:
- **It fits the world:** people like that a rebuild sits right in its neighbourhood and needs no changes (Strike Out!, Oasis Springs Library, The Futures Past).
- **Exactly what I was looking for:** Glimmer & Books, Lakeside Retreat, Cottage Am See, Gibbi Rec Center.

Worth knowing:
- One person found Cottage Am See through Reddit.
- On Lakeside Retreat, a commenter had first seen it re-uploaded by someone else and came looking for her original.
- Two possible fixes: on **Newcrest Community Space** someone said the court seemed bugged (she replied asking what; no answer). On **Strike Out!** someone said the game asks for a missing waste basket, which is an easy fix.
- A few comments are in German.
