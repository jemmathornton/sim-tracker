# Ondarion: deep dive for the big discussion (16 Oct)

For the TODO item "Ondarion: nothing decided yet, big discussion later". Sources: the Sims Wiki's Ondarion page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan, and the game's region tuning. Nothing is changed in her plan or on the site.

## The world in short

- **A royal kingdom on a Mediterranean-style sea, with three neighbourhoods, each home to a dynasty racing for the crown.**
  - **Dambele** is West African-inspired (Saint-Louis, Senegal and Sahel towns). It's known for "vibrant streets, communal spaces and a strong tradition of painting and crafting"; its highlight is "homemade traditional soups".
  - **Verdemar** is based on the Azores and Madeira. It's "fierce and untamed", with pirates and nobles side by side; its highlight is sailing for buried treasure.
  - **Bellacorde** is Italy, Spain and the south of France: "class, opulence and elegance… and crazy expensive".
- **No snow.** Every lot was built by EA Creator Network simmers.

## What it has and lacks

- **Has:** a market with a shared kitchen (Zanbira), a library by day and club by night (Hardcover), a pirate bar (Ye Olde Voyager) and a ballroom lounge (La Vivace).
- **Lacks:** a restaurant, a café, a park and a gym.
  - **Gym:** you've said Ondarion doesn't need one.
  - **Food:** Zanbira's kitchen and Dambele's soups cover home-style eating. There's no meal out in Verdemar or Bellacorde.
- **Allowed lot types:** all the everyday ones, including Restaurant, Café, Park, Wedding Venue, Spa, Pool, Karaoke Bar, Recreation Center, Custom Venue, Small Business and Haunted House. Beach isn't allowed.
