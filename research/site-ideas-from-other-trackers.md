# Jemma's Sim World: ideas from how other Simmers track and showcase saves

Research date: 13 Oct 2026. Web research only; I also read `claude/project-notes.md` and `claude/handover.md` so the ideas skip things the site already does (Stats page, neighbourhood cards with lot circles, Community Space versions, Gallery favourites/downloads/comments, "Owned by"/"Landlord of", tenants, weekly Gallery refresh).

A note on sources: several Notion templates and paid Gumroad/Ko-fi templates only load with JavaScript or hide their contents behind a purchase, so I could not see their fields. Reddit could not be read either (blocked to automated readers). Where a source's contents are taken from a description of it rather than the thing itself, I say so.

---

## 2. Ideas for Jemma's site

Effort: S = an hour or two, M = an evening, L = several sessions. "Input" = needs Jemma to decide or supply data.

### Data to add

1. **Lot traits and lot challenges on each lot page** (and a filter on Stats). Why: every fan database and the in-game Gallery card treat these as core lot facts; they also explain builds (e.g. Volcanic Activity in Sulani). Effort M. Input: maybe none if the Gallery item data (drop-api) carries them for shared lots (worth checking first); otherwise she fills a spreadsheet column for unshared lots.
2. **Beds by age group / capacity for houses and rental units** (e.g. "3 beds: 1 double, 2 single; fits 4"). Why: the EA-forum lot sheet tracks this to place families; it would make the planned per-unit rentals view useful. Effort M. Input: yes for anything not in Gallery bedroom/bathroom counts.
3. **Lot value** (price) on lot pages and as a Stats column. Why: Snooty Sims and the Rosannatxt template both list it; it is already in Gallery item data for shared lots. Effort S. Input: no for shared lots.
4. **Original vs new lot size** for rebuilds that resized a lot. Why: Lot Replacements shows "40x30 (was 30x20)"; tells the rebuild story in one line. Effort S. Input: yes (which lots she resized).
5. **Neighbourhood eco footprint (Green/Neutral/Industrial) and Evergreen Harbor development/NAPs** on neighbourhood cards. Why: shown in Manage Worlds and the Sims Wiki; she has a neighbourhood card per area already. Effort S. Input: yes (read from her game).
6. **World extras line: climate/weather, festivals and events, landmarks**. Why: the Sims Wiki and Snooty Sims list these per world; adds character to the world hero without art. Effort S (fixed facts, could come from the wiki). Input: light (pick which to show).
7. **Household basics: number of Sims and pets, life stages, family type, Neighbourhood Stories on/off, played/unplayed**. Why: Rosannatxt and Pleasant Sims track these; it is a much smaller step than full Sim profiles and works for all 128 households. Effort M. Input: yes.
8. **Upload and update dates** (first shared on the Gallery, last updated, "built on" date where known). Why: save files and CurseForge show dates; enables a "What's new" list and a timeline. Effort S for Gallery dates (item data), M if she adds build dates. Input: optional.
9. **Relationships between households beyond family** (friends, rivals, exes, colleagues). Why: Create4Sims and Threads of Life sell their saves on these links; her FAMILY_LINKS already handle relatives. Effort M. Input: yes.
10. **Packs needed per world** (union of packs used by its lots) in the world header. Why: every published save lists required packs; she already stores packs per lot. Effort S. Input: no.

### Pages / sections to add

1. **"What's new" changelog page** (dated entries: lots built, shared, households added, Gallery milestones). Why: Threads of Life's dated update notes are the clearest model; most public saves lack one; she reviews changes on the live site. Effort S if written by hand from the notes doc, M if generated by comparing data between builds. Input: light. (Check whether the "whats-new" backup zip mentioned in the handover already holds a start.)
2. **Vacancies page: "Empty homes and units"** across all worlds, with beds by age where known. Why: Create4Sims lists "3 of 4 units empty"; she has many empty rentals (Tail's End, Slip 42, Mossy Lane, Old Torget House...). Pairs naturally with the planned rentals per-unit view. Effort M. Input: no once units exist in data.
3. **Rentals per-unit view** (already planned): show each unit as a small row: unit, beds/baths, household or "Empty", landlord. Why: matches the apartment buildings layout and the vacancy idea. Effort M. Input: yes (unit by unit decisions are on her list).
4. **Before and after for EA rebuilds**: a two-picture (or slider) compare on lot pages, "EA original" vs "Mine". Why: the Lot Replacements overview and Lilsimsie write-ups lead with this; it is the core story of a world rebuild. Effort M. Input: yes (in-game screenshots of the EA originals; her own pictures, so no art issue).
5. **Gallery performance page** (or a Stats tab): top builds by favourites/downloads, biggest gains since the last weekly refresh, newest comments. Why: the Gallery itself has no stats history; her weekly refresh already collects the numbers. Effort M. Input: no (decide whether to keep a history of snapshots).
6. **Household page upgrade, stage 1**: members list with life stage only, backstory line, home, relationships. Stage 2 (only for households with profiles): Sim cards. Why: TheSimsTree and Legacy Hub start with name, life stage, photo; full per-Sim tracking was dropped as tedious even by keen trackers. Effort M (stage 1), L (stage 2). Input: yes.
7. **Family tree / generations page** for the one household with Sim profiles first. Why: the most common tool people use (TheSimsTree, Legacy Hub, Ko-fi generation dashboards). Draw it as simple HTML/SVG boxes; no portraits needed beyond her screenshots. Effort L. Input: yes (parents, partners, heirs).
8. **Bonus builds section** (already planned) styled like the save files' "new lots" subheadings: Robert Piggott School, Von-Windenburg Castle, Bubble World. Effort S. Input: confirm which builds.
9. **Challenge builds grouping** (e.g. her lilsimsie shell challenge uploads) as a filter or small section. Why: builders tag these on the Gallery; the site already knows which uploads are shell builds. Effort S. Input: confirm the list.

### Visual / navigation

1. **Site-wide quick search** (lot, household, Sim, Gallery title, in-game name). Why: with 352 lots and 128 households, a search box beats menus; the Stats table already has a search to reuse. Effort S-M. Input: no.
2. **Filter chips on world and Stats pages**: pack, lot type, lot trait, status, empty/occupied. Why: the Gallery and Notion trackers lean on filters. Effort M. Input: no.
3. **Jump links within long world pages** (neighbourhood contents strip). Why: the Lot Replacements overview uses a table of contents per world/neighbourhood. Effort S. Input: no.
4. **One-word world status chip** on the home tiles (Not started / Started / Almost / Complete), derived from her built/shared counts. Why: Threads of Life uses exactly this; quicker to read than numbers, and keeps percentages on Stats only (her rule). Effort S. Input: agree the thresholds.
5. **"Changed since last visit" dots** (per-viewer, browser storage): highlight lots with new comments or new favourites since she last opened the site. Why: she reviews on the laptop; helps spot new Gallery activity. Effort S. Input: no.
6. **Timeline strip** on the home page: builds by month shared. Why: Tiki-Toki style timelines are popular for legacies; dates come from the Gallery. Effort M. Input: no.

### Workflow / upkeep

1. **Automatic "Checks" panel** that lists mismatches the 12 Oct check found by hand: spreadsheet says not built/shared but on the Gallery, marked shared with no upload, missing lot size or type, duplicate names. Why: the EA-forum lot sheet updates its summary bar automatically; this would make each rebuild self-checking. Effort M. Input: no (she still decides fixes).
2. **Keep Gallery snapshots from the weekly refresh** (date, favourites, downloads, comments) rather than overwriting. Why: needed for the performance page and "biggest gains". Effort S. Input: yes/no on keeping history.
3. **"Data as of" footer line**: spreadsheet date, Gallery refresh date, game patch the maps were checked on. Why: published saves always state the game version; avoids confusion when numbers lag. Effort S. Input: no.
4. **New pack / new world intake checklist** in the notes doc: add world, map from 20th Century Plumbob, colours from map and builds, gridded map for neighbourhood squares, lots and households from the spreadsheet. Why: save creators update with each pack; makes the next world (e.g. a future one after Innisgreen) routine. Effort S. Input: confirm steps.
5. **Spreadsheet columns for new fields** (lot traits, beds by age, eco state, Neighbourhood Stories) so the World Plans sheet stays the single source. Why: avoids more entries in `live_overrides.json`. Effort S. Input: yes (her file, needs her OK).

## 3. Things to avoid

- **AI-generated or AI-upscaled pictures**, including stock "Sims-style" portraits for Sim cards or family trees. Use her screenshots, Gallery pictures and Lucide icons only.
- **EA pack logos or key art as icons**: use pack names or short codes in text instead, keeping the Lucide-only icon set.
- **Tracking everything per Sim** (likes/dislikes, skills, relationships of every townie): Pleasant Sims dropped likes and dislikes as too tedious. Keep Sim detail to households she plays.
- **Percentages outside the Stats page**: status chips should be words, not "64%", to keep her rule.
- **Copying paid templates' layouts or text**, and **public-facing features** (follow buttons, public comments, share counters): the site is for her own use.
- **Values that change every play session** (household funds, exact ages, relationship scores) unless she plays rotationally and wants them; they go stale fast.
- **Heavy new picture sets in the page files**: before/after and Sim pictures should go into the asset store, given the 511-file limit.
- **Phone-first redesigns** while phone layout is parked.
