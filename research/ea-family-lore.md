# EA's premade families: who's who and how they link (overnight 16–17 Oct)

Starting material for family trees and relationship webs (TODO §6d, research/sims-display.md). Research only: nothing on the site, in `data/` or in `tools/` is changed.

- **Sources:** the Sims Wiki, fetched through its API on 16–17 Oct: the 26 world pages with families, every premade Sim's own page (their family box and starting relationships), the family pages (Goth, Landgraab, Monty, Capp, Thebe, Darong, Alto, Laurent, Summerdream, Specter, Crumplebottom, Broke, Dreamer, Curious, Smith, Pleasant and others), Dynasty, and Patch 193 (the Feb 2026 family tree update). Pages are at `https://sims.fandom.com/wiki/<Page_Name>`. Her side comes from `data/worlds/*.json`, `research/worlds/*.md`, `research/ondarion.md`, `research/not-owned-worlds.md` and TODO.md.
- **Ages** are as the game ships them. YA = young adult. Occults are marked (vampire, ghost…).
- **The Sims 4 is officially an alternate timeline.** Sims 2 and Sims 3 history only counts where the Sims 4 itself says so (its family trees, household bios, Ravenwood Rumours). Older-game lore is kept apart in §3.
- **Patch 193** (Feb 2026) added relatives to some trees: Johnny Zest as a Landgraab son, Mortimer Goth's late mother and her family, the Vatores' mother and grandfather, Skip Broke as Dustin's father.

### 2c. Within a world, between households

- **Windenburg:** a friendly town. Bjorn Bjergsen and Jacques Villareal are friends and neighbours; Clara Bjergsen and Mila Munch work together; Dominic Fyres works with Candy Behr and Joaquin Le Chien; Sofia Bjergsen, Luna Villareal and Siobhan Fyres are friends; so are Max Villareal, Elsa Bjergsen and Lucas Munch; Morgan Fyres and Wolfgang Munch with Ulrike Faust; Gunther Munch works with Maaike Haas.
- **Mt. Komorebi:** Kado Akiyama and Megumi Ito are exes (childhood sweethearts, now "awkward friends"). Kiyoshi Ito and Kaori Nishidake are lovebirds, against her grandfather's feud: Shigeru Nishidake dislikes Naoki Ito over his tourism plans. Sachiko Nishidake and Megumi are good friends (Sachiko knew Megumi's mother).
- **Henford-on-Bagley:** Rahmi Watson had an affair with the mayor, Lavina Chopra (exes, now dislike each other). Rahmi's daughter Rashidah and Lavina's son Rahul are lovebirds. Ian Moody and Agatha Crumplebottom are good friends; Sara Scott and Agnes are friends. Cecilia Kang had one awkward date with Michael Bell (NPC).
- **Tartosa:** Hector Laurent and Lucia Markovic are exes who dislike each other, while Hilary Laurent and Lucia are good friends; the two families are business rivals. Mateo Markovic is good friends with Camille Soto, Dominique Soto with Faiz Jaleel.
- **Moonwood Mill:** Rory Oaklow is estranged from her adoptive father Kristopher but loves her adopted brother Jacob. Lou Howell is Celene López's ex. Everyone despises Greg. Wolfgang Wilder is Kristopher's good friend.
- **Copperdale:** Noah Kane (Harjo) has a secret crush on his rival Sidney Price. Ash Harjo and Molly Prescott are good friends; Molly and Savannah Price are rivals.
- **San Sequoia:** Jay Robles (Robles-Ruano) is Ignacio's child, Bernice's sibling and Aurelio's "pibling" (the game's word for aunt or uncle); Tala is Ignacio's granddaughter and Aurelio's cousin. Christopher Michaelson and Xochitl Luna are exes and still good friends; Ian Robles dislikes Xochitl, while Bernice is her good friend.
- **Chestnut Ridge:** Sienna Grove is Umber's sister and Wren's aunt, and Juniper's best friend. Don Gooseman is a friend of Sienna and Umber. Marissa Tracey dislikes Wren.
- **Tomarang:** Thi Linh is Arturo's daughter, Liên's sister and Cam's aunt (the family lives in the unit above her). Arturo and Bua Bun Ma are good friends; Vanesha Cahyaputri and Thi are friends. NPC Panya Li owns the Linh-Sadyas' property.
- **Ciudad Enamorada:** Manuel Aguilar and Mónica López are lovebirds; her son Carlos, his old classmate, dislikes Manuel. Valentina Nadir is friends with Mónica and Carmen Ortega. Mónica's ex-husband left to sculpt lawn gnomes; he isn't in the game (the wiki wonders, unconfirmed, whether he is Glen Nhoam).
- **Ravenwood:** Esther Gomes is Tziporah's sibling. Layne Coffin and Radwan Gomes are exes and good friends. Zelmira Gomes and Nyon Specter are good friends; the Gomeses dislike Olive. Zuleika Izadi is Olive's friend. Kai Aether is Layne's good friend; Ekade Aether and Radwan dislike each other. Lady Ravendancer Goth is a good friend of Esther and Zelmira.
- **Nordhaven:** the Lodens and Hoffmans feud over who invented the Jellybean Cookie (everyone in both families dislikes the other side). The Koopmans are friends with Hattie, Dahlia and Bruno. Cannonball Coleman is friends with Bruno, Hattie, Corey and Zain; Corey and Zain are good friends; Lerato Sarpong and Erik Loden are friends.
- **Innisgreen:** Iris Ladipo (Mystical Besties) is Flora and Ayo's daughter, with a rift between them. Ayo dislikes Elric DeMuray. Nadya Kishka's ex is Joe Trader (NPC).

## 3. Older-game lore (Sims 2 and Sims 3)

Background only; the Sims 4 doesn't carry it over unless noted.

- **Montys (Sims 2 Veronaville):** Patrizio and Isabella (elders); their grandsons Mercutio and Romeo (sons of the late Claudio and Olivia); their son Antonio, a widower with twins Beatrice and Benedick; their daughter Bianca. The feud began with Consort Capp's broken promise to Patrizio. The Sims 4 gives Romeo no relatives at all, so how he ties to her recreated Montys is open.
- **Capps (Sims 2):** Consort and Contessa's grandchildren are Tybalt, Juliette and Hermia (children of Caliban and Cordelia); cousins Miranda, Hal, Desdemona and Ariel. The wiki links the Capps to the Laurent and Thebe families in the older trees too.

- **Darongs:** in the Sims 2 they were Darren Dreamer's maternal ancestors (Sef and Lina; their daughter Delilah Dreamer).
- **Goths:** Bella was born a Bachelor. In the Sims 3, Gunther's sister Frida Goth fled a planned marriage to a Landgraab: the only Goth–Landgraab tie the wiki gives, and it's older-game only.
- **Brokes:** in the Sims 2, Skip was Brandi Broke's late husband and Dustin's father there too. The Sims 4 sets Skip's romantic preference to men.
- **Altos:** Holly comes from the Sims 3 (Sunset Valley, parents Nick and Vita), where her uncle was Bert Alto. The Sims 4 makes Enzo Bert's son, so the cousin link fits.
- **Specters:** Nyon is the Sims 2's Nervous Subject before the Beakers took him; his Sims 2 cousin was Ophelia.
