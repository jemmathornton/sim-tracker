# Gallery findability

Written overnight 16–17 October 2026. Research only: nothing in the app, data, tools or TODO.md was changed, and nothing was posted or changed on the Gallery. Suggestions only; every choice is hers.

Sources: her uploads as of the 14 Oct refresh (`data/gallery/`), EA’s help pages and patch notes, community guides and forum posts (listed at the end), the game’s own interface text, and a light, read-only look at EA’s public Gallery data on 17 Oct (the same data the web Gallery shows). Scripts ran from a scratch folder and weren’t kept. Many groups below are small, so read them as hints, not rules. “Typical” is the middle value, so one big hit doesn’t skew it.

## Headlines

- **Name search reads only the title. Hashtag search reads only the hashtags.** The description text is searched only by a new beta search in the game (Cross-Language, since May 2026).
- **She’s easy to find by name, hard to find by hashtag.** Searching “Oasis Springs Library” puts hers 4th and “Anchorpoint Rec Center” 2nd. But searching #library, #recreationcenter or #oasissprings, most downloaded first, none of hers is in the first 120. The Futures Past is 114th for #museum.
- **How many hashtags hardly matters.** Uploads with 7 to 15 do about the same. Finchwick Village Hall (879 downloads) has six.
- **The description box is full on many uploads.** It seems to stop at 500 characters: 7 uploads are exactly 500 and 40 are within 5 of it. Some of that room goes on doubles that few people search (#playertested, #moveobjectson, #under20000).
- **Hashtag slips:** 17 uploads have a misspelt tag (#winderburg, #britchester, #ecolifestye…). 14 linked uploads have no world tag at all, Izakaya Hanamigawa Koen (613 downloads) among them.
- **Uploads can’t be edited**, as far as I can find. A new title, description or tag means a new upload, which starts again from 0. So most tips are for new uploads.
- **Lot type labels:** the web Gallery shows “Missing sims translation” as the lot type of all 12 Community Space versions and both Recreation Centers. That’s EA’s fault, and her hashtags cover it. Holiday rentals all show as Residential (one as Park), so only hashtags say they’re holiday homes, and none uses #vacationrental.

## 1. How the Gallery finds builds

### Searching

| Search | What it reads | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Item Name (game) | The title only | Every word must be in the title (since Sept 2023); plurals match (“Cat” finds “Cats”) |
| Hashtag (game) | The hashtags only | Exact: #cosy and #cozy are different tags |
| EA Account ID (game) | The creator | Lists all of hers |
| Cross-Language, beta (game only, since 26 May 2026) | Title, hashtags **and description**, in other languages too | Sort doesn’t work with it |
| Text-to-Image, beta (game only) | What’s in the pictures (lots and rooms) | One word works best; a search can be 25 characters at most |
| Web Gallery search | “Search by name or #hashtag” | At least 4 characters |

### Sorting

| Where | Sort options | Opens on |
|---|---|---|
| In game | Newest, Most Popular, Popular Now (the game’s own labels) | Since the 17 Mar 2026 update, most downloaded/popular instead of Newest (players’ reports). On 27 Mar the Sims Team said it was “exploring rolling back” the default view. I found no sign that it happened. The same happened in 2018, and EA promised then to go back to Newest. |
| Web Gallery, Browse (refreshed July 2026) | Newest, Most Downloaded, Most Liked (that’s favourites), Trending | Newest |
| Web Gallery, Discover rows | Maxis Top Picks, Trending, Trending Lots, Popular Households, Base Game Only, Made With Your Packs | |

Her profile page reportedly lists her uploads by downloads too, so new ones sit at the bottom unless she showcases them (the Profile tab lets a creator showcase uploads and write a bio).

### Filters

- **In game:** Households, Lots or Rooms; packs, including hiding builds that need packs the player doesn’t own (“Include Unowned Content”); “Include Custom Content”; “Maxis Curated”; followed creators. Older guides also describe lot filters for size, price and lot type.
- **Web:** Households, Lots or Rooms; Packs; “Liked by me”. No lot type or size filter.
- So the Gallery already knows a build’s size, lot type and packs. Hashtags aren’t needed for those filters; they only matter for hashtag search. The lot type filter uses whatever type the lot had when it was saved.

### Trending

- The game has a “Trending Hashtags” panel (back since Sept 2023).
- Trending seems to be for new uploads only. On 17 Oct, sorting her uploads by Trending listed only her 8 newest (12–14 Oct, plus Crow’s Chronicles); the other 218 didn’t appear. A new upload’s first days are when it’s most visible. Her newest gained up to 9 downloads between 14 and 17 Oct (Bedlington Barn 0 → 9, Tidal Tower 6 → 15).

### Maxis picks

EA’s label is **Maxis Curated** (game) or **Maxis Top Picks** (web); I found no “Staff Pick” label. The Sims Team chooses. From a 2021 summary of a SimGuru forum post:
- no custom content;
- must look right **without** move objects (the team doesn’t test with it): no missing or flickering objects;
- Sims must be able to use everything and reach every room, garden and pool;
- original only: no re-uploads, nothing copyrighted;
- detailed builds preferred, but simple ones get picked; price and season don’t count; small creators are looked for; holiday builds are favoured around holidays.

The public data doesn’t show whether any of hers is a Maxis pick.

### The 500-character limit

Her longest descriptions are exactly 500 characters, and none is longer, so that looks like the game’s limit. 40 uploads are at 495 or more, mostly from 2020–22 (28 of them). On a typical upload the hashtags take about 126 characters. Doubles that use room:
- #playtested and #playertested together: 95 uploads (20 of them at the limit). #playertested is the far less used one.
- #moo and #moveobjectson together: 17.
- #under20k and #under20000 together: 9 (and 7 with only #under20000, the rarely used one).

## 3. Tips (her call)

None of this needs her to write differently: her titles and descriptions stay as she writes them.

### For new uploads

1. **The title is what name search reads.** Her titles that say the place and what it is (Oasis Springs Library, Anchorpoint Rec Center) come up in the top five for those words. Titles that are a name only lean on hashtags.
2. **Small, specific hashtags are where she can be near the top:** the neighbourhood, the game’s lot name, the lot’s own name. Busy ones (#library, #oasissprings) are crowded and mostly help in the first days, when a search is sorted by Newest.
3. **Put the core hashtags first, then the extras**, so nothing important is lost at 500 characters. A core set from her own habits: the lot’s name, the game’s lot name, its neighbourhood, its world (the game’s spelling), what it is (#library, #recreationcenter, #communityspace, #vacationrental), #nocc, #playtested, a “packonly” tag when it’s true, #basegame only when no pack is needed.
4. **Room to spare:** #playertested, #moveobjectson and #under20000 are much less used than #playtested, #moo and #under20k. Dropping the rare twin saves 12 to 15 characters each.
5. **#cosy and #cozy** reach different searches; #cozy is the busier one. Having both reaches both.
6. **A quick read of the hashtags before sharing**, for the spelling of world and neighbourhood names. If she likes, a small checker could do it against her plan (it would need a new script, so only if she wants one).
7. **Set the lot type and the bedrooms and bathrooms before saving.** These are what the filters and the Gallery page show (Residential Rental or University Housing rather than Residential, where that’s what it is).
8. **For holiday rentals**, #vacationrental, since the Gallery shows them as Residential.
9. **The picture:** daytime, with the build in view. An older guide says the save offers morning, afternoon, evening and night, or she can pause the game when the light is right and save then.
10. **Maxis picks** go to builds with no custom content that look right without move objects. The Sims Team doesn’t test with move objects on, so a build that only looks right with it (#moo) won’t be picked.
11. **Profile showcase:** since her profile reportedly lists by downloads, showcasing new uploads there puts them in front of visitors.

### For old uploads

- Descriptions and hashtags can’t be edited after sharing (a player answer on EA’s forums; EA’s help shows no way). A fix means a new upload: it starts from 0 downloads and 0 favourites, while the old copy keeps its numbers and comments. Her one-upload-per-lot rule then applies.
- So fixes are worth it only when she re-uploads anyway (Glimmer & Books as a Backroom, Mossy Lane as University Housing). Then the tags can be fixed at the same time.
- If she ever re-uploads any of these, the findability fixes are: Cottage Am See and Coorinberg Cottage (#windenburg), Drake Hall and Wyvern Hall (#britechester), Izakaya Hanamigawa Koen (#mtkomorebi), 3 Olde Mill Lane (#henfordonbagley), Town Square Stores (#copperdale), Gibbi Rec Center (#jellyfishjunction), Fern Park Flats (#ecolifestyle) and Newcrest Arts Center (#civiccliffs).
- Nothing she can do about “Missing sims translation”: it’s EA’s label. She could report it on EA’s forums if she wants to.
