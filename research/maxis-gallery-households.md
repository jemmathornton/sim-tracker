**Things to know before downloading:**
- **The Beakers' Nervous Subject is your Nyon Specter.** In The Sims 4 he is Olive's son, “pre-Nervous”, and he's a teen in your Windenburg Specter household. Bring the Beakers without him (the earlier proposal kept him in).
- **The Landgraab upload's Malcolm is your teen Malcolm as an elder**, and Mimi and Dudley are his children in the console games. While your Malcolm is a teen, they can only come as cousins (or not at all).
- **“Malpractice” contains your Willow Creek Eric Lewis.** Leave him out; the short shows him as a doctor, so the Doctor career suits your Eric.
- **Gallery uploads don't carry family links to Sims outside the household** (Sebastian and Celeste, Merrilyn and Nigel, Ophelia and Olive, and so on): set them with MCCC, like the other links on your list.
- **Teens and children can't live alone**, so Liam Beckett and the teen and child groups need an adult in their household.
