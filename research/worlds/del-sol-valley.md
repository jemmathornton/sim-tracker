# Del Sol Valley: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Del Sol Valley page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **11 lots, plus the secret Plumbob Pictures studio** (50×50, reached only through the acting career).
- **Mirage Park:** Vacuous Green (empty, 50×40, vacant "after the fires"), Inner Circle (an uninhabited starter home, 40×30), Upland Place (Venessa Jeong, 40×30).
- **The Pinnacles:** Chateau Peak (uninhabited, 64×64, on the tallest hill), The Ward Den (Judith Ward, 50×40), Bailey-Moon Manor (the Bailey-Moons, 50×40).
- **Starlight Boulevard:**
  - Orchid A Go Go: Lounge, 30×30, a "grungy" bar for lesser celebrities.
  - Studio PBP: Lounge, 40×30, the exclusive club with a bouncer.
  - Plumbob Pictures Museum: Museum, 40×30, film props and famous scenes.
  - Pectoral Fitness: Gym, 30×30.
  - Ward Park: National Park, 20×20, a quiet spot to "listen to the sounds of water".
- **Homeless celebrities:** Holly Alto, Baby Ariel, Dustin Broke, Brytani Cho, Dirk Dreamer, Izzy Fabulous and Kayla Flemming.
- **Open world:** the Starlight Boulevard walk of fame, the views from The Pinnacles, and celebrities. Much of the city is decorative shells, and the beach can't be used. Sunny most of the year, no snow.

## Her plan against that

- **Vacuous Green → Strike Out!,** a bowling nightclub (built, shared).
- **Inner Circle and Upland Place → 4-unit rentals** (both built, shared):
  - Inner Circle: "Roomies".
  - Upland Trailer Park: the Eclectic Arts, StrangerVille's game household (their game home is her StrangerVille Library).
- **Venessa Jeong isn't at Upland Place.** Her plan has a "Jeong" household at 1310 21 Chic Street in San Myshuno. Probably Venessa, but that isn't written down anywhere.
- **Chateau Peak:** "My Celebs" (not built).
- **Pectoral Fitness → Valley Views,** a high-end restaurant (built, shared).
- **Ward Park → Starlight Fitness,** the gym (built, shared). So the gym moved onto the park's lot, and the park is gone.
- **Plumbob Pictures Museum → Small Business (Museum & Acting Classes)** (not built).
- Studio PBP (not built) and Orchid A Go Go (built, shared) stay Lounges.

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it).
- "Acting Studio" is listed as not allowed on the ordinary lots: it's the secret studio's own type.
