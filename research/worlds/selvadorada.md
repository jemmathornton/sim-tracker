# Selvadorada: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Selvadorada page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **A holiday world: 7 lots, no homes and no families.** The locals and vendors are townies.
- **Puerto Llamante Marketplace** (the town):
  - Alam Museum of Archaeology: Museum, 64×64.
  - Cantina "El Árbol del Jaguar": Bar, 40×30.
  - Hillview Hideaway (20×15) and Selvadorada Villa (30×20): holiday rentals.
- **Belomisia Jungle:**
  - Belomisia Trailhead: National Park, 40×20. Its archway leads into the jungle.
  - Jungle Bungalow (30×30) and Belomisia Field Station (30×20): holiday rentals.
  - Hidden behind the trailhead: the Omiscan Royal Baths (40×30, a lot with its own type) and the Omiscan Temple (50×50, five layouts that change after each visit). The other jungle areas (plane crash site, gardens, lagoon, island) aren't lots.
- **The game's lot descriptions are blank** for every Selvadorada lot, so there's no game text to compare her plan with.
- **Open world:** market stalls where Sims try local food and buy adventure gear, the statue of Madre Cosecha, and the jungle (ruins, dig sites, artefacts). Sunny all year, no snow, heat waves in summer. No festivals and no eco footprint.

## Her plan against that

- **One type change:** Belomisia Field Station is a Custom Venue (built, shared; settled 14 Oct). Her upload calls it a base for exploring the jungle, with shared dorms, dinner, a pool and archaeology mentoring.
- **Kept as the game has them:** the three holiday rentals (built, shared; Hillview Hideaway is her "Hillside Hideaway") and the Cantina (built, shared).
- **Not built:** the Alam Museum, Belomisia Trailhead, and the Omiscan Royal Baths (added 15 Oct as a secret lot).
- The Omiscan Temple is left out on purpose (its layout resets itself).

## Allowed lot types

- **No homes of any kind:** Residential, Residential Rental, Tiny Home Residential and University Housing aren't allowed. Nor are Retail or Beach.
- **Holiday rentals must use "Vacation Rental - Selvadorada";** the plain Vacation Rental isn't allowed. Her save has the right one on all three.
- Restaurant, Café, Small Business Venue, Custom Venue and Community Market are all allowed.
- "Omiscan Royal Baths" is listed as not allowed on the ordinary lots: it's the hidden lot's own type.

## Notes for later

- **A holiday world, so no everyday places are needed** (no library, gym or shops).
- **No restaurant** (still open on the TODO §5 restaurant list). Food comes from the market stalls and the Cantina. Every lot already has a job; the unbuilt ones are the museum, the trailhead and the baths.
- **Belomisia Field Station:** her upload describes somewhere to stay, but in the game only Vacation Rental lots can be booked for a stay. As a Custom Venue it's a place to visit, not a holiday base. Noted only; the type was settled 14 Oct.
- **The Alam Museum is the biggest lot (64×64) and isn't built yet.** It fits the "Ancient Artifacts" highlight, and a Museum runs by itself.
- **Build notes already on TODO §6b:** the Omiscan Royal Baths must keep the gateway to the Omiscan Gardens and the dig sites (free build needed). A National Park, like the trailhead, needs a grill, an outdoor bin and a picnic table.
