# Gibbi Point: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Gibbi Point page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **13 lots.** A getaway destination that also has residents.
- **Wanderwood Wilds** (the forest round Hothead Rock Geyser):
  - Hothead Rock Hideaway: empty lot, 64×64, with views of the Old Firewatch Tower.
  - Camp Gibbi Gibbi: Custom Venue (a getaway camp), 50×50, lake and dock. Nigel Chavez-Ralston is the camp director.
  - Wanderwood Wonder: the Chavez-Ralstons, 30×20, by the Butterfly Ranger Station.
  - Plumbird Park: Playground, 40×40, looking over the geyser.
- **Jellyfish Junction** (the arty seaside town; kayaking):
  - Jellyfish Jubilee: holiday rental, 40×30.
  - Seabreeze Scenic: the Perrys, 40×30.
  - Fletcher's Cottage: empty starter home, 30×20.
  - Revive and Thrive Retreat: Custom Venue, 64×64, Freeda Perry's fitness retreat.
- **Crystal Valley** (the old gold-mining town, now crystals):
  - Prospector's Paradise: empty lot, 40×30, by Scoop's Gold (crystal panning).
  - Crystal Cottage: empty modern home with a pool, 30×20.
  - Love Highland: the Addam Commune, 50×50. Their home and their "Love Highland Getaway" (hot tub, kayaking).
  - Fearful Fish Lounge: Lounge, 20×20, for anglers.
  - Miner's Manse: empty home, 30×20.
- **Homeless townies:** Laxmi and Raheel Kunal are homeless premade townies (corrected 17 Oct, research/townies-and-roles.md; the wiki world page doesn't list them).
- **Open world:** Hothead Rock Geyser, the Electric Lagoon, Underlight Bay, archery, kayaking, pools with diving boards, a public playground in each neighbourhood, and underground hot springs (in the blurb). Festivals include Fitness Fest and Glow and Flow.

## Her plan against that

- **Revive and Thrive moves.** Hothead Rock Hideaway becomes "Revive and Thrive", Custom Venue (built, shared): the business's second site, with fitness, yoga, massage and a natural hot spring.
- **Revive and Thrive Retreat → Gibbi Rec Center** (built, shared): play areas, puzzle rooms, workout and creative spaces, a splash zone, playground and pool.
- **Fletcher's Cottage → Gibbi Point Library** (built, shared).
- **Jellyfish Jubilee:** holiday rental → Restaurant (not built).
- **Fearful Fish Lounge → Thrift 'n' Fish,** Thrift and Boba Store (not built).
- **Love Highland:** the Addams' home → Custom Venue (not built). The Addams move to Miner's Manse.
- **Crystal Cottage → Onsen Bathhouse** (15 Oct).
- **Prospector's Paradise → Paradise Stables,** a home with horses (built, not shared; residents undecided).
- **Unchanged, not built:** Camp Gibbi Gibbi, Plumbird Park, Wanderwood Wonder (Chavez-Ralstons), Seabreeze Scenic (Perrys).

## Allowed lot types

- Every everyday type is allowed. Beach isn't, even though Gibbi Point has waterfront lots (the second world with them, after Sulani).

## Notes for later

- **No bar, lounge or nightclub now.** Fearful Fish Lounge was the only one, and Thrift 'n' Fish counts as a café. Jellyfish Jubilee (restaurant, not built) is the only evening place. For a holiday world that may be enough.
- **No holiday rental now.** Jellyfish Jubilee was the game's only one. Visitors would come for Getaways at the three custom venues (Revive and Thrive, Camp Gibbi Gibbi, Love Highland) instead.
- **Love Highland:** in the game it's both the Addams' home and their getaway business. A Getaway can run on a home lot too, with guests paying the household (research/lot-types-reference.md). Worth knowing before it's built.
- **Wellness three ways:** the Rec Center (gym, pool), Revive and Thrive (fitness, spa, hot spring) and the Crystal Cottage onsen. That fits the hot springs and Fitness Fest, and covers the missing gym in practice.
- **Paradise Stables (TODO §5):** residents still to decide. All three game families are housed, and no home in the world stands empty. The game sets the lot by Scoop's Gold for crystal panning.
- **Custom Venues:** Gibbi Point has 3 of the 5 in her save.
