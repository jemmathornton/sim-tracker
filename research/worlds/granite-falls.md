# Granite Falls: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Granite Falls page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **A holiday world (Outdoor Retreat).** Sims stay for up to a week and can't live there. No families.
- **Campground: five holiday rentals:** Campground (30×20), Forest Hideaway (40×30), Green Getaway (30×20), Lakeside Retreat (40×30) and Riverside Retreat (30×20).
- **Granite Falls Forest:** National Park, 50×50, with the secret Hermit's House (30×20) deep in the woods.
- **Other holidaymakers:** while her household is on holiday, the game fills the empty rentals with random households from the save. Unplayed Sims also turn up like townies.
- **Open world:** hiking, campfires for grilling and stories, ranger stations selling camping gear, bears, insect collecting, and the giant Ranger Stanley Jackalope statue at the campsite. Snowy winters, hot summers.
- No lot descriptions in the game's files.
- The wiki says it's based on Washington's forests, and was first going to be called Misty Pines.

## Her plan against that

- **Two of the five rentals become community lots** (built, shared):
  - Forest Hideaway → The Forest Tavern (Restaurant). Her upload: "Nobody wants to cook on holiday anyway!"
  - Riverside Retreat → Granite Falls Spa (a natural pool and yoga with a forest view).
- **Three stay holiday rentals** (built, shared): Campground, Green Getaway and Lakeside Retreat.
- **Granite Falls Forest** stays a National Park. It and the Hermit's House are not built.
- **No homes** (her view: a holiday world).

## Allowed lot types

- **No homes possible:** Residential, Residential Rental, Tiny Home Residential and University Housing aren't allowed. The only rental type is "Vacation Rental - Granite Falls".
- **Retail isn't allowed** (Small Business is). Nor is Beach.
- Restaurant, Café, Bar, Spa, Community Market and the other community types are all allowed.
- Hermit's Hut isn't on the list for ordinary lots: it's fixed on the secret lot.

## Notes for later

- **Three rentals means at most two other households staying** while hers is there (the game fills empty rentals). Fewer rentals, fewer holiday neighbours; townies still wander in.
- **Holiday basics are covered:** somewhere to eat (The Forest Tavern), to unwind (the spa), the park, and the open-world campfires and ranger stations. Everyday places (library, gym, shops) aren't needed.
- **No café or bar.** For a camping holiday that's probably fine; the game would allow either.
- **Granite Falls Forest is unbuilt.** A National Park needs a grill, an outdoor bin and a picnic table (game files).
- Her save has The Forest Tavern and the spa set as holiday rentals; the plan and Gallery say Restaurant and Spa. The three rentals' uploads show as Residential (the Gallery doesn't keep the holiday rental type).
