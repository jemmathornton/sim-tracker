# Chestnut Ridge: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Chestnut Ridge page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **13 lots: 9 homes, 1 holiday rental, 3 community lots.**
- **New Appaloosa** (the old town: the equestrian centre, the dance hall, nectar):
  - Champion's Grove: Sienna & Flapjack, 30×20.
  - The Cozy Corner House: empty cottage, 20×20, with a pen for mini goats and sheep.
  - The Oak Barrel: Bar, 30×20 (classy nectar tasting).
  - The Rusty Horseshoe: Nightclub, 30×20, the dance hall (food and ranch dancing).
  - Duke's Hall: Park, 40×30, a rest stop for riders and horses with a social space.
- **Riders' Glen** (ranchers and nectar makers, under Ol' Biscuit):
  - Biscuit's Bastion: the Groves, 40×30.
  - Sweet Nectar Glade: the Nectar Making Duo, 30×20.
  - Canter Cottage: empty home, 30×30, set up for livestock and horses.
  - Grapevine Terrace: empty home, 30×20, made for nectar making.
  - Red Roan Field: empty lot, 64×64, for a ranch.
- **Galloping Gulch** (trails and views):
  - Canyon Crossing: the Goosemans (Don and his horse Duke), 20×20.
  - Palomino Junction: empty cabin, 50×50.
  - Big Sky Reach: holiday rental, 20×15.
- **Nobody is without a home.**
- **Open world:** the Hay Now! Equestrian Center (competitions; Sienna runs it), a clothing shop and a grocery in the town centre, jumps and barrels in public spaces, horse trails with camp spots, Dreadhorse Caverns, rare fishing spots, the ghost horse.

## Her plan against that

- **The Oak Barrel:** Bar → Restaurant (built, shared): nectar with an experimental food menu.
- **Duke's Hall → Appaloosa Stables,** a home for a horse-loving elder couple (not a game family; built, shared). Her upload makes it a community stable with six horses where visitors are welcome.
- **Sweet Nectar Glade:** the Duo's home → Bar (stays a bar beside the vineyard, her choice). The Duo move to Grapevine Terrace (not built).
- **The Cozy Corner House → Library** (not built; no name of her own yet).
- **Canter Cottage → Canter Clinic,** Vet Clinic (not built).
- **Red Roan Field → Red Roan Farm,** for her own Sims ("Mine", not made yet). Built, shared.
- **Palomino Junction:** the Palumbos' horse-rescue ranch (not a game family; built, shared).
- **The Rusty Horseshoe** stays a Nightclub (it's the game's dance hall).
- **Unchanged:** Champion's Grove, Biscuit's Bastion and Canyon Crossing (not built); Big Sky Reach, Vacation Rental (built, shared).

## Allowed lot types

- Every everyday type is allowed, Café, Cemetery, Park and Community Market included. Beach isn't.
- There's no ranch lot type; ranches and farms are Residential in the game.

## Notes for later

- **No Park lot now.** Duke's Hall, the game's only park, is Appaloosa Stables. Her build keeps it open to visitors, and the Gulch's trails and camp spots work as open-world parkland.
- **No café and no cemetery,** two of the places on the village list. Pub, library and vet are there.
- **Every lot now has a type or residents,** and no home stands empty, so any new place means swapping something. The unbuilt ones are The Cozy Corner House (Library), Sweet Nectar Glade (Bar), Canter Clinic, The Rusty Horseshoe and four family homes.
- **Shops:** the open-world clothing shop and grocery. A Community Market here is still an open idea (TODO §8).
- **Gym:** none; horse riding and the trails do that job here.
- **Nights out are well covered:** a nightclub, a bar and a nectar restaurant.
- **Game texts match the plan well:** Canter Cottage is written for livestock and horses (now the vet), Grapevine Terrace for nectar makers (now the Duo).
