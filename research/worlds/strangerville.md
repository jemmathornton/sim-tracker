# StrangerVille: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's StrangerVille page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **12 lots: 9 homes, 2 community lots and The ██████ Lab** (64×64, a special lot that can't be edited).
- **StrangerVille Plaza:**
  - 8 Bells: Bar, 20×20, near the military base, where soldiers wind down.
  - StrangerVille Information Center: Library, 20×20, with the "StrangerVille Archive", where scientists research.
  - Carpophagous Corner (uninhabited, 30×20), Slip 42 (uninhabited, 20×15, the trailer lot a conspiracy theorist walked out of), Old Penelope (George Cahill, 50×40, his crashed cargo plane), Riverside Grove (the Sigworths, 30×20).
- **Shady Acres** (Victorian houses with the best views): StrangerVille Overlook (the Roswells, 40×30), Dream Weavers Way (the Eclectic Arts, 30×20), Plateau Place (uninhabited, 40×30), Cliff Side Crest and Creek Corner Cove (empty, 30×20).
- **Families:**
  - Sigworths: Jess works on the base.
  - George Cahill: a retired pilot.
  - Roswells: Ted is the mayor behind the lab.
  - Eclectic Arts: three artist friends.
  - Erwin Pries, the conspiracy theorist, is an NPC.
- **Open world:** the Curio Shop stand by the trailer park, the crater and the base, soldiers and scientists about town, and the "strange weather" of the story. Sunny, heat waves in summer, no snow. It's the only residential world with no eco footprint.

## Her plan against that

- **Information Center: Library → Backroom** (not built).
- **Dream Weavers Way → StrangerVille Library** (built, not shared). The Eclectic Arts move to Upland Trailer Park in Del Sol Valley.
- **The Roswells move from StrangerVille Overlook to Creek Corner Cove** (not built). The Overlook becomes SU Overlook Residence, University Housing, empty (built, not shared).
- **Plateau Place → SU Plateau Residence,** University Housing, empty (built, not shared).
- **Cliff Side Crest → StrangerVille Gym&Pool** (built, not shared).
- **Carpophagous Corner:** Vacation Rental (built, shared; settled 14 Oct).
- **Slip 42:** a 2-unit rental, empty (built, shared).
- **Unchanged:** 8 Bells (Bar, not built), Old Penelope (Cahill, not built), Riverside Grove (Sigworths, built, not shared).

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it).
- "Secret Lab" is listed as not allowed on the ordinary lots: it's the crater lab's own type.

## Notes for later

- **The story lots are settled:** 8 Bells stays a Bar and Dream Weavers Way the Library; the Information Center is a Backroom.
- **Backroom build note:** with bookcases as its three daytime objects it brings readers by day, which matches the game's "archive" text. It also needs a bar, a stereo, 4 barstools, a sink, a toilet and a bin.
- **No restaurant or café** (restaurants are still to look at, TODO §5). A diner is the classic desert-town choice.
- **Nothing unbuilt is spare.** The unbuilt lots all have a job (8 Bells, the Information Center, Old Penelope, Creek Corner Cove). The lots with nobody in them are all built: Slip 42, Carpophagous Corner and the two SU residences.
- **No park or shops as lots.** The Curio Shop stand covers odd shopping, and it's a desert town.
- Carpophagous Corner's game text puts it "right across from the StrangerVille Library and the hottest bar in town", so the game's library is the Information Center.
- Her save has Slip 42 as Residential; the plan and its rental upload say Residential Rental.
