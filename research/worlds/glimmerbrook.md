# Glimmerbrook: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Glimmerbrook page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **5 lots, plus The Magic Realm** (a special lot through the portal).
  - Rock Ridge Canyon: the Charms, 40×30, a quiet spot for spellcasters to study.
  - Creek Side Corner: the Amicable Acolytes, 30×20, with views of Glimmerbrook Falls.
  - Brooks Bridge Borough: uninhabited, 30×20, a simple cottage by the fishing spots and the bridge.
  - Glimmerbrook Watch: empty, 30×20. Its last owner guarded a relic atop the waterfall and vanished one night.
  - Elixirs and Brews: Bar, 30×20, the only community lot, "a local establishment" that's "full of nostalgic charm".
- **Families:**
  - Charms: Minerva, Darrel, Gemma, and Darrel's fiancée Emilia.
  - Amicable Acolytes: Grace and Tomax, roommates racing to become Adept.
  - The three Sages (L. Faba, Morgyn Ember, Simeon Silversweater) belong to the Magic Realm.
- **Open world:** the portal above the waterfall, Casters Alley in the Magic Realm (wands, potions and brooms), and the Sages. Snowy winters, plenty of rain. The game has no description for the neighbourhood.

## Her plan against that

- **Elixirs and Brews → Glimmer & Books,** a Library with magic books and practice space for spellcasters (built, shared). So Glimmerbrook has no bar now.
- **The Amicable Acolytes move from Creek Side Corner to Brooks Bridge,** now a Haunted House Residential (built, shared; settled 14 Oct). Her upload adds rumoured spirits; the game's text has none.
- **Creek Side Corner and Glimmerbrook Watch:** homes built for "3 Bed" and "1 Bed", no household named yet (both built, shared).
- **Rock Ridge Canyon:** the Charms (not built).
- **The Magic Realm:** built, shared, 50×40 (the game's size).

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it).
- "The Magic Realm" is listed as not allowed on the ordinary lots: it's the special lot's own type.

## Notes for later

- **A quiet village with only a library.** Villages usually have a pub, a café or tea room, a library or village hall, a cemetery and a vet. Glimmerbrook has no restaurant, café, bar or pub, park, gym, cemetery or vet.
- **The game's one community lot was the pub.** Its in-game name (Elixirs and Brews) and its "local establishment" text read like a bar. Glimmer & Books kept that text in its upload.
- **Restaurants:** still to look at for Glimmerbrook (TODO §5).
- **No spare lot.** The only homes with no household named are Creek Side Corner and Glimmerbrook Watch, both built and shared. A pub, café or tea room would mean a rebuild, or a small business run from one of those homes once someone lives there (it would only open when she plays the owners).
- **Glimmerbrook Watch gets the most favourites for its downloads of all her uploads** (research/gallery-deep-dive.md). Worth knowing before changing it.
- **Magic shopping is covered:** Casters Alley sells wands, potions and brooms.
- The game's ghost-story text belongs to Glimmerbrook Watch (the owner who vanished), not to Brooks Bridge, her haunted house. Only matters if she wants the game text and the plan to match.
