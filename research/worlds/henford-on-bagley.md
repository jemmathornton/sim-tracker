# Henford-on-Bagley: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Henford-on-Bagley page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **12 lots, only 2 of them community lots** (the pub and the National Park).
- **Finchwick** (the old mill village; a square with a pub, stalls and a garden):
  - The Gnome's Arms: Bar, 30×20, owned by the Scotts. Local food downstairs, football on the telly upstairs.
  - 1 Cobblebottom Street: empty home, 20×15.
  - 3 Cobblebottom Street: the Scotts, 20×20.
  - 5 Cobblebottom Street: empty home, 30×20.
- **Old New Henford** (estates and the community garden; eco footprint starts green):
  - 2 Olde Mill Lane: empty lot, 50×40.
  - 3 Olde Mill Lane: empty riverside home, 50×40.
  - 4 Olde Mill Lane: Cecilia Kang, 30×20, a converted barn.
  - Olde Mill Hill: the Watsons, 64×64.
- **The Bramblewood** (the woods; eco footprint starts fully green):
  - 13 Nettle Lane: holiday rental, 30×20.
  - 14 Nettle Lane: Moody & McMillan, 40×30.
  - Cordelia's Secret Cottage: empty home, 30×20, above Cordelia Falls.
  - Isle of Volpe Park: National Park, 50×40, with ruins (the plan's Isle of Volpe).
- **Without a home:** the Chopras (Lavina, the village mayor, and her son Rahul). The Crumplebottoms (the garden shop stall) are hidden.
- **Open world:** Finchwick's stalls (grocer, garden shop, Creature Keeper), the weekly fair, the community garden, swimming holes, ruins, and wild birds, rabbits and foxes. The lore mentions a Finchwick cemetery.

## Her plan against that

- **5 Cobblebottom Street → Finchwick Village Hall**, Library (built, shared). Her build has a bar and wedding hall downstairs, a library upstairs and a graveyard out front. Her third most downloaded build. Stays a Library.
- **2 Olde Mill Lane:** empty lot → Vet Clinic for the Watson-Holmes household (not a game family), who live next door at 3 Olde Mill Lane (built, shared). Stays a vet.
- **Moody & McMillan move** from 14 Nettle Lane to 3 Cobblebottom Street, now a 2-unit rental they own and live in, with Cecilia Kang as their tenant (built, not shared). The Scotts are listed on the pub as NPCs.
- **4 Olde Mill Lane → The Olde Mill,** an empty home (built, not shared).
- **13 Nettle Lane:** holiday rental → empty home (not built).
- **Empty homes:** 1 Cobblebottom Street (built), The Olde Mill (built), Cordelia's Secret Cottage (built, shared), 13 and 14 Nettle Lane (not built).
- **Unchanged, not built:** The Gnome's Arms, Olde Mill Hill (Watsons), Isle of Volpe.

## Allowed lot types

- Every everyday type is allowed, Café, Cemetery and Community Market included. Beach isn't.

## Notes for later

- **Against the village list** (pub, café or tea room, library or village hall, cemetery, vet): pub, village hall and vet are there. No café or tea room. No Cemetery lot, though the Village Hall has a graveyard.
- **A tea room is the gap a posh English village would feel most.** Finchwick's only free lot is 1 Cobblebottom Street (20×15, built, empty, not shared). The unbuilt free lots (13 and 14 Nettle Lane) are out in the woods.
- **Shops:** Finchwick's stalls and the weekly fair cover them. A Community Market here is still an open idea (TODO §8), though they do much the same.
- **13 Nettle Lane's game text is a holiday let** ("the perfect rental for any Sim wanting to visit"). Henford has no holiday rental now. A B&B custom venue there was an idea not taken (15 Oct).
- **The Chopras have no home in the game or the plan,** while five homes stand empty. Lavina is the mayor, so a Finchwick home would fit her story.
- **Restaurant and gym:** none; the pub serves food, and a village wouldn't have a gym.
- **Open TODO items:** 3 Cobblebottom Street's units and households (§2).
