# Magnolia Promenade: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Magnolia Promenade page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **A shopping street (Get to Work): one neighbourhood, four lots, no families.**
  - Paddywack's Emporium: Retail, 20×20, toys and collectables.
  - JF&S Clothiers: Retail, 20×20, a clothes shop.
  - The Roadstead: Retail, 40×30, the biggest lot, a general store (mannequins, electronics).
  - Preeminent Domain: empty, 30×20.
- **Highlights:** "Scout Out Your Shop Location" (it's meant for player-run shops), a small playground with an outdoor eating area, and riverside seats where the ducks play.
- **Open world:** also a fishing spot, and Willow Creek's paddle steamer docked by the promenade.
- **Next to Willow Creek?** The wiki thinks it's in or near downtown Willow Creek: same architecture, and it shares its name with Magnolia Blossom Park.
- **Get to Work's hidden lots:** the Police Station and Willow Creek Hospital face each other in one plaza; Sixam is the alien world.
- No lot descriptions in the game's files.

## Her plan against that

- **Only one of the three shops stays a shop:**
  - Paddywack's Emporium → Paddy's Place, a Restaurant (built, not shared).
  - JF&S Clothiers → The Llama Picturehouse, a cinema with three screens and a private one (Small Business/Bar; both versions are on the Gallery, the Small Business one leads).
  - The Roadstead → Magnolia Mall, Retail/Thrift and Boba Store (not built).
- **Preeminent Domain → Magnolia Bowling Alley** (Bar: bowling, arcade games, a private league upstairs; built, shared).
- **Secret Lots group:** Police Station, FutureSim Labs, Willow Creek Hospital and Sixam (none built). Kept here for now (15 Oct).
- **No homes** (her view: a shopping world).

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it). Homes and rentals are allowed too; she's chosen none.
- Police Station, FutureSim Labs, the hospital's type and Sixam's aren't on the list for ordinary lots: they're fixed on their hidden lots.

## Notes for later

- **The shopping world has one shop, and it isn't built.** A lot takes one type in game, so Magnolia Mall will be either Retail (opens only when she plays the owners) or a Thrift and Boba Store (runs itself, counts as a café).
- **What runs on its own:** Paddy's Place and the bowling alley. The Picturehouse runs itself as a Bar but only opens with its owner as a Small Business.
- **Park, library and gym** are missing by lot type, but the open-world playground, picnic area and duck-watching seats do a park's job, and a library or gym wouldn't suit a shopping street.
- **Nights out for Willow Creek:** with its own Blue Velvet now a café and Movers & Shakers a Recreation Center, the bowling alley, cinema and restaurant here are its nearest evenings out.
- **Where the Secret Lots belong (open, TODO §5):** the wiki is split. Its Willow Creek page puts the Police Station and Hospital in a hidden Willow Creek area, this page says they seem to be set here, and its Oasis Springs page puts the Science Lab (FutureSim Labs) in Oasis Springs. The game's files only show each in its own Get to Work hidden area, not which world it belongs to.
- **Other open items:** The Llama Picturehouse is in the small business type check (TODO §5). Paddy's Place is built but not shared. The bowling alley is Residential in her save; the plan and Gallery say Bar.
