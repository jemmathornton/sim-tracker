# Brindleton Bay: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Brindleton Bay page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **16 lots: 11 homes and 5 community lots.**
- **Sable Square:** Pupperstone Park (Park, 40×30, a playground and a dog obstacle course), Brindleton Pawspital (Vet Clinic, 40×30), It's A Good House (the Heckings, 40×40), Domus Familiaris (uninhabited, 30×30), Sporting Space (empty, 30×20).
- **Whiskerman's Wharf:** Club Calico (Lounge, 40×30, a former yacht club with a pool and wedding area), Salty Paws Saloon (Bar, 20×15), Catscratch Cottage (the Lynxes, 20×15), Ragdoll Refurb (uninhabited, 20×20, a converted cannery warehouse), Tail's End (empty, 40×30).
- **Cavalier Cove:** Chateau Frise (the Delgatos, 40×40), Bedlington Boathouse (uninhabited, 20×20), Hound's Head (uninhabited, 64×64, the biggest lot, with a private beach), Dachshund's Creek and Hindquarter Hideaway (empty, 30×20).
- **Deadgrass Isle:** Deadgrass Discoveries (Museum, 30×30), the old lighthouse keeper's home, now a museum of the Bay's maritime history.
- **Families:**
  - Heckings: Brent, Brant and their dog Rosie.
  - Lynxes: Catarina and four cats.
  - Delgatos: Supriya dreams of being a vet, and the game says the Pawspital's owner wants to sell to a young vet.
  - Mayor Whiskers is a stray cat.
- **Open world:** sandy dog-walking paths in Cavalier Cove, the Brindleton Light on Deadgrass Isle (a woohoo spot), the wharf (sea breeze, chowder, lobster rolls and the local cats), and the pet monument in Sable Square. Deadgrass Isle has "rumours about strange visitors after sundown". Snowy winters.

## Her plan against that

- **Salty Paws Saloon → The Bay Café,** a cat café (Café; built, shared).
- **Catscratch Cottage → a seafood restaurant** (not built). The Lynxes don't appear anywhere in her plan.
- **Ragdoll Refurb → Brindleton Library** (built, shared).
- **Dachshund's Creek → Creek Clinic,** a second vet. The Venturas are named on it and on Hindquarter Hideaway in the plan (both built, shared). The Pawspital stays a vet too (not built).
- **Deadgrass Discoveries: Museum → Cemetery** (not built).
- **Club Calico → Calico,** still a Lounge (built, shared).
- **Homes:**
  - The Heckings and Delgatos stay in their game homes (not built).
  - Tail's End Apartments: a 4-unit rental, empty (built, shared).
  - Brindleton Boathouse (Sporting Space) and Bedlington Barn: built, shared, empty.
  - Domus Familiaris: built as a "Large 3 Bedroom" home, no household named.
  - Hound's Head: a farm, empty, not built.

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it). Restaurant, Retail, Vet Clinic and Community Market are all allowed.

## Notes for later

- **A seaside pet town keeping both vets** (settled). The Delgatos' story fits the Pawspital, as Supriya wants to buy it. Its owner isn't in the plan yet.
- **The Lynxes have no home** now Catscratch Cottage is the restaurant. Empty homes in her plan: Tail's End Apartments (4 empty units, same neighbourhood), Bedlington Barn, Brindleton Boathouse, Domus Familiaris and Hound's Head.
- **No bar or pub.** The game's Salty Paws Saloon is now the café; Calico (a lounge) covers evenings.
- **No shops.** Retail only opens when its owners are played (corrected 16 Oct: you can visit an NPC-run small business or shop as a customer, e.g. Nordhaven's Koffieboon; she has done it).
- **No museum.** Deadgrass Discoveries' game text is a maritime museum; her plan makes it a cemetery. The isle's name and its after-dark rumours suit a cemetery; the lighthouse keeper text doesn't. It isn't built, so either is easy.
- **The wharf highlight (chowder and lobster rolls) suits the seafood restaurant.** Catscratch Cottage is small (20×15).
- **Hound's Head (64×64) is the one big lot with nobody in it and nothing built.**
