# Oasis Springs: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Oasis Springs page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **21 lots (16 homes, 5 community lots) in six neighbourhoods,** plus the secret Forgotten Grotto (15×10) in Desert Bloom Park.
- **Bedrock Strait** (kitsch billboards, lawn gnomes, Barry the Brontosaurus): Slipshod Mesquite (Johnny Zest, 40×30); Agave Abode, Nookstone and Sandtrap Flat (uninhabited); Pebble Burrow (empty).
- **Parched Prospect** (tumbleweeds and cacti): Cacti Casa (the Roomies, 40×30); Vista Quarry, Raffia Quinta and Springscape (uninhabited); Dusty Turf (empty).
- **Skyward Palms** (gated, green lawns): Sultry Springside (the Calientes, with Don Lothario); Granada Place and Rio Verde (uninhabited); Arid Ridge (empty, 40×30).
- **Acquisition Butte:** Affluista Mansion (the Landgraabs, 50×50); Yuma Heights (uninhabited, 40×30).
- **Mirage Canyon:** Burners & Builders (Gym, 40×30), The Futures Past (Museum), Rattlesnake Juice (Bar), The Solar Flare (Lounge).
- **Desert Bloom Park:** Park, 50×50, the only area with a green eco footprint.
- **Open world:** the abandoned mine near Acquisition Butte, Mirage Canyon's waterfall and lookout, Barry the Brontosaurus. Sunny most of the year, summer heat waves, no settling snow.
- **Get to Work:** the wiki puts the Science Lab (FutureSim Labs) in a hidden Oasis Springs area (her plan keeps it in Magnolia Promenade's Secret Lots).
- The game's files have no description for any Oasis Springs lot.

## Her plan against that

- **Three homes become community lots** (all built, shared):
  - Slipshod Mesquite → De Costadas, an Italian Restaurant run by the De Costas, who live at Dusty Turf (built, not shared).
  - Cacti Casa → Palm Springs Public Pool (indoor and outdoor pools, hot tub, bar).
  - Arid Ridge → Lotus Ridge Spa (gym, pool, bars, yoga, sauna, massage).
- **The Solar Flare:** Lounge → Oasis Springs Library.
- **Rio Verde** → Rio Verde Retirement Home, a rental with 6 flats for elders.
- **Game families:**
  - The Landgraabs stay at Affluista Mansion (not built).
  - The Calientes move to Yuma Heights (not built); Sultry Springside goes to the Smiths.
  - Don Lothario lives alone at Agave Abode.
  - Johnny Zest isn't in her plan.
  - The Roomies aren't in Oasis Springs. A household called Roomies rents Inner Circle in Del Sol Valley (maybe them, not confirmed).
- **Other households:** Leah-Moon Cadberry (Nookstone), the Riveras (Raffia Quinta, tiny home), the Curious household (Granada Place). Sandtrap Flat, Pebble Burrow, Vista Quarry and Springscape have no family named.
- Mirage Canyon keeps the game's gym, museum and bar.

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it).
- Forgotten Grotto's type isn't on the list for ordinary lots: it's fixed on the secret lot.

## Notes for later

- **No café.** The bars at the pool and the spa and Rattlesnake Juice are the nearest. A Thrift and Boba Store would count as one.
- **No spare unbuilt lot.** The only unbuilt lots are the Landgraab and Caliente homes. A café would mean reusing a built lot; Springscape is the one home with nobody planned ("Empty", built, shared).
- **No shops,** but retail only opens when she plays the owners. No cemetery or vet either.
- **Suits a hot desert suburb already:** a pool, a spa, a gym, and Desert Bloom with a splash pool, slip and slide, barbecue, fishing and a playground (her upload).
