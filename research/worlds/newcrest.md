# Newcrest: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Newcrest page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **15 empty lots, no builds and no families.** The only Sims 4 world with no premade lots (added free in June 2015).
- **Bridgeview** ("family-friendly", a picnic area, a city skyline backdrop): Fern Park and Beech Byway (30×20), Midtown Meadows (40×30), Oak Alcove and Comfy Cubby (20×15).
- **Llama Lagoon** (lakeside walkways, public playground equipment): Twin Oracle Point (50×40, the biggest), Avarice Acres and Rippling Flats (40×30), Tranquil Crescent (30×30), Asphalt Abodes (30×20).
- **Ridgeline Drive** (a busy strip with hiking trails): Sandy Run (40×20), Cookout Lookout, Civic Cliffs and Hillside Highlands (30×20), Optimist's Outlook (40×30).
- **Highlights:** lots of space, peace and quiet, room to jog. The skyline's houses and high-rises are decoration only.
- No lot descriptions in the game's files. Every area starts neutral for eco footprint.

## Her plan against that

Everything is hers: 8 homes and 7 community lots, all built and shared except Rippling Flats.

- **Bridgeview:**
  - Fern Park Flats: rental, 5 flats with a gym and laundry, a household called BFF (the name of Willow Creek's game household).
  - Midtown Meadows: rental, 5 terraced houses, Single Mum Friends.
  - Newcrest Community Space: all four versions built (market, garden, makers, community space).
  - Oak Alcove (Versaree) and Comfy Cubby (empty).
- **Llama Lagoon:**
  - The Ice Pavilion (Park): ice rink in winter, roller rink in summer, food stalls and swings.
  - Twin Oracle Point (empty), Avarice Acres (LeBlanc), Asphalt Abodes (Sylvia and Kenny Cadberry).
  - Rippling Flats: rental, 2 units, for her current uni Sims (not built).
- **Ridgeline Drive, the commercial strip:** The Aqua Grill (seafood Restaurant), Newcrest Wedding Hall, Newcrest Arts Center, Cookies 'n' Cream (Retail: bakery and ice cream) and Optimist's Outlook (Small Business: woodworking, pottery, tattoos, music, a playground).
- **Her view (15 Oct):** Newcrest is a suburb of San Myshuno, so no gym.

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it).

## Notes for later

- **Still without:** a bar or pub, a library and a pool. By the same suburb reasoning as the gym, San Myshuno's bars, clubs and karaoke cover nights out; the Wedding Hall has a bar for parties.
- **Neither Newcrest nor San Myshuno has a library.** The nearest are Oasis Springs Library and Willow Creek Archive (not built).
- **Two lots open only when she plays the owners:** Cookies 'n' Cream (Retail) and Optimist's Outlook (Small Business). Running on their own: the Community Space, The Ice Pavilion, The Aqua Grill, the Wedding Hall and the Arts Center.
- **Plenty for children:** Llama Lagoon's open-world playground, the swings at The Ice Pavilion, Optimist's Outlook's playground, and the Community Space's basketball court and kids' activity tables.
- **Room to change without moving anyone:** Twin Oracle Point (50×40) and Comfy Cubby (20×15) are homes with nobody planned, though both are built. Rippling Flats is the only unbuilt lot.
