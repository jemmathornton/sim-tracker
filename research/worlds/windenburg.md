# Windenburg: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Windenburg page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **27 lots, the most of any world:** 14 homes and 13 community lots (3 of them special lots) in four neighbourhoods; 26 residents.
- **Olde Platz** (the cobbled old town): Factory One (Partihaus), Waterlock Redoubt (the Behrs), Havisham House and Proprietor's Square (uninhabited); Quad Manor (Library), Old Quarter Inn (Bar, 20×20), The Narwhal Arms (Nightclub), South Square Coffee (Café).
- **Lykke Center** (the modern shore), all community: Bathe de Rill (Pool, 40×30; the wiki says it's the game's only premade public pool), Hare Square (Park), Hare and Hedgehog (Café), Harbor Quarter Gym (Gym, 30×30), Discotheque Pan Europa (Nightclub).
- **Windslar** (countryside): Coorinberg Cottage (the Munches), Cottage Am See (Free Spirits), Dresden House (the Fyres, 64×64), Rustic Residence (uninhabited), The Summer Home (empty, 40×30), The Shrieking Llama (Bar, 20×20).
- **The Crumbling Isle:** Von-Windenburg Estate (the Villareals, 64×64), The Lighthouse (the Bjergsens), Dock Den (the Bro household), Pier Palace (uninhabited), Mid-Nowhere (empty, 20×20).
- **Special lots:** Von Haunt Estate (Chalet Gardens: a hedge-maze garden, ghosts included), Ancient Ruins (walking paths, secret parties), The Bluffs (Island Bluff: a steamy natural pool over the lake).
- **Open world:** the Olde Platz ruins around the old castle. Snowy winters.
- Only the Ancient Ruins has a description in the game's files.

## Her plan against that

- **Lykke Center becomes the University of Windenburg** (all built, none shared yet):
  - Hare Square and Hare and Hedgehog → UoW Hare Halls and UoW Hedgehog Halls (University Housing, empty).
  - Harbor Quarter Gym → UoW Students Union (a Library by type, really a student union).
  - Discotheque Pan Europa → UoW Sports Center (Gym/Pool); Bathe de Rill → La Discotheque (Nightclub).
- **Olde Platz:** Waterlock Redoubt (the Behrs' home) → Olde Platz Inn, a Restaurant with four guest rooms. Old Quarter Inn: Bar → Museum of the Arts. The Narwhal Arms: Nightclub → Bar (a pub). South Square Coffee → Pixel Café (with an arcade). Quad Manor stays a Library (not built).
- **Windslar:** The Summer Home → Vet Clinic (not built). The Shrieking Llama stays a pub. Rustic Residence becomes a tiny home.
- **The Crumbling Isle:** all four homes go to the Tiquelles (four related households); only Von-Windenburg Castle is built. Mid-NoWhere is Generic (her upload: woods with a portal to the Magic Realm).
- **Families:** the Behrs and the Bro household move to San Myshuno (702 and 701 ZenView), the Bjergsens and the Villareals to Willow Creek. The Munches, Free Spirits, Fyres (not built) and Partihaus stay. Havisham House goes to the Specters.

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it).
- Windenburg is the only world that allows Ancient Ruins, Chalet Gardens and Island Bluff (its special lots).

## Notes for later

- **Quad Manor (open, TODO §5):** Backrooms are now 4, so it would be the 5th, inside the 3–6 range (the TODO's "6th" was counted before Movers & Shakers became a Recreation Center). Either way the Students Union is still a Library by type. It's unbuilt, so both options are easy.
- **No park lot** since Hare Square became halls. The Ancient Ruins, The Bluffs, Von Haunt's garden and the old-town ruins do much of a park's job.
- **Village check:** two pubs, a café, an inn with a restaurant, a museum, and a library and vet (both planned). **No cemetery and no shops.** The unbuilt lots are Quad Manor, The Summer Home, Dresden House and three Tiquelle homes on the isle.
- **Two lots have two types in the plan:** Olde Platz Inn (Restaurant/Vacation Rental) and UoW Sports Center (Gym/Pool). A lot takes one type in game, so the inn's rooms can't be let while it's a restaurant.
- **Spelling:** the game writes "Von Haunt Estate" and "Mid-Nowhere"; the plan has "Von-Haunt Estate" and "Mid-NoWhere".
