# Ravenwood: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Ravenwood page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **13 lots plus the Netherworld Department** (reached through the Reaper career).
- **Crow's Crossing** (the haunted village founded by Moppy the Goat):
  - Specter Family Manor: the Specters, 30×20, above the Specter cemetery.
  - Crow's Perch: empty home, 30×30, at the highest point of the village.
  - Moppy Manor: empty home, 30×20, built for Moppy, beside abandoned houses.
  - Old Ravenwood Estate: empty home, 50×50, with flickering lights and an abandoned greenhouse; once a Spellcaster's summer home.
  - Eternal Hollow: Cemetery, 64×64, the game's first; its pond has Death Frogs and Bonefish.
- **Whispering Glen** (quiet countryside; the nightly Moon Revelry):
  - Teardrop Cottage: the Gomes family, 30×30.
  - The Marigold Chateau: the Aethers, 50×50.
  - Hay Hill Landing: empty lot, 64×64.
  - The Final Draught: Bar, 30×30 (dim lights, gentle tunes; the upstairs hosts wakes).
- **Mourningvale** (the ghost town; Grim wanders through):
  - The Cozy Casket House: empty home, 30×20, once haunted by Catie Casket.
  - Mourning Mist Manor: the Izadi-Gomes household, 20×20.
  - Club Eternity: Nightclub, 30×30.
  - Mystic Crossroads: empty lot, 30×30.
- **Nobody is without a home.**
- **Open world:** the haunted playground, crypts, the Well of Longing and Regret, the Baleful Bog, Tarot Tome Shrines, collector signs, Whispering Glen's ruins, and festivals in every neighbourhood.
- **Netherworld Department:** 20×20 in the game files; the plan keeps her confirmed 30×30.

## Her plan against that

- **Specter Family Manor → Ravenwood Wedding Hall** (built, not shared).
- **The Specters move to Moppy Manor,** renamed Specter Row: their home, with tenants. Rental or Haunted House, undecided; units not decided. Not built.
- **Crow's Perch → Crow's Chronicles,** Library (built, shared).
- **Old Ravenwood Estate and The Cozy Casket House → Haunted House Residential,** both empty (15 Oct).
- **Hay Hill Landing:** vineyard or farm, empty (not built).
- **The Final Draught:** Bar → Restaurant (not built).
- **Mystic Crossroads:** to be decided, "something commercial".
- **Unchanged, not built:** Eternal Hollow, Club Eternity, and the Gomes, Aether and Izadi-Gomes homes.

## Allowed lot types

- Every everyday type is allowed, Café, Bar, Retail, Small Business Venue and Haunted House Residential included. Beach isn't.
- "Vineyard/Farm" isn't a game lot type; in the game a farm or vineyard is Residential.

## Notes for later

- **Missing everyday places:** café, bar or pub, park, gym, shops. Club Eternity covers nights out. The Final Draught was the only bar and is planned as a restaurant.
- **Mystic Crossroads (30×30, Mourningvale):** the game writes it as a starter plot for "an eerie setting and a charming afterlife aesthetic". Ideas so far (TODO §5): an occult curiosities shop, a gothic tea room or café, a pub. A Café or Bar runs by itself; Retail and small businesses only open when she plays the owners.
- **The Final Draught's upstairs is for wakes in the game.** research/lot-types-reference.md lists bars among the places a funeral can be held, but not restaurants. Worth checking in game if wakes there matter.
- **Haunted houses:** 2 in Ravenwood now. Specter Row as a third would make 5 across her save. A lot can't be a rental and a haunted house at once. Moppy Manor's own text has glowing statue eyes and flickering lights next door. The On Hallowed Grounds lot trait (ghostly goings-on on any lot) could keep a rental spooky; check it works on a rental lot.
