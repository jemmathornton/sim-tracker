# Britechester: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Britechester page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **13 lots:** 2 student commons, 6 University Housing lots, a library, a bar and 3 homes.
- **University of Britechester** (the old arts university):
  - Darby's Den: UBrite Commons, 30×20 (afternoon tea, poetry readings, debates).
  - Darkwing House: University Housing, 30×20, said to be the old home of Felix Psyded, the university's founder.
  - Drake Hall and Wyvern Hall: dorms, 30×20 (the Best of Friends in Wyvern Hall).
- **Foxbury Institute** (the modern tech campus):
  - Larry's Lagoon: Foxbury Commons, 40×30 (food, study space, computers, robotics).
  - Maritime Manor: University Housing, 30×20, homely student houses.
  - Tidal Tower and Briny Tower: dorms, 30×20 (Cameron Fletcher in Briny Tower).
- **Gibbs Hill** (the old town between the two):
  - Honeydew Fields: uninhabited, 20×15, budget lodging by the pub and library.
  - Laurel Library: Library, 50×40.
  - Pepper's Pub: Bar, 20×20, cheap drinks and a home for student societies.
  - Mossy Lane (the Pleasant twins) and Spring Steppes (the Elderberrys), both 20×15.
- **Homeless:** four Sims who may be professors. Felix Psyded is the world's one dead Sim (of laughter).
- **Open world:** the two campuses, bicycles, swimmable water, and "campus ghosts" and a Secret Society. Events: four at the University of Britechester, three at Foxbury, and the Sprite Celebration Ritual in Gibbs Hill. Often cloudy, some snow in winter.

## Her plan against that

- **Darkwing House → The Old Clocktower,** a Nightclub (a student shop by day in her upload).
- **Maritime Manor → Foxbury Court,** a Nightclub (the university shop by day).
- **Honeydew Fields → Britechester B&B,** Café and Vacation Rental, both versions kept. Her upload has a café and a gym downstairs and three bedrooms upstairs.
- **Pepper's Pub → The Royal Crown,** a classic pub.
- **Mossy Lane → University Housing,** empty (14 Oct). The Pleasants are tenants at Spring Steppes, a 3-unit rental the Elderberrys own.
- **Darby's Den** is "UoB Commons", her name for the game's UBrite Commons.
- **Unchanged:** Drake Hall and Tidal Tower (empty), Wyvern Hall (Best of Friends), Briny Tower (Fletcher), Larry's Lagoon and Laurel Library.
- **All 13 lots are built and shared.** University Housing is now 5 lots, 3 of them empty.

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it).
- Both commons types are allowed in every world except the holiday ones, not just here.
