# San Sequoia: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's San Sequoia page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **12 lots, 4 per neighbourhood.** Only 2 community lots.
- **Anchorpoint Wharf** (the old fishing and canning centre; a cinema and parklets):
  - Robles Point: the Robles family, 50×40.
  - Manzanita Terrace: empty lot, 30×20, across from the library.
  - Anchorpoint Library: Library, 30×20, with a children's story corner.
  - Anchorpoint Abode: holiday rental, 30×20 (hot tub, shark-watching).
- **Gilbert Gardens** (the big park; eco footprint starts green):
  - 13 Acacia Avenue: the Robles-Ruanos, 40×30.
  - Parkside Place: empty starter home, 30×20.
  - 18 Celebration Way: empty lot, 40×20, between the lake and the bay.
  - Celebration Center: Recreation Center, 64×64 (gym space, art corner, music room, an elders' corner). The game's first Rec Center.
- **Hopewell Hills** (the quiet suburb; eco footprint starts green):
  - 7 Eucalyptus Lane: the Michaelsons, 50×40.
  - 23 Eucalyptus Lane: empty lot, 30×20.
  - 36 Bayani Place: the Luna-Sullivans, 30×20.
  - Sequoia Cottage: empty starter home, 30×20.
- **Without a home:** Kyle Kyleson.
- **Open world:** Gilbert Gardens (Whalebert's splash pad, a pond, walking paths round Celebration Lake, a gazebo), a small park with a pirate ship playset in Hopewell Hills, the wharf cinema, a food cart. It never snows.

## Her plan against that

- **Anchorpoint Library → Anchorpoint Rec Center** (built, shared; 621 downloads). Her upload text still describes a library: books, computers, a children's story corner.
- **Anchorpoint Abode → Anchorpoint Works,** Restaurant/Bar (built, shared): family restaurant downstairs, adults' party space upstairs.
- **Manzanita Terrace:** empty lot → Residential Rental, 3 units, owned by the Robles family (built, shared).
- **Parkside Place → Parkside Playground** (built, shared): water play, a treehouse, sand and a BBQ.
- **18 Celebration Way → Community Market** (15 Oct; was a Thrift and Boba Store). Not built.
- **Celebration Center:** Recreation Center → Wedding Venue/Cemetery (not built).
- **23 Eucalyptus Lane:** empty lot → the Lovejoys' home (not a game family; built, shared).
- **Sequoia Cottage → Residential Rental,** 2 units, owned by the Lovejoys (not built).
- **Unchanged, not built:** Robles Point, 13 Acacia Avenue, 7 Eucalyptus Lane, 36 Bayani Place.

## Allowed lot types

- Every everyday type is allowed. Beach isn't.

## Notes for later

- **The library and the Rec Center swapped jobs.** Anchorpoint is the Rec Center now, and Celebration Center, which the game built as a Rec Center, is planned as a wedding venue or cemetery. There's no Library lot, though her Rec Center build is full of books.
- **Celebration Center has two types in the plan.** A lot can only have one, and it's not on the TODO list yet. It's the world's biggest lot (64×64). A Senior Living custom venue there was an idea not taken (15 Oct).
- **No café now.** 18 Celebration Way was the world's only Thrift and Boba Store before it became the market. The market's shared kitchen and the food cart help.
- **Park and gym:** no lot of either type, but Gilbert Gardens is a big open-world park with Parkside Playground in the same neighbourhood, and the Rec Center has a gym space.
- **For "the perfect place to raise a family":** Rec Center, playground, park, splash pad and market are all there.
- **No holiday rental** (Anchorpoint Abode was the game's one). A family suburb doesn't need one.
- **Kyle Kyleson** has no home in the game or the plan.
- **Anchorpoint Works** is set as a Vacation Rental in her Building save (named On Point there). The plan-vs-game check read that as how it was set while building; worth a look in game.
