# Sulani: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Sulani page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **14 lots on three islands: 11 homes and 3 community lots.** Eight of them are on the water.
- **Mua Pel'am** (the wild island: volcano, waterfall, cave):
  - Admiral's Wreckage: uninhabited, 50×50, a landlocked old trawler.
  - Caldera Camp: uninhabited, 30×20, a shack at the foot of the volcano.
  - Key Point: empty, 40×30, a deserted islet, "the perfect untouched beach".
- **Ohan'ali Town** (the busy old fishing village):
  - Kin-Ship: the Kealohas, 30×30, an old boat dock made into a home.
  - Chieftain's Villa: the Hoapilis, 40×30, the chief's home and the town's "spiritual and civil" centre.
  - Lagoon Look: an uninhabited beach house, 30×20.
  - Tangled Flat: an uninhabited hut, 20×20.
  - The Sand Bar: Bar, 30×20, open-air, the "informal town center".
  - Ohan'ali Beach: Beach, 30×30.
- **Lani St. Taz** (the upscale island): Reef Finery (the Ngatas, 40×30), Journey's End (the Kahananuis, 40×30), Pier Perfection (uninhabited, 30×20), Sand Simoleon Beach (Beach, 40×30), Sapphire Shores (empty, 50×50).
- **Homeless locals:** Kalamainu'u Iona, Nalani Mahi'ai, Paka'a Uha and Ukupanipo Hekekia.
- **Open world:** the volcano, swimming, snorkelling and dolphins, and island culture. Ohan'ali Town has seven events (Island Celebration, Fishing Competition, Beach Bonfire, Town BBQ, Town Potluck, Flowers and Music, Family Fun Day); Mua Pel'am has Turtle Hatching. Monsoons in summer and autumn, no snow.

## Her plan against that

- **Admiral's Wreckage → Community Space,** all four versions built and shared.
- **Tangled Flat → Ohan'ali Community Center,** a Library with painting, woodworking and things for kids (built, shared).
- **Lagoon Look → Lagoon of Love,** a Vacation Rental (built, shared; type settled 14 Oct). Her upload makes it a beach wedding spot.
- **Caldera Camp:** her own household ("Mine", not made yet; built, shared).
- **Key Point:** to be decided.
- **The Ngatas and Kahananuis aren't in her plan.** Their game homes, Reef Finery (not built) and Journey's End (built, shared), are empty, as are Pier Perfection (built, not shared) and Sapphire Shores (built, shared).
- **Unchanged:** the Kealohas at Kin-Ship (built, shared), the Hoapilis at Chieftain's Villa (not built), The Sand Bar and both beaches (built, shared).

## Allowed lot types

- **Beach is allowed here** (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it).
- Residential Rental isn't allowed on Sulani's waterfront lots (research/lot-types-reference.md).
- Every other ordinary type is allowed.

## Notes for later

- **Island vibes; no restaurant needed** (settled). There's also no café, park, gym or shop as a lot. The beaches, The Sand Bar, the Community Space and the open world cover a lot of that.
- **Key Point stays undecided and minimal** (not a market, beach or national park). Facts for when she decides:
  - The game calls it a deserted islet and "the perfect untouched beach".
  - Some sources say it has the Off-the-Grid trait, which only works on homes, rentals, small businesses and Generic lots.
  - A Custom Venue (watersports or a beach camp) is still listed in TODO §8; a Custom Venue needs only a sink, a toilet and a bin.
- **Four empty homes in Lani St. Taz,** two of them the game's homes for the Ngatas and Kahananuis, who have no home in the plan. Changing any of them moves nobody; only Reef Finery isn't built.
- **Ohan'ali Town is the social island** (seven events, the bar, the beach, the community centre); Lani St. Taz is quiet and, in her plan, almost all empty homes.
