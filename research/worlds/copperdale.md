# Copperdale: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Copperdale page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **12 lots: 7 homes and 5 community lots.**
- **Rockridge Heights:** Copperdale High School (High School, 64×64) and the Auditorium (Auditorium, 30×20: career day, prom, graduation).
- **Prescott Square** (the town centre, with the founder's statue):
  - Water Tower Way: the Harjos, 20×15.
  - Town Square Terrace: empty home, 20×15.
  - ThrifTea: Thrift and Boba Store, 20×15, run by Eloise Hiddlestix.
  - Lakeview Library: Library, 20×20 (once the city hall).
  - Golden Peak: the Prescotts, 30×30.
- **Plumbite Cove** (on Lake LaSuli; the old mines became the Plumbite Pier fairground):
  - LaSuli Point: empty starter home, 20×15, "a stone's throw away from the Pier".
  - Totter Park: Park, 50×40, known for Teeter Rock.
  - BridgeCreek Drive: the Prices, 30×30, near a teen hideaway.
  - Little Falls Nook: empty waterfront lot, 20×20.
  - Hillside Haven: holiday rental, 30×20, on the cliff.
- **Families:** the Harjos (they met over food; Ava cooks at the school), the Prescotts (Mei is the principal) and the Prices. The wiki names nobody without a home.
- **Open world:** the pier fairground, Prescott Square, the water tower, the lake, and school events (sports days, science fairs).

## Her plan against that

- **Prescott Square reshuffled (all four built):**
  - Water Tower Way → Copperdale Library (shared).
  - Town Square Terrace → Town Square Stores, Thrift and Boba Store (shared), with team space upstairs and club space in the basement.
  - ThrifTea → Prescott Boarding House, University Housing for the chess and computer teens (not shared).
  - Lakeview Library → Lakeview Boarding House, University Housing for the cheer and football teens (not shared).
- **Families moved:**
  - The Harjos: Water Tower Way → Little Falls Nook (not built).
  - The Prices: BridgeCreek Drive → Totter Park, now Residential (not built). Totter Park was planned as a cemetery before that.
- **Hillside Haven:** holiday rental → home for the Donovans (her note says "move!"). I couldn't find who they are.
- **BridgeCreek Drive → Backroom** (gym by day; not built).
- **LaSuli Point → Park or Cemetery,** undecided.
- **As the game has them, not built:** the High School, the Auditorium, Golden Peak (Prescotts).

## Allowed lot types

- Every everyday type is allowed. Beach isn't.
- The region tuning doesn't list Auditorium for any world, Copperdale included, though Copperdale ships one.

## Notes for later

- **No Park lot now.** Totter Park, the game's only park, is the Prices' home, so LaSuli Point is the only way back to one. The game's text doesn't say whether Teeter Rock is on the lot or beside it.
- **LaSuli Point's spot:** on the lake, a stone's throw from the Pier fairground. A busy spot for a cemetery, an easy one for a park. Her view: a teen area, so not a playground.
- **No Cemetery lot** unless LaSuli Point becomes one.
- **No restaurant** since 15 Oct (LaSuli Point was one). Copperdale is still on the restaurant list (TODO §5). No unbuilt lot is free apart from LaSuli Point. If eating out comes up, the Harjos' story is about food.
- **No holiday rental** now (Hillside Haven was the game's one). A family town doesn't need one.
- **Teens are well served:** the High School, two boarding houses, Town Square Stores and the library. Nights out are the Backroom's job.
- **Build note:** BridgeCreek Drive needs gym kit for its day side, plus a bar, stereo, 4 barstools, sink, toilet and bin (TODO §6b).
- **Open TODO items:** LaSuli Point, park or cemetery (§5). The Donovans still to move.
