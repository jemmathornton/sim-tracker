# Willow Creek: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Willow Creek page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **21 lots (16 homes, 5 community lots) in six neighbourhoods,** plus the secret Sylvan Glade (15×10), reached through a tree in Foundry Cove.
- **Foundry Cove** (modest homes, a canal and a railway): Garden Essence (the BFF household, 40×30); Streamlet Single, Crick Cabana and Daisy Hovel (uninhabited); Bargain Bend (empty, 30×20).
- **Courtyard Lane:** Pique Hearth (the Pancakes); Brook Bungalow, Riverside Roost and Rindle Rose (uninhabited); Potters Splay (empty).
- **Pendula View** (historic gardens, mansions): Ophelia Villa (the Goths); Parkshore and Umbrage Manor (uninhabited, 40×30); Hallow Slough (empty).
- **Sage Estates:** Cypress Terrace (the Spencer-Kim-Lewises); Oakenstead (uninhabited, 50×50).
- **Crawdad Quarter** ("the heart of Willow Creek, from parks to nightlife"): The Blue Velvet (Nightclub), Movers & Shakers (Gym), Municipal Muses (Museum, 40×30), Willow Creek Archive (Library).
- **Magnolia Blossom Park:** Park, 50×50, the only area with a green eco footprint.
- **Open world:** the ferryboat on the canal, Pendula View's willows and gardens, and Bob Pancakes wandering Courtyard Lane.
- **Get to Work:** the wiki says it adds a hidden Willow Creek area with the Police Station and Willow Creek Hospital (her plan has them in Magnolia Promenade's Secret Lots).
- The game's files describe only the homes; the community lots have no text.

## Her plan against that

- **Three homes become community lots:**
  - Bargain Bend → Willow's, a family diner (Restaurant, built, shared).
  - Pique Hearth → Courtyard Lane Playground (built, shared). The Pancakes move to Rindle Rose, still in Courtyard Lane, so the "Home to Bob Pancakes" highlight still fits.
  - Parkshore → Cordelia Church, a Cemetery (not built).
- **Crawdad Quarter loses its nightlife** (her choice: the quiet family town; nights out are in San Myshuno):
  - The Blue Velvet → Café Llama (Café, built, shared).
  - Movers & Shakers → Recreation Center (not built; it has a gym space).
  - Municipal Muses stays a Museum; her upload says it's a wedding venue too. Willow Creek Archive stays a Library (not built).
- **Ophelia Villa:** the Goths stay; Haunted House Residential since 15 Oct (not built).
- **Game families:** the Spencer-Kim-Lewises stay (not built). The BFF household isn't in Willow Creek; a household called BFF rents Fern Park Flats in Newcrest (probably them, not confirmed).
- **From Windenburg:** the Bjergsens (Brook Bungalow) and the Villareals (Umbrage Manor), both not built.
- **Other households:** two Davidson households (Streamlet Single, Garden Essence), Broke, Winters, Reynolds, Kopf, Barraza (Oakenstead) and Ella-Mint Cadberry (Hallow Slough, built, not shared).

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it).
- Sylvan Glade, Police Station and the hospital's types aren't on the list for ordinary lots: they're fixed on their own hidden lots.

## Notes for later

- **Nothing is spare.** Every lot has a family or a job. The unbuilt ones are four family homes (Brook Bungalow, Umbrage Manor, Cypress Terrace, Ophelia Villa), Cordelia Church, Movers & Shakers and Willow Creek Archive.
- **Still without:** a bar or pub (on purpose), a pool, a vet and shops.
- **Evenings close by:** the wiki places Magnolia Promenade beside downtown Willow Creek. Its bowling alley, cinema and Paddy's Place are nearer than San Myshuno.
- **Parkshore's game text is a home** ("verandas and balconies galore"). A church and cemetery suits Pendula View's historic gardens; it's unbuilt with no residents, so it stays easy to change.
- **Spelling:** her plan writes "Villareel"; the game's family is the Villareals.
