# San Myshuno: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's San Myshuno page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **18 lots:** 9 apartment buildings (21 flats), 2 penthouse homes, 1 house and 6 community lots, in four districts plus Myshuno Meadows.
- **Spice Market** (family district; Spice Festival, Flea Market): Culpepper House (the Rasoyas in 20), Jasmine Suites (the Bheedas in 2A), The Old Salt House (an empty house, 30×30), Waterside Warble (Karaoke Bar).
- **Arts Quarter** (bohemian; Humor and Hijinks Festival): Medina Studios (Salim Benali in 910), Hakim House (the Jangs in 121), FountainView Penthouse, Casbah Gallery (Arts Center, 40×30).
- **Fashion District** (young and trendy; Romance Festival, GeekCon): ZenView (the Karaoke Legends in 701), 21 Chic Street (Penny Pizzazz in 1312), Torendi Tower Penthouse (a home, 40×30), Planet Honey Pop! (Karaoke Bar, 30×20).
- **Uptown** (rich and powerful): Alto (Diego Lobo in 1020), Landgraab (the Fengs in IX), 888 Spire (empty), Skye Fitness (Gym), Stargazer Lounge (Lounge, a rooftop penthouse).
- **Myshuno Meadows:** Center Park, 64×64, around Barnabas Skye's old observatory; green eco footprint.
- **Open world:** festivals every day, night life, jogging and ball games in the park.

## Her plan against that

- **Community lots:**
  - The Old Salt House → Restaurant (built, shared; her upload has a small library and art gallery inside).
  - Torendi Tower Penthouse → Torendi Tunes, a Karaoke Bar (not built).
  - Planet Honey Pop! → Thrift and Boba Store (not built). So both karaoke bars stay; the second moves to the penthouse.
  - Stargazer Lounge → Stargazer, a Nightclub (built, shared).
  - Unchanged: Waterside Warble and Skye Fitness (built, shared); Casbah Gallery and Myshuno Meadows (not built).
- **Flats:**
  - Game families who stay put: Rasoya, Benali, Jang, Pizzazz, Lobo, Feng (all not built).
  - The Bheedas move to 920 Medina Studios; 2A and 2B Jasmine Suites become the Fraternity and Sorority Apartments (empty).
  - 701 ZenView: "Bro & Kennedy"; 702: the Behrs (both from Windenburg). The Karaoke Legends aren't in her plan.
  - Her own: Jeong (1310), Livin' In The City (930), Santiago Ojo (FountainView Penthouse, built, not shared).
  - Ten flats have no household named.

## Allowed lot types

- Every ordinary type except Beach (only Sulani and Tartosa allow it).
- Apartment and Penthouse are fixed types, so flats can't become community lots. A penthouse can be one, though: the game's own Stargazer Lounge is.

## Notes for later

- **No library, and Newcrest (its suburb) has none either.** Every community lot is spoken for; the unbuilt ones are Casbah Gallery, Torendi Tunes, Planet Honey Pop! and Myshuno Meadows. A Backroom (library by day, club by night) would add one without losing a night spot, but Backrooms are meant to stay rare.
- **Eating out is thin for a city:** one restaurant and one café-type place (Planet Honey Pop!, not built). The festivals' food stalls fill some of the gap.
- **Culture:** Casbah Gallery isn't built yet, and there's no museum.
- **Game text that no longer fits:** Planet Honey Pop!'s describes a karaoke bar "in perfect harmony with the songs of the metro train below"; The Old Salt House's describes a family home. Both changes are settled.
- **Open items:** Bubble World (a Thrift and Boba Store build) sits on The Old Salt House lot, unlinked until the bonus builds are decided (TODO §0, §5). The map boxes need a recheck (TODO §4).
- Her save has The Old Salt House as a Thrift and Boba Store (probably Bubble World) and Stargazer as a Lounge; the plan and Gallery say Restaurant and Nightclub.
