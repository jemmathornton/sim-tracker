# Mt. Komorebi: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (16 Oct)

For future lot decisions. Sources: the Sims Wiki's Mt. Komorebi page, the game's own lot descriptions, her plan and Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (which lot types the world allows).

## As the game ships it

- **14 lots plus 3 hidden ones.**
- **Wakaba** (the new town, with Yamachan the mascot):
  - 2-4-1 Wakabamori: empty lot, 20×15, for "any home or business" by the town centre.
  - 2-4-2 Wakabamori: holiday rental, 30×20.
  - 2-4-3 Wakabamori: empty starter home, 20×15.
  - 2-5-1 Wakabamori: the Itos, 50×50.
  - Hazakura Lounge: Lounge, 30×20 (drinks, locals, live music).
- **Senbamachi** (the oldest town, in the bamboo forest):
  - 5-3-1 Shinrinyoku: the Akiyamas, 30×20.
  - 5-6-1 Shinrinyoku: the Nishidakes, 20×15.
  - 6-4-1 Hanamigawa: empty starter home, 20×15.
  - Hanamigawa Koen: Park, 30×20.
  - Izakaya Ippai: Bar, 30×20.
- **Yukimatsu** (the ski slopes; always snowy):
  - 5-1-1 and 5-1-2 Kiyomatsu: holiday rentals, 30×20.
  - Sutefani Onsen Bathhouse: Onsen, 30×20. The mountain climb starts here.
  - Kiyomatsu Point: empty lot, 50×50.
  - Hidden: Base Camp and The Croft Icefall (20×15, stages of the climb) and Mt. Komorebi Peak (20×20, Mount Komorebi Summit).
- **Families:** the Itos, Akiyamas and Nishidakes. Nobody is without a home.
- **Open world:** skiing, snowboarding, sledding and rock climbing; the climbing excursion; the festivals of Youth, Light and Snow.

## Her plan against that

- **The Akiyamas move** from 5-3-1 Shinrinyoku to 2-4-2 Wakabamori (the game's rental; built, shared as a family home).
- **5-3-1 Shinrinyoku → Sento Senbamachi**, a second Onsen Bathhouse (built, shared; also works as a spa).
- **Hanamigawa Koen → Izakaya Hanamigawa Koen**, Restaurant with the park behind (built, shared; works as a bar too; 613 downloads).
- **Izakaya Ippai → Boba Ippai**, Thrift and Boba Store (built, not shared). So the izakaya moved to the park lot.
- **Hazakura Lounge → Recreation Center** (her choice: Wakaba is the residential side).
- **2-4-1 Wakabamori → Cemetery** (her choice: suits Japan).
- **6-4-1 Hanamigawa:** empty home → Vacation Rental. Still 3 holiday rentals, just not the same three.
- **Kiyomatsu Point:** Custom Venue (not built), with a bar and restaurant area planned.
- **2-4-3 Wakabamori** stays an empty home (built, shared).
- **As the game has them, not built:** the Itos, the Nishidakes, Sutefani Onsen.
- **Hidden lots:** the Peak is Mount Komorebi Summit; Base Camp and The Croft Icefall have no type yet.

## Allowed lot types

- Every everyday type is allowed (Café, Gym, Library, Pool, Spa, Karaoke Bar, Community Market, Playground). Beach isn't.
- The only world that allows Mount Komorebi Summit.
- research/lot-types-reference.md notes Sutefani's Onsen type is locked, since the climb starts there.

## Notes for later

- **No lot is typed Bar or Lounge now.** Her reasoning (15 Oct): the izakaya works as a bar and Kiyomatsu Point will have a bar area. Until Kiyomatsu Point is built, the izakaya is the only place for a drink.
- **No library.** Every lot has a type or residents; the only home with nobody in it is 2-4-3 Wakabamori (20×15, built, shared).
- **No gym lot,** but Hazakura as a Rec Center has a gym space, and the slopes are the world's exercise.
- **Two onsens** (Sento Senbamachi, Sutefani) suit the blurb's "healing hot springs".
- **Game texts that differ from the plan (settled, noted only):** 2-4-1 Wakabamori is written as a town-centre plot, now a cemetery; 2-4-2 Wakabamori as a rental for travellers, now the Akiyamas' home.
- **Open TODO items:** Base Camp and The Croft Icefall need their lot type checked in game (one Gallery copy suggests Mount Komorebi Summit). Edit them with `bb.enablefreebuild`; keep the tent, trail marker, bonfire and wood pile; saving mid-build may end the climb. Izakaya Hanamigawa Koen is ticked for screenshots but has no Builds folder.
