# Everyday places by area (overnight 18 Oct)

Research only: nothing in `site/`, `data/` or `tools/` was changed. Suggestions only; every choice is hers.

**What this is:** the community-lot gap check from `community-lots-mix.md`, redone for her 12 areas (`world-areas.md`) instead of world by world, so a gap in one world can be covered by its neighbours.

**Sources:** lot types from her plan (`data/worlds/*.json`, 18 Oct), Sims from each world’s `households` (only households filled in so far: 118, out of about 160 homes with a family planned, so the numbers will grow), open-world notes from `research/worlds/` and her settled choices in `TODO.md`.

**How lots are counted:** Thrift and Boba Stores, the bakery and coffee-selling businesses count as cafés (her view, 15 Oct). Recreation Centers count as having a gym space. A Backroom counts as nightlife and as its daytime side (library, museum or gym). “To build” means planned but not built. Schools aren’t lots except Copperdale High, which every world’s teens use; primary school is off-screen everywhere, so schools are never a gap.

**In short:**
- **The biggest real gaps are libraries for children:** San Myshuno’s area has none, Willow Creek’s is still unbuilt (15 children, the most of any area), Mt. Komorebi and Evergreen Harbor have none and no close neighbour.
- **Food is mostly fine** once areas share, except the spooky trio (nothing at all), Gibbi Point and Tomarang (no restaurant between them) and the big island and desert (no café).
- **Nightlife, gyms and parks are well covered by area.** Willow Creek’s lack of a bar is fine: San Sequoia has Anchorpoint Works, and San Myshuno is the night-out city.
- **Pets are only in areas 5 and 6 so far, and both have vets.** Chestnut Ridge’s 10 horses get Canter Clinic (to build).
- **Cemeteries and wedding venues** are missing in some areas (desert, area 6, area 9 apart from ZoSul Taman Botani), but graves can be kept at home and weddings travel. Not counted as gaps.

## 1a. San Myshuno, Newcrest, Magnolia Promenade

**Families:** 15 households (11 in San Myshuno, 4 in Newcrest): mostly young adults, 1 child, 1 teen, 3 elders, no pets. Newcrest’s Rippling Flats (to build) is for her current uni Sims.

**Has:**
- **Eating:** The Old Salt House, The Aqua Grill, Paddy’s Place; Cookies ’n’ Cream (bakery and ice cream); Planet Honey Pop! (to build).
- **Nights:** Waterside Warble and Torendi Tunes (to build, karaoke), Stargazer (nightclub), Magnolia Bowling Alley (bar), Llama Picturehouse.
- **Gym:** Skye Fitness.
- **Parks:** The Ice Pavilion; Myshuno Meadows (to build); Newcrest’s open-world playground and Optimist’s Outlook’s playground.
- **Shops:** Optimist’s Outlook, Llama Picturehouse, Magnolia Mall (to build).
- **Culture:** Newcrest Arts Center; Casbah Gallery (Arts Center, to build).
- **Also:** Newcrest Community Space, Newcrest Wedding Hall.

**Missing:**
- **Library: none in the area.** The nearest is Willow Creek Archive in the other city (not built). Only 1 child and 1 teen today, but San Myshuno has 22 homes and only 11 are filled.
- **Vet:** none, but no pets here yet; San Sequoia’s 36 Bayani Place (to build) is the other city.
- **Cemetery, pool, spa:** none. Fine for a city.

**Candidate lots for a library (options):**
- **Casbah Gallery** (San Myshuno, Arts Quarter, 40×30, to build): a library or a library-by-day Backroom suits an arts quarter. Arts Centers would drop from 2 to 1, but the other (Newcrest Arts Center) is in the same area.
- **Magnolia Mall** (40×30, to build): less natural; it’s planned for the Landgraabs and is the Paragons and Renegades club’s meeting place.
- **Comfy Cubby** (Newcrest, 20×15, empty home, built) or **Twin Oracle Point** (Newcrest, 50×40, empty home, built): both would mean rebuilding a built lot.
- **Or none:** let Willow Creek Archive serve both cities.

**Has:**

- **Daycare:** 3 babies and toddlers, and the only daycare in the save is Nordhaven’s Canalside Daycare. A bonus-story idea only, not a gap.
