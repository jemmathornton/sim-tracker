# Playtest checklist by lot type (6 Nov)

**What each lot type needs, straight from the game's own files** (each venue's required-object list, read 6 Nov on patch 1.129). The game shows the same list in Build mode under the lot's venue info. Optional items are marked as such.

The "watch for" lines come from your notes and earlier research. Report only.

## Bars, clubs and lounges
- **Bar.** Needs:
  - a bar, 4 barstools;
  - 2 dining tables, 4 dining chairs;
  - a stereo and a TV;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
  - **Watch for:** a bartender turning up, and patrons.
- **Karaoke Bar.** Needs:
  - a bar, 4 barstools;
  - 2 dining tables, 4 dining chairs;
  - a karaoke machine;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
  - It doesn't need a stereo or TV.
- **Nightclub.** Needs:
  - a bar, 3 barstools;
  - 3 dining tables, 6 dining chairs;
  - a stereo;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
  - **Watch for:** a bartender and a DJ if you've placed a DJ booth.
- **Lounge.** Needs:
  - a bar, 3 barstools;
  - 2 dining tables, 4 dining chairs, 2 love seats;
  - a stereo, a microphone and a musical instrument;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
- **Backroom.** Needs:
  - **3 of one daytime kind:** bookshelves, art, or gym equipment;
  - a bar, a stereo, 4 barstools;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
  - Optional: 2 desks, 2 desk chairs, 2 computers, an easel, a shower.
  - **Watch for:** daytime visitors matching what you placed, the switch to the club at 9 pm, and a bartender at night. Your notes for The Old Mill: make the 3 daytime objects ordinary gym kit, because a climbing wall may not count.

## Food and drink
- **Restaurant.** Needs:
  - a chef station, a waiter station, a host station;
  - **4 dining spots** (tables with chairs);
  - a sink and a toilet.
  - **Watch for:** host, chef and waiter all turning up, and food being served.
  - Note: a small business can't be run on a restaurant lot.
- **Café.** Needs:
  - an espresso bar, an espresso machine and grinder;
  - 3 tables, 6 dining chairs;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
- **Thrift and Boba Store.** Needs:
  - 4 thrift racks, a retail counter;
  - 2 dining tables, 4 dining chairs, a coffee table, a sofa;
  - a bubble tea counter, a microphone, a wall speaker;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
  - **Watch out:** EA's bubble tea vendor bug. No vendor turned up at Boba Ippai, or in your other test. Your mods aren't the cause.

## Culture, learning and fitness
- **Library.** Needs:
  - 3 bookshelves;
  - 3 desks, 5 desk chairs, 2 computers;
  - 2 armchairs and a chess table;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
- **Museum.** Needs:
  - **10 wall decorations, 5 sculptures**, an easel;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
- **Arts Center.** Needs:
  - a bar, 4 barstools;
  - an easel, a musical instrument, a woodworking table, an activity table, a mural;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
- **Gym.** Needs:
  - **4 pieces of workout equipment**;
  - a shower, a toilet and a sink.
  - StrangerVille Gym&Pool is set as a Pool (on your list).
- **Pool.** Needs:
  - a pool with a ladder;
  - 4 dining chairs;
  - a shower, a toilet and a sink.
- **Recreation Center.** Needs:
  - 3 games, 2 musical instruments, 2 pieces of workout equipment, a jungle gym;
  - a stereo;
  - a shower, a toilet and a sink.
- **Spa.** Needs:
  - **4 spa items**, a storage locker;
  - a shower, a toilet and a sink.
- **Onsen Bathhouse.** Needs:
  - a hot spring, a locker;
  - a couch, 2 armchairs;
  - a shower, a toilet, a sink and a bin.

## Outdoors
- **Park.** Needs:
  - playground equipment;
  - 2 benches, a chess table, a patio table, 4 patio chairs;
  - a toilet and a sink.
- **Playground.** Needs:
  - **4 pieces of playground equipment**;
  - 2 benches, a patio table;
  - a toilet and a sink.
- **National Park:** a grill, a picnic table and a bin.
- **Cemetery.** Needs:
  - headstones or urns, 2 benches;
  - a toilet and a bin.
  - Optional: 2 graves, a crypt, a casket.

## Community
- **Community Market.** Needs:
  - 2 stoves, a fridge, a podium;
  - a dining table, 4 dining chairs;
  - **3 sales tables**;
  - a sink, a toilet and a bin.
  - Optional: toys, a musical instrument, 2 picnic tables.
- **Community Space:** just a community board. Each voted version has its own list:
  - **Maker Space:** fabricator, recycler, plus the usual sink, toilet and bin.
  - **Community Garden:** 4 planters, plus the usual sink, toilet and bin.
  - **Marketplace:** 3 sales tables, plus the usual sink, toilet and bin.
- **Wedding Venue:** nothing is required. A wedding arch, a bar, chairs, plants, a stereo, a sink, a toilet and wall decorations are all optional.
  - So "Wedding Venue" won't complain if something's missing. Check the arch and the bar are there (your list: check each wedding venue's lot type when you share it).
- **Custom Venue:** only a sink, a toilet and a bin are required. The schedule and roles are what need testing: does each role fill when its slot starts?

## Homes and businesses
- **Small Business** (venue or residential): nothing required; a ticket kiosk and business sign are optional.
  - **Watch for:** when it opens. You've visited the game's NPC-run Koffieboon while it was open. Whether one you set up for an unplayed owner opens the same way is still to check.
- **Residential Rental:** nothing required. The game lists a toilet, fridge, oven, shower, bed, activities and decorations as optional.
  - **Watch for:** each unit having its own traits and its own bed and bath counts (on your list).
- **University Housing.** Needs **4 beds**, a toilet and a shower; a bin is optional.
- **Vet Clinic.** Needs:
  - an exam table, a vet podium and a bin.
  - Optional: a surgery station, a medicine crafting table, a medicine vending machine.
- **Auditorium:** a podium, a toilet, a sink and a bin. Each event version adds its own: careers day needs 3 career day tables; the formal dance needs a podium, speaker, photo booth, punch fountain and notice board.
- **High School:**
  - 2 toilets, 2 sinks, 2 whiteboards;
  - 8 desks, 8 lockers, 2 dining tables;
  - a cafeteria station, the principal's desk and a loudspeaker.
- **Haunted House, Residential, Tiny Home:** nothing required (a ticket kiosk and business sign are optional, for a home business).
