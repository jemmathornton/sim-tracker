# Neighbourhood banners: do any look worse than the whole map?

Checked overnight, 16–17 October 2026, for the TODO “Some may look worse than the whole map: name any to put back”. Pictures of every banner: `for-jemma/hood-banners/` (five JPGs). Nothing in the app was changed.

**Result: none looks clearly worse than the whole map.** 48 are good and 29 so-so. Showing the whole map instead would make each neighbourhood a small patch (about a twelfth of the banner, against about a quarter now), and for 16 of them it would sit under the dark fade at the bottom. Four barely zoom at all, so putting those back is purely her taste (see suggestion 2).

How it was checked: the banner maths from the site (`hoodHeroView`) was redone in Python for all 77 neighbourhoods with a box, at two window sizes: her laptop (1280×800 window, banner 985 wide) and a wide screen (1920×1000 window, banner 1625 wide). Every zoom and position came out exactly as the app works it out. Banner heights were measured in the app, as the text makes them taller or shorter. Each picture shows the fade, the text (redrawn roughly, as the site’s font isn’t on this PC) and her box, faintly.

## Seen across all of them

1. **The bottom of every box is under the fade.** The code works out the zoom for a banner 352 px tall on her laptop (a little under half the window’s height), but the real banners are 380 to 686 px tall (489 in the middle of the range), because the title, description and neighbourhood links make them taller. So each box fills only the middle of the banner, from about a sixth to about seven tenths of the way down, and roughly the lowest quarter of the box sits under the dark fade. The code’s own comment says the banner’s real shape is used, so this may count as a bug rather than a choice.
2. **The big title covers the left side.** A neighbourhood at the left edge of its map can’t slide out from under it (The Crumbling Isle, Llama Lagoon, Foxbury Institute, Prescott Square).
3. **Sharpness is fine on her laptop.** Every crop has at least one map pixel per screen pixel, except Myshuno Meadows (San Myshuno’s map is the smaller one, 1800 px). On a wide screen the ones at the 3× limit are stretched a little and look slightly soft. If her laptop uses Windows display scaling above 100%, all of them are stretched more than these pictures show.
4. **Window height barely matters.** If her app window is shorter than 800 px inside, the banners zoom about a tenth less. The verdicts stay the same.
5. **Wide screens only:** Campground, Glimmerbrook and Belomisia Jungle lose the top of their box, as the box is taller than the banner.

## Suggested changes, for her to decide

Nothing below has been applied. The banner has no setting of its own for each neighbourhood yet (`mapZoom` only changes the cards), so 3 and 4 would need a small new setting, like the world banners’ `heroY`.

1. **For all banners:** work out the zoom from the banner’s real height and keep the box above the fade (its middle about a third of the way down instead of nearly half). That one change would cure most of the laptop so-sos: Lykke Center / University of Windenburg, Hopewell Hills, Deadgrass Isle, The Crumbling Isle, Plumbite Cove, and partly Magnolia Blossom Park and Puerto Llamante Marketplace.
2. **Put back to the whole map (purely her taste):** Belomisia Jungle (1.01×), Campground (1.03×), Glimmerbrook (1.09×) and Conifer Station (1.15×). These hardly zoom, so the zoom only slides the map down and loses the sky. Glimmerbrook is the strongest case, as its whole map has the sunset.
3. **Zoom out a bit:** Acquisition Butte, Desert Bloom Park and Galloping Gulch (at the 3× limit, mostly bare rock), Myshuno Meadows (the softest), Yukimatsu (mostly snow).
4. **Shift (only if 1 isn’t done):** Lykke Center / University of Windenburg and Hopewell Hills higher in the banner; Magnolia Blossom Park and Puerto Llamante Marketplace to the right, out from under the title.
5. **Leave as they are:** all the others, including the mostly-water ones (Lani St. Taz, Cavalier Cove, Jellyfish Junction, Prescott Square) and San Myshuno, which is dark whether zoomed or not. The whole map would be no better for any of them.
