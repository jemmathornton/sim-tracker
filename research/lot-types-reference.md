# The Sims 4 – Lot Types (Lot Assignments / Venue Types) Reference, as of October 2026

Compiled 13 October 2026 from web sources (listed at the end). The Sims Wiki (sims.fandom.com) could only be reached intermittently, so several entries rely on simscommunity.info, snootysims.com, EA's own pages and EA Forums Q&A threads.

**Confidence labels**
- **[C]**: confirmed by at least two sources, or by an official EA source.
- **[S]**: one reliable source only.
- **[?]**: unconfirmed or conflicting. The note explains why.

**Context for 2026.** Royalty & Legacy (12 Feb 2026) appears to be the last traditional expansion. In March 2026 EA said future content would come as free updates plus Marketplace "Kits" and "Maker Packs". Simscommunity read that as meaning no more Expansion, Game or Stuff Packs. I checked the 2026 patch notes for 3 Feb, 17 Mar, 16 Apr, 30 Jun, 21 Jul, 6 Aug, 25 Aug and 22 Sep. None of them added a new lot type. The only lot-level addition after Royalty & Legacy was the event-reward lot trait *Regal Residence* (see the lot traits section). I found no 2025–2026 kit, stuff pack or Maker Pack that adds a lot type.

## 1. Placeable community lot types (venues)

These can be assigned to any normal lot in any non-destination world, unless a note says otherwise. Each type's current object requirements are shown in-game under Build Mode → Venue Info. Where I give requirements below, the source is named.

### How lot types combine

- **Small business layering (Businesses & Hobbies):** A business can run on (a) the household's home lot as a Residential Small Business, (b) a Small Business Venue lot, or (c) an existing community venue such as a bar, spa or gym, by adding a ticket kiosk. Classes can also be held on community lots. San Myshuno and Evergreen Harbor apartments count as residential and can host a business. Residential Rental units cannot.
- **Community Space sub-types (Eco Lifestyle):** Community Space, Community Garden, Marketplace and Maker Space share one lot. The Neighbourhood Action Plan vote picks the active one.
- **Custom Venue and Getaways (Adventure Awaits):** A Getaway is not a lot type. It is a multi-day scheduled event (Roles, Schedules, Locations, Activities, up to 5 Rules) held on a lot. The location filter covers most venue types plus other Sims' residential lots (you pay to use them) or your own home (guests pay you; earnings rise with more Sims and higher lot value). Premade Getaways in Gibbi Point: Camp Gibbi Gibbi, Revive & Thrive Retreat, Love Highland. There is **no “Getaway Venue” lot type** (checked in the game’s files, 17 Oct, research/getaways-check.md). **Getaways can’t be held on Vacation Rentals or Residential Rentals** (nor University Housing, Restaurants, Retail, Small Business Venues, Vet Clinics or Wedding Venues). Guests only pay a household you’re playing, at its own home.
- **Backroom and secret passageways (Royalty & Legacy):** The passageway object, disguised as a grandfather clock or bookcase, links lots for teleport travel or traps. It is also a WooHoo spot, and cats can forage through it. A Backroom's nightclub area can connect to other nightclubs or backrooms in the same world.
- **Haunted House Residential + On Hallowed Grounds:** Snootysims (L&D dev stream) says the two stack for extra scares.
- **Tattoo tables (Businesses & Hobbies)** auto-hire a tattoo artist on bars, nightclubs, museums, karaoke bars, spas, arts centres, and Marketplace and Maker Space community spaces.

## 2. Residential lot types

| In-game name | Pack | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Residential** | Base game | No required objects. Palaces and estates in Royalty & Legacy are ordinary Residential lots; Grand Balls are hosted from them. Royalty & Legacy also added new buildable lot sizes (29 Jan 2026 update). [C] |
| **Residential Rental** | For Rent (EP), Dec 2023 | A permanent home split into units. **2–6 units** by default (`bb.increaserentalunitcap` lifts the cap), up to **99 units per save**, up to 8 Sims per unit. Units don't need to connect. Each has a tenant agreement (rent, lease, rules). Not allowed on Sulani or Gibbi Point waterfront lots, vacation worlds or hidden lots; **penthouses can be rentals since Jul 2024** (corrected 17 Oct, research/game-changes-2026.md). **Maintenance Troubles:** web sources say it’s applied automatically, but the game’s trait tuning doesn’t let it be picked on rentals (research/lot-trait-ideas.md, 17 Oct); check in game. Cannot host a small business. [C] |
| **Vacation Rental** (formerly "Rental") | Outdoor Retreat, now apparently base game | Non-permanent holiday home. [?] Pack history unconfirmed. |
| **Tiny Home Residential** | Tiny Living (SP), Jan 2020 | Indoor tile limit 100. Tiers: **Small Home ≤100**, **Tiny Home ≤64**, **Micro Home ≤32** tiles. Smaller tiers unlock more perks (lower bills, relationship and skill bonuses). Reportedly not offered alongside some lot challenges. [C] |
| **Haunted House Residential** | **Paranormal Stuff (SP)**, Jan 2021, *not* Life & Death | Any residential lot can be set to this, even an occupied one. Nightly specters and a spiritual balance meter (cursed objects push it down; the séance table and gifts restore it). Guidry visits nightly. Temperance turns up on night 5. Successful nights earn reward points. Leads to the Paranormal Investigator career. [C] |
| **University Housing** (dorm) | Discover University (EP) | Assigned to one of the two universities, with optional gender and club/organisation restrictions. Roommates spawn according to bed count. You can't use Build mode while active Sims live there without `bb.enablefreebuild`. Placeable in any world. [C] |
| **Residential Small Business** | Businesses & Hobbies (EP), Mar 2025 | The household's home is also its business. Rooms are zoned Residential, Employee or Public. EA Help: "Residential zoning option is only available on Small Business Residential Lots." Can use a ticket kiosk for entry fees or time limits. [C] |
| **Apartment / Penthouse** | City Living (EP). Eco Lifestyle adds Evergreen Harbor apartments. | Not assignable from the lot type dropdown. These are fixed multi-unit "special" lots in San Myshuno (21 apartments, 3 penthouses) and Evergreen Harbor. Penthouses keep their own behaviour and cannot be made Residential Rentals. Residential Rentals are For Rent's buildable replacement. [C] |
| **Horse ranch** | Horse Ranch (EP), Jul 2023 | **Not a separate lot type** as far as I could find. Ranches are Residential lots with stables and paddocks, e.g. Biscuit's Bastion and Champion's Grove in Chestnut Ridge. Several Chestnut Ridge lots carry the **Wild Prairie Grass** lot challenge. [?] |
| **"Two Units" lots (Ondarion)** | Royalty & Legacy | Pre-built Residential Rental-style lots with two units, in Dambele and Bellacorde. [S] |

## 4. New in 2025–2026 packs (detail)

### Businesses & Hobbies (EP, 6 Mar 2025, Nordhaven)
- **New lot types:** Residential Small Business and Small Business Venue lot (details in sections 1 and 2).
- **Ticket kiosk:** Charges an entry fee or sets a time limit. Works on residential or venue business lots, and on existing community venues.
- **Room zoning:** Public, Employee or Residential.
- **Restrictions:** Not compatible with Residential Rental units or Restaurants. One open business per household at a time.
- **Nordhaven:** 12 lots across Gammelvik and Iverstad. Hobby Meetups are festivals held in the world, not lots.
- **Lot traits:** none found. [?]

### Enchanted by Nature (EP, 10 Jul 2025, Innisgreen)
- **New lot types:** none found.
- **New lot trait:** **Fairy Passage** (see section 5).

### Adventure Awaits (EP, 2 Oct 2025, Gibbi Point)
- **New lot type:** **Custom Venue** (details in section 1).
- **Getaways:** an event system that runs on venues, including Custom Venues, or on residential lots.
- **Modular playground objects:** included in the pack.
- **Playground venue:** came in the same-week **free patch (18 Sep 2025)**, not in the pack itself.
- **Gibbi Point lots:** Camp Gibbi Gibbi (getaway), Revive & Thrive Retreat (Custom Venue, 64×64), Love Highland (getaway, residential), Jellyfish Jubilee (Vacation Rental), Plumbird Park (Playground), Fearful Fish Lounge (Lounge), plus residential lots.
- **Lot traits:** none found. [?]

### Royalty & Legacy (EP, 12 Feb 2026, Ondarion: Dambele, Verdemar, Bellacorde)
- **New lot types:** **Backroom** and **Community Market** (details in section 1). Both are confirmed by the EA Q&A, simscommunity and EA's blog.
- **New lot trait:** **Clandestine** (see section 5).
- **Other world features:**
  - **Secret Passageways** (buy-mode object linking lots).
  - **Gazebos and pavilions** in every neighbourhood host Swordsmanship Tournaments.
  - **Palaces and estates** (Bellacorde Palace, Verdemar Palace, Abrantes Estate) are Residential lots that host Grand Balls.
  - New lot sizes.
- **Ondarion lots (16 new, 18 living):** Backroom *Hardcover* (20×20, Dambele), Zanbira Market (Community Market, Dambele), Ye Olde Voyager (Bar, Verdemar), a 40×30 Nightclub in Bellacorde, two "2 Units" residential lots, an empty 64×64 lot in Bellacorde, and more.
- The 16 Apr 2026 patch fixed the Verdemar lot being shown reversed on the map.

### Free patches and other content, 2025–2026
- **18 Sep 2025:** **Playground** venue added. A venue-type filter was added to Event Planning.
- **3 Feb 2026:** Patch added activities to Clubs, Small Businesses and Custom Venues. The **Lost Legacies** live event (to 16 Mar 2026) gave away the **Regal Residence** lot trait.
- **17 Mar 2026:** Kits moved to the in-game Marketplace.
- **Mar–Oct 2026 patches** (16 Apr, 30 Jun, 21 Jul, 6 Aug, 25 Aug, 22 Sep): no new lot types. Fixes included:
  - many love seats and benches now count as "Benches" for venue requirements (16 Apr);
  - Wedding Venues placed from the Gallery no longer become Residential (22 Sep);
  - Residential Rental lot taxes restored (Aug).
- **Kits, Maker Packs and capsule kits in 2025–2026:** none found that add a lot type.

### Earlier recent packs (for completeness)
- **Growing Together (Mar 2023):** Recreation Center.
- **Horse Ranch (Jul 2023):** Equestrian Centre (hidden); Wild Prairie Grass lot challenge.
- **For Rent (Dec 2023):** Residential Rental; Mold and Maintenance Troubles lot challenges.
- **Lovestruck (Jul 2024):** no new lot type; **Singles Hangout** lot trait.
- **Life & Death (Oct 2024):** **Cemetery** lot type; **On Hallowed Grounds** lot trait.

## 5. Relevant lot traits and challenges from the newest packs
