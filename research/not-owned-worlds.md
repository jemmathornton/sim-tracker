# Worlds she doesn't own yet: rough lot ideas (16 Oct)

Rough ideas for the five worlds she doesn't own yet, so there's a plan ready if she buys one. Nothing is decided and nothing on the site has changed. Sources: the Sims Wiki world pages, the game's own lot descriptions, her placeholder plan, and the game's region tuning (which lot types each world allows). Counts in the tables are her current totals across the 25 owned worlds.

## Forgotten Hollow (Vampires)

### As the game ships it
- **One neighbourhood, five lots round a square** with a statue of Vlad, the founder. All five are Residential:
  - Straud Mansion (40×30): Vladislaus Straud.
  - Wolfsbane Manor (30×30): Caleb and Lilith Vatore.
  - Garliclauter Place (30×20): uninhabited gothic Victorian. Lot trait Vampire Nexus ("Vampires are more likely to visit").
  - Widowshild Townhome (30×20): uninhabited, 1 bedroom. Lot trait Registered Vampire Lair (the game says it works on homes only).
  - Fledermaus Bend (30×30): empty, with bats in a nearby cave.
- **No community lots at all.** No families without a home.
- **Open world:** the square, a graveyard (scenery, not a lot), Plasmafruit and the Sixam Mosquito Trap. Nights last longer here. With Werewolves, Moonwood Mill's tunnels can come out at Fledermaus Bend.

### Character
- A tiny gothic hamlet: a vampire village with long nights. Too small for a full set of everyday places.

### Allowed lot types
- Everything ordinary, newer types included. Beach isn't allowed.

## Tartosa (My Wedding Stories)

### As the game ships it
- **Porto Luminoso** (the village and coast):
  - Thebe Estate (50×50): the Laurents, who own the wedding venue.
  - Piccola Luce (20×20): an uninhabited cottage on its own tiny island.
  - Baia dell'Amore (40×20): an empty lot on a sandy beach that glows at night.
  - Villa Vigna (30×20): a Vacation Rental on the cliffs above the vineyards.
  - Celebrazioni d'Amore (50×40): a Lounge, "the best place to host a true party", with gardens and a sea view.
- **Terra Amorosa** (the falls and estuary; starts Green):
  - The Old Wood Nectary (40×30): the Markovics, the wedding musicians.
  - Rifugio dei Pirati (30×20): an uninhabited starter above Hideaway Falls, with a "hidden history" to sleuth out.
  - Via Romanza (30×30): empty. The game asks whether it should be a venue, a honeymoon rental or a home.
  - La Coppia Serena (50×40): a Wedding Venue, "the golden crown of Tartosa's wedding industry".
- **Families without a home:**
  - The Sotos: retired. Camille is a writer and the village historian; Dominique paints in the square.
  - Faiz Jaleel: single, sells flowers in the square.
- **Open world:**
  - The village square: shops, market stalls, the patisserie (fruit tarts and wedding cakes), City Hall for quick weddings, the fountain and a flower cart.
  - Hideaway Falls, the Lover's Tree and the glowing beaches.
  - Never snows.

### Character
- A Mediterranean seaside village that lives off weddings and honeymooners: half holiday resort, half village. The square covers shopping and cake, so it needs few everyday lots.

### Allowed lot types
- Everything ordinary, and **Beach** too. Tartosa is the only world besides Sulani that allows it.

## Moonwood Mill (Werewolves)

### As the game ships it
- **One neighbourhood, five lots**, all built by one simmer (James Turner):
  - The Collective Cabin (40×30): the Volkovs (Kristopher, Jacob, Lily Zhu), the Moonwood Collective pack.
  - New Moon Shack (20×15): an uninhabited starter near the Collective, 2 bedrooms.
  - Prowler's Patch (30×30): empty, across from the library. The game adds: "maybe this would be a great commercial venue".
  - Grimtooth Bar & Bunker (50×40): a Bar run by Celene López. The werewolves' "secret" bunker is part of the lot.
  - The Moonwood Mill Library (40×30): a Library in an old logging factory (librarian Wolfgang Wilder), with a make-shift gym on the old loading dock.
- **Families without a home:** Rory Oaklow (leader of the Wildfangs) and Lou Howell (newly turned).
- **Open world:**
  - Lake Lunvik, the Moonwood River and tunnels under the town.
  - Special locations, not lots: the two pack territories, the mountain top and Greg's home.
  - Luna Fish and Moonpetal Flowers. Snowy winters.

### Character
- A quiet, remote former lumber-mill village in the woods (a Pacific Northwest feel), split between two werewolf packs. Its bar and library already cover the village basics.

### Allowed lot types
- Everything ordinary, newer types included. Beach isn't allowed.

  - Mansión de la Pasión (30×20): an uninhabited family home.
  - Casita del Amor (30×20): uninhabited, for young couples.
  - Caminito del Deseo (50×40): empty.
  - Laguna del Abrazo (30×20): a Lounge. Casita del Amor's text promises "singing, drinking, and a fun game of Who's Up" there.

### Character
- A romantic, Mexican-inspired city in three parts: a nightlife downtown with towers, a park district and a family suburb. Nights out are well covered; eating out and culture aren't.

### Allowed lot types

- The Penthouse lot type isn't allowed here, so the two penthouses are probably ordinary Residential lots (check in game).

## Innisgreen (Enchanted by Nature)

### As the game ships it
- **Coast of Adhmor** (a busy coastal village that honours gnomes):
  - 8 Gnome Coast Street (30×20): Glen Nhoam.
  - 1 Mari Shores Road (30×20): an uninhabited two-storey home, close to the museum.
  - 42 Normfair Drive (40×30): the Ladipos.
  - The GNOM Exhibit (40×30): a Museum in an old lighthouse.
- **Sprucederry Grove** (a country village known for foraging; starts Green):
  - Fern & Frond Wading Pond (64×64): empty, "enough resources for those living off the land", with "local shops" nearby.
  - Whistling Thistle Gardens (40×30): a Park, run as a community garden.
  - Greenwood Hamlet Residence (30×20): an uninhabited starter near the park and Clover's Seeds and Supplies, pitched at apothecary Sims.
  - Gringle's (30×30): a Bar. Devlin's Books, behind it, is a location, not a lot.
  - Gimmley Gardensbrook (30×30): the Enchanted Lovers (Todd Attenberry, who runs an apothecary business, and Elric DeMuray).

  - Crannamor Residential (20×20): an uninhabited home built into a huge tree.
  - The Watering Hole (40×30): empty, "just enough to live off".
  - Starsong Lounge (30×30): a Lounge.
  - Puckitt Hall (50×50): the Summerdreams (High Fae).
  - Bard Boulevard (30×20): the Mystical Besties (Nadya Kishka and Iris Ladipo).
- **No families without a home.**
- **Open world:**
  - The Spruce Almighty and the Fairy Council.
  - Causeway-style rocks on the coast.
  - A teleporter in Everdew to other worlds' secret lots.
  - Village stalls and Clover's garden shop.
  - Snowy winters, except in Everdew.

### Character
- An Irish-inspired island of three villages: a coastal village, a country village and a fairy forest. It has a pub, a lounge, a museum, a park and a bookshop. It has no café, nowhere to eat, no library, no cemetery and no vet.

### Allowed lot types
- Everything ordinary, newer types included. Beach isn't allowed, despite the coast.

## Across the five

- **Newer lot types they'd add:**
  - Backroom: Garliclauter Place (4 → 5, still about one per world).
  - Community Market: Caminito del Deseo and Fern & Frond Wading Pond (4 → 6). Markets also lift shops, which are low.
  - Karaoke Bar: Laguna del Abrazo (3 → 4). It would make a third karaoke world after San Myshuno and Tomarang.
  - Beach: Baia dell'Amore (2 → 3). That makes a second beach world; only Tartosa and Sulani allow beaches.
  - Residential Rental: New Moon Cabins (20 → 21).
  - Spa: The Watering Hole (3 → 4).
  - Small Business Residential: maybe Faiz's florist cottage, only if she plays him.
- **Older types they'd help:**
  - Restaurants: Villa Vigna and Calle del Ensueño (14 → 16, still below 18–25).
  - Café lots: 1 Mari Shores Road (4 → 5).
  - Cemeteries: Fledermaus Bend (6 → 7).
  - Gyms: SUDOR, as shipped (5 → 6).
- **More nightlife as shipped:**
  - Lounges 5 → 7 (Celebrazioni d'Amore, Starsong Lounge), even with Laguna del Abrazo as karaoke.
  - Nightclubs 8 → 9 (Mirador del Amor).
  - Both go just over the ranges, which were set for 25 worlds.
- **Not used, on purpose:**
  - Onsen: suits Japan-style or hot-spring worlds.
  - Thrift and Boba Store: no natural spot.
  - Recreation Center: an option for Vista Hermosa.
  - Custom Venue: an option for Fern & Frond.
  - Haunted House Residential: no empty home has a ghost story; Villa Cálida's is a rental.
