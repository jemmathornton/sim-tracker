# Moving households in the new save (researched 23 Oct 2026)

## At a glance

1. **Moving inside one save is safe for relationships.** Every Sim in a save shares one pool, so moving a household to another lot or world keeps friends, family tree, jobs and clubs. Only the furniture choice (keep or sell) and money change.
2. **The Library breaks links to anyone outside the household.** Relationships and family-tree links survive only between Sims saved together in one household. Skills, traits and looks travel; inventories don't.
3. **To keep a family tree across saves, save the whole family as one household** (up to 8 Sims and pets), place it, then split it in Manage Households. Splitting inside a save keeps every link.
4. **Heart every household you place or move** (My Households). Homeless and unhearted households can be culled, even premades.
5. **The big 2026 risk is the family tree rework (3 Feb 2026).** Players still report wrong or missing relatives in long saves. Check each family's tree after moving, and keep a backup before each batch.

Note: EA's forums block automatic reading, so some points come from search snippets of forum posts. Dates for those are approximate. Points marked **unconfirmed** come from one player or a low-quality guide.

## 1. Moving a household inside the save

**Steps**
1. Manage Worlds, open the household's world, select its home (or open Manage Households), then **More (…) > Move Household**.
2. Pick the new lot. It can be in any world except the holiday worlds ("You can't move Sims into destination worlds").
3. Choose **Keep furniture** (it goes into the household inventory and the old house is left empty) or **Sell furniture** (it stays behind and they get the money).
4. If the lot is lived in, the game offers to evict the household there or merge with them. Don't merge unless you mean to.
5. Heart the household afterwards (see 4).

**What changes and what doesn't**
- **Relationships, family tree, traits, skills, aspirations:** kept. All Sims in a save live in one shared pool across worlds, so a move doesn't touch them. Guides agree, though I found no EA page that says it outright.
- **Jobs:** normally kept. One EA forum request says career data was lost on move-in for some players (**unconfirmed**, no date).
- **Clubs:** membership belongs to each Sim, so it is kept. A club whose hangout is in the old world still works. (Reasoned, **unconfirmed** by a source.)
- **Dynasties (Royalty & Legacy):** a developer said dynasty membership "is only restricted to family (not world, household or career)", so relatives in different worlds and households can share a dynasty (SimsCommunity, 29 Jan 2026). I found nothing on prestige changing when a household moves.
- **Irreplaceable items** (paintings, upgraded objects): one guide warns these don't come with the family. Put them in a Sim's inventory first (**unconfirmed**).
- **Money:** if they buy a lot they pay for it. Evicted households keep "any simoleons they had, plus the value of the home" (SimsCommunity, Sep 2023).

**The heart and Neighbourhood Stories**
- Households you have played, edited or placed go into **My Households** with a blue heart. Clicking the heart moves a household between My and Other Households. This doesn't reset anything; it only changes which list it sits in (SimsCommunity, Sep 2023).
- Neighbourhood Stories is on for Other Households and off for My Households by default. A per-household setting always beats the global one.
- To stop the game moving premades into homes you've planned for someone else, switch off **Move In / Move Out** in Neighbourhood Stories (global, or per household). "Fill empty homes" no longer exists; Move In replaced it. Setting a house to 0 beds also stops the game moving Sims into it (one player's tip).

## 2. Library: from an old save to the new one

**Steps**
1. Back up the old save first.
2. In the old save, open the household in CAS (Manage Households > Edit in CAS) and save it to the Library. Give it a clear name.
3. In the new save, Manage Worlds > Library (the household icon) > choose the lot > Place.
4. Heart it straight away.

**What's kept and lost**
| Kept | Lost |
|---|---|
| Looks, traits, skills (as when saved) | Relationships with any Sim outside the household |
| Relationships and family links between Sims saved together | Family-tree links to anyone outside the household |
| Pets in the household | Sim inventories |
| Occult (reported to carry over; **unconfirmed** for ranks and powers) | Memories and sentiments with outsiders (go with the relationships) |
| | Links to collectibles; containers on the lot come back empty |

- One guide (TheSimsTree, 23 Feb 2026) warns that "sometimes even sims who are in the same household lose their family relationships". Check the tree after placing.
- **Careers, aspirations and money:** I found no 2025–2026 source that settles these. Check each household after placing (**unconfirmed**).
- **Dynasties:** I found no source on what a Library copy keeps. Expect to set the dynasty up again in the new save (**unconfirmed**).

**Keeping a family tree across saves**
- Put the whole family into **one household** in the old save (8 Sims and pets at most), save that to the Library, place it, then split it in the new save (see 3). Links survive because they were saved together.
- If a family is bigger than 8, save the closest links together (parents with children, spouses together). Other links have to be made again. Without mods the only way is merging in CAS and setting relationships there. With mods, MC Command Center can add them.
- The 2026 family tree update (3 Feb 2026) added in-laws, step and half relatives, Distant Relative, and up to 10 generations. It did **not** change what the Library keeps.
- Dead relatives: save them in a household too, or they drop off the tree (TheSimsTree, Feb 2026).

## 3. Splitting and merging

**Steps**
1. Manage Households, select the household, click the two-arrow icon (Transfer Sims).
2. **Split:** select the Sims who leave, click **Create a New Household**, then move the new household onto a lot (or leave it homeless and heart it).
3. **Merge:** pick the other household from any world on the right-hand side and move Sims across.

**Rules**
- A household holds at most 8 Sims and/or pets. A pregnant Sim counts as two, or more with twins or triplets.
- Children, toddlers, babies and pets need a teen or older in the household. Teens may live alone.
- Splitting and merging inside a save keeps every relationship.
- One older EA forum report says a parent vanished from the tree after a child moved out, unless a sibling stayed with the parent (date unknown, **unconfirmed** in 2026).
- Asking a homeless Sim to join a household froze the transfer screen in Apr 2024 (Steam). Use Manage Households to split or join instead.

## 4. Homeless premades: finding and protecting them

**Finding them**
- Manage Households > Other Households. Filter by world, and by **not in world** (homeless). Each household shows as in world or not.
- Households evicted when you placed a build over their home are "homeless townies" and stay in this list.

**Protecting them from culling**
- Heart them (My Households). An EA staff member said being housed doesn't protect a household in Other Households. Only My Households is safe.
- Culling removes a whole household at once, never one Sim. Ghosts have their own timer (90 days after death).
- Keep the My Households limit high enough: options are 80 (default), 150, 200 (recommended) and Unlimited (SimsCommunity, Sep 2023). Unlimited means no cap on how many you protect, not that everyone is protected.
- The whole-world limit is about 800 Sims, not counting ghosts (forum figure, **unconfirmed**).

## 5. Known 2025–2026 bugs

- **Family tree rework (3 Feb 2026, PC 1.121.342):** EA warned it could show "missing relatives" for players with family mods. Hotfixes on 12 and 19 Feb 2026 fixed missing tree links, mixed-up Distant Relative labels, overlapping portraits and a crash. Players still report parents turned into aunts or uncles and couples turned into siblings, some on 1.124.55. One player said re-doing the relationships fixed the tree but cut links to everyone outside the household.
- **Premade pets and horses disappearing:** under investigation in EA's Laundry List of 27 Jan 2026. I found no later note saying it's fixed. Check pets after moving premade families.
- **Dynasties vanishing:** one Xbox player lost every dynasty in a very large save (EA forum, about Sep 2026, no fix found).
- **Custom-painted pets and occults** placed through Manage Households lost their texture; fixed 10 Jul 2025.
- **Merging with "not in world" Sims:** the button was greyed out; fixed in May 2024.
- **Aspiration goals reset** after "Bring Here" on a travelled Sim; fixed 4 Nov 2025.
- **For Rent, Life & Death, Businesses & Hobbies:** I found no patch note or report of traits or relationships lost when moving households after these packs.
