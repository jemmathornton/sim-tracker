## How small businesses work (the bits that matter here)

- **Setting up:** register for §750, then pay §75 each time it opens. Pick a name, a logo, a description and **up to 5 activities** (or a preset: Bar, Gym, Library, Lounge, Museum, Nightclub, Park, Pool, Pottery Studio, Shop, Tattoo Parlor).
- **Customers:** pick the kind of Sim you want (age, career, traits, skill level and so on), as required or preferred.
- **Rooms:** each one is Residential (household only; Small Business Residential lots only), Public or Employees only.
- **Getting paid:**
  - **Selling things:** anything on a retail surface or display wall is for sale (Buy mode, Objects by Function, Small Business). Only things that fit on a display: crafted items, small and medium objects, wall pictures. **No furniture and no clothes on mannequins.** No restocking by itself: stock up before opening, or have someone make things while it’s open.
  - **Ticket kiosk** (§225): one-time entry fee or an hourly fee. This is how classes, cafés and services earn.
  - **Food:** self-serve only, with no waiters. Cooked food handed out earns nothing by itself, so cafés charge entry. Bars and espresso bars do take money. For food, the owner must be on the lot.
  - **Price modifier:** −50% to +100% on everything.
- **Staff:** up to 3 employees (teens and up, Sims they know), with up to 5 tasks each. Household members can help without being employees.
- **Classes:** the Whiteboard lets a Sim teach a class or lecture in any skill they’re good at.
- **Renown and perks:** five levels, from Hopeful Hustle to Local Legend.
  - **Alignment:** the way the owner treats customers pushes the business towards **Dreamer** (honest) or **Schemer** (shady).
  - **Perks** follow the alignment, e.g. Sway to Buy (Schemer: talk customers into buying), Bartering (Dreamer: customers pay in rare collectibles), Customer Appreciation Day.

## 1. The Quarry Building (Evergreen Harbor, Grims Quarry)

**The lot:** 40×30, Small Business Residential, not built.
- The game’s text: the old quarry building is “ready for a makeover” from “lonely and obsolete to new and fun at parties”.
- Grims Quarry is the green neighbourhood. Port Promise is the industrial one.

**The owners:**
- **Bess Sterling:**
  - Eco Innovator aspiration; Cheerful, Ambitious, Bro; bonus trait Collector.
  - Civil Designer (she keeps the job, her call 22 Oct).
  - Logic 5, Charisma 6, Juice Fizzing 4, Handiness 3.
- **Jules Rico:**
  - Mansion Baron aspiration; Materialistic, Ambitious, Bro; bonus trait Business Savvy.
  - Style Influencer.
  - Writing 4, Photography 2.
- **EA’s bio:** they’re a power couple who want money. Bess steers city programmes towards “her bottom line”. Jules loves “convincing people to buy his rejected styles”.

**Already in the world:**
- The Portsmouth Promenade: the Greenburgs’ classes and port clean-up.
- Canal Corner: the Tinkers.
- The Caboose (bar), The Old Mill (Backroom) and Sprucewood Square (Community Market).
- So The Quarry Building shouldn’t be another bar or another class venue.

### Idea A: upcycled design studio and showroom (best fit)
- **Fit:** Bess’s Eco Innovator aspiration is about fabricating and recycling. Jules is the salesman. It turns an old quarry building into a trendy “eco” shop, which matches the game’s makeover text.
- **In game:**
  - **Activities:** Browse and Buy Items, Fabricate Objects (Eco Lifestyle), View Art, Take Photos, plus one spare.
  - **What sells:** Bess fabricates decor between shifts and stocks the retail surfaces and display walls before opening. Big furniture can stand around as a showroom, but can’t be sold.
  - Jules works the floor.
  - **Perks:** Sway to Buy fits Jules exactly. Bartering suits Bess as a Collector.
- **Bonus story (optional):** run it **Schemer**, a “green” shop that’s really about money. Bess already dislikes Knox Greenburg, and the Greenburgs’ Portsmouth Promenade could be the honest Dreamer business across town.
- **Changes the game:** nothing. The lot is already their home and business in her plan.

### Idea B: style studio and photo shoot space (Jules’s side)
- **Fit:** Jules is a Style Influencer. A styling studio lets customers dress up and get their picture taken.
- **In game:**
  - **Activities:** Try On Outfits (dressers and mirrors), Take Photos (a backdrop corner), Record Video (Get Famous), Browse and Buy Items (prints of his photos on a display wall), Drink Coffee.
  - **Paid:** an hourly fee at a ticket kiosk.
- **Catch:** a small business **can’t sell clothes**. If she wants a real clothes shop, the lot would have to be Retail, and then nobody could live there. As a styling studio it works as it is.
- **Changes the game:** nothing.

### Idea C: co-working hub with a juice bar
- **Fit:**
  - Hustlers renting desks to other hustlers.
  - It fits Evergreen Harbor’s “what future will it be?” story.
  - It gives Bess, the Civil Designer, a place to pitch her plans.
  - Her Juice Fizzing skill makes the drinks.
- **In game:**
  - **Activities:** Browse the Web, Program, Drink Coffee, Read, Attend Classes and Lectures (Bess teaches Charisma or Logic on the Whiteboard).
  - **Paid:** an hourly “desk” fee at the kiosk.
  - **Check in game:** whether fizzed juices can be sold from a display.
- **Changes the game:** nothing.

### Idea D: party and event hall (the game’s own “fun at parties”)
- **Fit:** it uses the lot’s game text. But Evergreen Harbor already has a bar and a Backroom, so it adds the least.
- **In game:** Dance, Listen to Music, DJ (Get Together), Mix Bar Drinks, Take Photos. Hourly fee.
- **Changes the game:** nothing.

**A mix that uses all 5 activities:** Browse and Buy Items, Fabricate Objects, Try On Outfits, Take Photos, Drink Coffee. That’s a design and style showroom with a coffee counter: Bess makes the stock and Jules sells it.

## 2. 1 Cobblebottom Street (Henford-on-Bagley, Finchwick)

**The lot:** 20×15, 1 bedroom and 1 bathroom, Small Business Residential, built (her build), not shared.
- The game’s text calls it a “cozy but cluttered home” with an “excess of character”.
- **What Henford lacks:** a café or tea room. Finchwick has a pub, the Village Hall and stalls (a grocer, a garden shop, a Creature Keeper) and a weekly fair.
- **Downstairs shop, flat above:** the one bedroom suits one Sim or a couple living upstairs or at the back.

### Business ideas

**A. Village tea room (best fit)**
- **Fit:** it fills the gap a posh English village would feel most. It’s Koffieboon’s model (Drink Coffee, Drink Tea, Read, Classes).
- **In game:**
  - **Activities:** Drink Tea, Eat (cakes put out on a counter), Bake, Read, Play Cards.
  - **Paid:** a one-time entry fee at a kiosk. Handed-out food and tea don’t earn by themselves.
  - Pottery tea sets (Businesses & Hobbies) suit the tables.
  - The owner must be on the lot when food is served.
- **Changes the game:** nothing.

**B. Tea room with resident cats**
- **Fit:** Henford’s whole story is “friendship in the furry and feathery creatures”. Cats & Dogs lets small businesses run as pet cafés. The lot-trait idea already on file for this lot is Homey, Chef’s Kitchen and Cat Hangout.
- **In game:** Drink Tea, Eat, Play with Pets, Read, plus one spare.
- **Changes the game:** nothing.

**C. Old-fashioned sweet shop**
- **Fit:** jars of sweets in a village shop window. The candy machine (Businesses & Hobbies) makes cotton candy, lollipops and jelly beans.
- **In game:**
  - **Activities:** Make Treats, Browse and Buy Items, Eat.
  - The owner or a helper keeps the machine going and stocks the display cases. Sweets are small enough for displays (check in game).
- **Changes the game:** nothing.

**D. Bric-a-brac and antiques shop, with tea**
- **Fit:** it plays on the game’s “cluttered” and “excess of character” text.
- **In game:**
  - **Activities:** Browse and Buy Items, View Collectibles, Drink Tea.
  - Stock is found and collected things (crystals, fossils, frogs and so on) and small decor.
  - The Bartering perk brings in more collectibles.
- **Changes the game:** nothing.

**E. Village crafts shop: wool, stitching and flowers**
- **Fit:** Cottage Living is Henford’s pack, and cross-stitch comes from it.
- **In game:**
  - **Activities:** Cross-stitch, Knitting (Nifty Knitting), Flower Arranging (Seasons), Browse and Buy Items, Attend Classes and Lectures (a stitching circle at the Whiteboard).
  - Stock: hoops, knits and bouquets on displays.
- **Catch:** it partly overlaps the Crumplebottoms’ garden-shop stall.
- **Changes the game:** nothing.
