# Build priorities

Written overnight 16–17 October 2026. Research only: nothing in the app, data, tools or TODO.md was changed. Suggestions only; every choice is hers.

From her plan as it is in `data/worlds/` (16 Oct), the Gallery numbers of the last refresh (14 Oct) and `data/gallery/history.csv` (checks on 11, 13 and 14 Oct). Scripts were run from a scratch folder and not kept in the project.

## Headlines

- **25 owned worlds, 359 lots planned: 195 built, 168 shared, 162 still to build.** 18 of the 162 are special or secret lots (police station, hospital, hidden lots and the like), which are harder to edit.
- **Closest to done:** Britechester is complete. Newcrest needs only Rippling Flats. Granite Falls and Glimmerbrook have 2 lots left each.
- **Quickest wins:** 28 lots are built but not shared. Boba Ippai (Mt. Komorebi) stands out: a Thrift and Boba Store in her best world for downloads.
- **What does well:** 30×20 community lots (7 of her 8 most downloaded builds), small businesses, Thrift and Boba Stores, Recreation Centers and Community Spaces. Rentals get the most favourites for their downloads. Builds from the newer worlds (Mt. Komorebi, Henford-on-Bagley, Nordhaven) do best. Builds needing 4 or more packs get about a third of the downloads of those needing 3 or fewer.
- **Growth can’t be judged yet:** three checks in four days. The first full week comes with the Sunday refresh on 18 Oct.
- **Top suggestions:** share Boba Ippai; build Rippling Flats (finishes Newcrest), The Gnome’s Arms, Hazakura Lounge and Willow Creek Archive. The full order is in section 4.

- Her 2017–2019 builds typically used 8 to 11 packs, so age and packs overlap. Looking only at 2020 onwards, the drop is still there: 3 packs or fewer typically get 66 to 68 downloads, 4 or more 22.
- Every one of her shared builds in a pack world uses that world’s own pack, so a base-game-only build is possible only in Willow Creek, Oasis Springs and Newcrest. From 2020 on, builds needing just their world’s pack typically get 61 downloads; with one or two more, 68; with four or more, 22.
- Builds using the newer packs do better (High School Years, Snowy Escape, Eco Lifestyle and Growing Together typically 49 to 55). Builds using older “extra” packs do worse (Get Together, Seasons, Spa Day, Parenthood and Cool Kitchen Stuff typically 16 to 19), probably because they push the pack count up.
- Newer uploads do better: typically 71 to 72 for 2022 and early 2023, 52 for later ones, against 13 to 34 for 2017–2021.
