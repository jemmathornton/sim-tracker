# Kaori Nishidake's parents: what the game says, and how to give them graves (prepared 6–7 Nov)

For the job on your list ("make them in Create a Sim … graves in a Nishidake family plot at 2-4-1 Wakabamori"). Report and how-to only. The names, looks and story are yours to choose.

## What EA says
- **The Nishidakes' game bio:** "Kaori moved to Mt. Komorebi after losing her parents." So her parents lived and died somewhere else, and she came to her grandparents afterwards. A family plot near the grandparents still fits: the family brought them home, or it's a memorial.
- **The game's family tree** puts Shigeru and Sachiko on her **mother's** side. So her mother was their daughter, and her father is unknown. That's why the site shows one "Unknown" mother box above Kaori.
- **No names, ages or causes of death** are given anywhere: not in the game's files, the Sims Wiki or simscommunity's Mt. Komorebi guide.

## What you'd make
- **Her mother:** Shigeru and Sachiko's daughter. Kaori is a teen, so her mother would have been an adult when she died.
- **Her father:** anyone you like. Kaori has the Nishidake surname, which could be her father's, her mother's, or one she took when she moved in with her grandparents. Your call.

## How to give them real graves (to check in game)
1. **In the new save, in Manage Households**, edit the Nishidakes and add two Sims. In Create a Sim, use the relationship tool to make:
   - the mother Shigeru and Sachiko's daughter;
   - both of them Kaori's parents.

   Adding them inside the Nishidake household is what lets you set those family links.
2. **Their deaths have to happen while you're playing that household.** Players report that Sims who die off screen leave no tombstone or urn.
   - MC Command Center can end a Sim's life on the spot, and lets you choose how they die.
   - Their remains (a tombstone or an urn, whichever the game picks) then go to the household's inventory.
3. **Take the remains to Wakabamori Cemetery** in a Sim's inventory and place them on the lot. Life & Death's **gravesites** (dirt or stone plots) are made to hold them.
4. **Then move the extras out:** Kaori's household stays Shigeru, Sachiko and Kaori.

**Watch out:**
- **Ghosts:** a dead Sim's remains can let their ghost appear at night. At a cemetery that's part of the charm, but it's worth knowing.
- **Family words:** once the parents exist, the site's family tree shows them instead of the "Unknown" box, after I read your new save. Their dates and how they died come from the save.
- **Simpler:** if you'd rather not create Sims, ordinary headstones in a family corner look the same and change nothing.
