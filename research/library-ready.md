**How to read it**
- **From:** a. EA premade, stays in the save (never through the Library, as `new-save-plan.md` says). b. Your Library. c. Only in a save (named). d. Nowhere yet: you make them.
- **Dates** are the Library file dates. Lots of files say 21 Dec 23, probably the day the folder was copied, so the real date may be older.
- **Creators:** copies saved under MsTiquelle or MissTiquelle are shown with that name. They look like your older Gallery names (the Tiquelle Legacy, a Sim called Jemma Tiquelle). Downloads from other creators and EA’s own Library copies are left out.
- A household’s name can appear twice when more than one plan line uses it (Kirkland in Newcrest and Magnolia Promenade). The plan counts 141 households, this list 142. One is probably a double (Davidson and “Davidson 1”, or the two Kirklands).
