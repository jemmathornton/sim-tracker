# Your own Sims’ relationships in your saves (overnight 18 Oct)

Research only. Nothing in `site/`, `data/` or `tools/` was changed and nothing was committed. Saves were read from temporary copies through `tools/sims4files.py` (`read_save`); the two “Building…” saves were skipped. Relationship names come from the game’s own text (750+ relationship bits; hidden markers such as “Has Met” and conversation moods left out).

What was read: all 28 other saves, for every pair where **one Sim is one of yours on the site and the other is also on the site** (matched by full name). Newer saves keep relationships in one list (`relationship_service`); the older ones keep them on each Sim, one direction each, so a pair can show two slightly different levels (e.g. Friend one way, Acquaintances the other).

## Suggested data changes (not applied)

Family words are worked out from `parents` and `partners`, so family goes there; friendships go in each Sim’s `rels` as `[name, label]`, using the game’s label.

**Family across households (straightforward)**
1. `sylvia-cadberry`: `"parents": ["leah-moon-cadberry"]` (*Not So Berry*).
2. Natalie and Thomas Watson: a placeholder mother in `people.json`, e.g. `"unknown-watson": {"name": "Unknown", "sex": "f", "placeholder": true}`, and `"parents": ["unknown-watson"]` on both `natalie-watson` and `thomas-watson`. This gives EA’s Thomas a sister (your story, consistent in three saves).

**Couples inside households (straightforward)**
