# Nordhaven: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (15 Oct)

For the underused-lot-types talk (gyms). Sources: the Sims Wiki's Nordhaven page, the game's own lot descriptions, her Gallery uploads, and the game's region tuning (region_Residential_EP18World).

## As the game ships it

- **12 lots, all built by EA Creator Network simmers.**
  - **Homes with a business:** Terra-Potta (Koopmans, pottery), Spilled Neon (Sarpongs, tattoo) and Koffieboon (Lodens, café).
  - **Plain home:** The Old Foundry (the Urbane Housesharers).
  - **Uninhabited:** Old Torget House, and New Harbor Park (50×40). The game describes New Harbor Park as a business plan that "fell through, leaving not much more than an empty shell of a building", with locals hoping an entrepreneur will take it on.
  - **Empty:** Fisher's Horizon, Lighthouse Lookout (50×40), Inked Inlet and Comfortably Canalside.
  - **Community:** The Rouge Note (Lounge) and Konst Modern (Museum, with pottery, painting and woodworking stations).
- **Families without a home:** the Hoffmans (the candy stall), Corey Nyhus (a bartender) and Zain Amri (a singer).
- **Open world:** market squares with daily hobby meetups, and stalls including the Hoffmans' candy stall, Gramma Hattie's bakery stall and the Terra-Potta stall.
- **No gym, restaurant or library.**

## Her plan

- **Gammelvik:**
  - Konst Modern (Museum, not built) and The Rouge Note (built).
  - Koffieboon (Lodens, not built).
  - Old Torget House (rental, 3 units, built, empty).
  - Fisherman's Wharf on Fisher's Horizon (Johanssons, built). Her upload says it houses "a tattoo parlour, pottery studio and candy cafe", with the owners living upstairs.
  - Canalside Daycare on Comfortably Canalside (built).
- **Iverstad:**
  - New Harbor Park (Small Business Residential, empty, not built).
  - Terra-Potta (Koopmans), The Old Foundry and Spilled Neon (Sarpongs), none built.
  - Inked Inlet (Library, not built).
  - Lighthouse Lookout (rental, empty, not built).

## Allowed lot types

- Gym, Restaurant, Recreation Center, Café, Library, Park, Pool, Spa, Karaoke Bar, Community Market and Small Business are all allowed.
- Beach isn't.

## Notes for the talk

- **New Harbor Park** is the one big free lot in town. Its "empty shell" in the old industrial district is a natural converted-warehouse gym.
- **The city also has no restaurant.** The only other big free lot is Lighthouse Lookout (a rental with no residents planned), which could be a seaside restaurant.
- **Possible double-up:** Fisherman's Wharf already has a tattoo parlour and a pottery studio, and Spilled Neon (tattoo) and Terra-Potta (pottery) are planned as business homes too.
