# Lot-trait ideas for every lot still to build (overnight 5–6 Nov)

One or two ideas for each lot, ready for when you build it. **Every idea is a suggestion.** Your rule (1 Nov) is that 1 or 2 traits is fine, just not none, so most lots get two.

How the ideas were picked:
- **"EA:"** is what the game's untouched lot has. Keeping one of those is often the simplest choice.
- Ideas fit who lives there or what the place is for, and build on research/lot-trait-ideas.md (17 Oct).
- **Every idea is allowed on that lot type**, checked against the game's own trait rules (read 5 Nov). For example, Cat Hangout isn't allowed on restaurants, spas, onsens or vet clinics, and Hottest Spot in Town isn't allowed on homes.
- **Left out:** traits from packs you don't own (Vampires, Lovestruck, Enchanted by Nature), Regal Residence (only if you unlocked it in February's event), challenges (they don't count as traits), and special lots, which can't take traits.
- **San Myshuno's flats** come with a trait the game sets and you can't change. It's marked "locked".
- **Some traits are mostly for the story.** Chef's Kitchen on a restaurant only helps if one of your Sims cooks there (the staff do the cooking). Good Schools only helps children and teens who live on the lot. Private Dwelling does little on a venue. What each trait does is in research/lot-trait-ideas.md, section 1.
