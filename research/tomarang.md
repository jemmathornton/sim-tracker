# Tomarang: how the game ships it, and what her plan changes (15 Oct)

For the underused-lot-types talk (TODO §5). Sources: the Sims Wiki's Tomarang page, the game's own lot descriptions (research/data/game-lots.json) and the game's region tuning (which lot types Tomarang allows).

## As the game ships it

- **9 lots:**
  - Morensong (the bustling town): Ro Kaya Rockside (empty, 40×30), Taka Soi 15 (rental, the Cahyaputris), Chee-wit Chee-waa Kanto (an empty shophouse home) and ZoSul Taman Botani (National Park, a garden).
  - Koh Sahpa (quieter, along the river): Isda Riverfront (empty), Sungai Point (rental, the Linh-Sadyas and Thi Linh), The Screaming Gecko (Lounge), Tam Nang Sands (empty, beachfront) and Hothotok Shore (the Bun Mas).
- **Open world:**
  - The **Night Market** in Morensong, the world's headline feature: "after-dark shopping and mouth-watering cuisine".
  - A playground (marbles), Tinsu Temple, beaches, Beard Beard Cove, ruins and the Sulea Tiger Sanctuary.
- **The Screaming Gecko** is open-air, with live music, "curated cuisine" and a hot tub. Sungai Point's description puts it "a minute's walk to a screaming good time".
- **Tam Nang Sands:** "Catch a glimpse of aquatic life floating by or soak up the sun! … beachfront access and exceptional views of Koh Sahpa's crystal clear waters."

## Her plan against that

- **Ro Kaya Rockside:** Restaurant (not built). The game says it's "a hopscotch away from the beloved Night Market".
- **Chee-wit Chee-waa Kanto:** a home becomes a Thrift and Bubble Tea Store (built, shared).
- **ZoSul Taman Botani:** National Park becomes a Wedding Venue (a garden wedding).
- **The Screaming Gecko:** Bar.
- **Tam Nang Sands:** Cemetery (not built).
- **Isda Riverfront:** a rental (built).

## Allowed lot types (region_Residential_MultiUnitWorld)

- **Allowed:** Café, Cemetery, Community Market, Gym, Karaoke Bar, Library, National Park, Onsen, Park, Pool, Spa.
- **Not allowed:** Beach (only Sulani's island world allows it).

## Notes for the talk

- **A Community Market would double up on the Night Market.**
- **Tam Nang Sands as a cemetery is at odds with its sunny beachfront description.** Cemeteries are already at the top of their range (7).
- **Karaoke suits Tomarang**, which is inspired by Southeast Asia, where karaoke is everywhere. The Screaming Gecko could become a Karaoke Bar by adding a karaoke machine (and its "screaming good time" fits).
- **A spa also suits Southeast Asia** (massage and wellness). Spas are underused (2).
