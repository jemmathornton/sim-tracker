# Copies in your Library (6 Nov)

Your Library holds 589 items: 273 lots, 93 households and 40 rooms. For 107 names there is more than one copy. This lists them, oldest copy first, so you can tidy up if you ever want to. **Report only: nothing was changed or deleted, and deleting is yours to do.** Read from copies of your Library files.

How to read it:
- The date is the one saved inside each item, not the file date.
- The value is the lot's value in simoleons.
- A name in brackets is who the game credits as the maker: MsTiquelle and MissTiquelle are you, under your old names; the others are the shell-challenge makers (lilsimsie, stiplemad, elizeschreuders) or Maxis.
- "(on the Gallery)": that copy carries a Gallery id.

Worth knowing:
- **18 items are just called "Empty lot"** (2019–2026), some yours and some elizeschreuders'. They're left out of the list below.
- **Lakeview Boarding House:** your newest copy (8 Feb 2026) is saved as a **Custom Venue**, but your plan has it as a 2-unit rental. In "Building #2" it's plain Residential (on your list).
- **Oak Alcove** (25 Oct 2026) is credited to MsTiquelle even though you saved it recently. The game keeps the name a lot was first made under.
- **The Smith Family** (Maxis) is in twice.
- Copies made for a different use are worth keeping until you've decided: The Waterfront as a restaurant (2017) and as a Community Space (2020), and Britechester B&B as a Café and as Residential (same day).
