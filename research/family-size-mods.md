# Capping family size (24 Oct, research only: nothing downloaded)

Her ask (23 Oct): Neighbourhood Stories' Have a Baby stays off until a mod can keep families small.

What turned up (fan forums and guides; check the mod's own pages before installing):

- **MC Command Center (MCCC)**, the main option. Gameplay Settings > Maximum Household Size sets the household cap (it can go lower or higher than the game's 8). A full household can't get pregnant, so a lower cap also caps families. It applies to every household, hers too.
- **MCCC's MC Pregnancy:** Pregnancy Percentage per age group (0 stops random pregnancies) and Valid Pregnancy Ages. No setting named "max children" was found.
- **Full House / Larger Sims Households:** these only raise the cap (to 10–25), the opposite of what she wants.

Nothing found from 2026, so the guides may be older than the current game. MCCC also has its own story progression, which would sit alongside the game's Neighbourhood Stories.

Sources: [EA forum: MCCC household size](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/the-sims-4-gameplay-en/how-do-you-make-your-household-bigger-using-mc-command-center/8498865/replies/8498867), [MC Woohoo docs](https://deaderpool-mccc.com/mcwoohoo.html), [Snooty Sims: household limit mods](https://snootysims.com/wiki/sims-4/sims-4-household-limit-mod/), [CyberPost: MCCC pregnancy chance](https://cyberpost.co/how-to-change-pregnancy-chance-with-mccc/).
