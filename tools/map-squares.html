Where are the neighbourhoods?

Each map has a grid: columns A–H, rows 1–6. Pick a neighbourhood on the right, then click its spot on the map (or type the square, like F4). The next neighbourhood is picked for you. Leave a box empty if a neighbourhood has no single place (some Secret Lots groups), and its card keeps showing the whole map.
