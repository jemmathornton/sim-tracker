She playtests every lot before she uploads it to the Gallery. Many built lots still need testing, so my checks (lot type, traits, units) can't tell whether a lot is ready to upload.

**Why:** on 1 Nov 2026 I listed 14 lots as "ready to upload"; she said she playtests everything first and a bunch still need testing.

**How to apply:** call lots that pass my checks "no problems found, ready for your playtest". Don't present them as ready to upload. Quick to playtest: houses, bars and the like. Slow: residential rentals, wedding venues, custom venues and similar, which need much more testing, so don't expect them as quick uploads. Owners or residents still to pick don't block an upload (her point, the same day). Related: [[lot-traits-enough]].
