When she says she wants to decide something like the community-lot mix, she wants the full breakdown across every world, talked through in chat. On 15 Oct I first offered three open-lot questions as a menu, and she stopped it: "I wanted a full breakdown of entire world." Then I started a for-jemma page, and she said: "I don't want a page I want to disucss it here."

**Why:** she decides by talking it through with the whole picture in front of her. A shortlist hides options, like re-tagging planned lots in other worlds. Menus felt repetitive to her earlier (the Backroom talk), and a page takes the discussion out of the chat.

**How to apply:** give the breakdown in chat, grouped so it can be scanned, then ask in plain words where to start. Save a page or multiple-choice questions for when she asks for one. Related: [[app-shows-main-checkout]]
