When weighing ideas for lots and households, don't count "it adds a story the game doesn't have" as a negative. Jemma already adds her own story (e.g. recreating the Sims 2 Montys, Romeo and Juliette marrying in Tartosa as dynasty outcasts, the Alto family vineyard). She said on 16 Oct: "I'm already adding bits of story the game doesn't have, that shouldn't be a negative. I don't want to change things too much but I like adding in bonus stuff."

**Why:** she enjoys building on EA's lore. What she avoids is changing too much of what the game ships, not adding extras.

She added: "I just want my changes to make sense, like the Monty bit I think is more fixing stuff and runaways to Tartosa makes sense." So the test is whether a change makes sense and fits the lore (ideally fixing something), not whether it's new.

**How to apply:** offer story-rich options freely, and check the lore so additions fit it. The thing to flag is how much an idea changes what the game ships (moving EA families, re-typing lots), not whether the story is invented. Related: [[whole-picture-first]], [[wait-before-next-item]]
