When suggesting where things go, an empty Residential lot (no family planned) is fair game for a venue, and families can be moved to other homes. On 16 Oct she said: "residential with no families can be converted, plus families can move around, don't be too tied to the exact lot."

**How to apply:** look at all lots, including empty homes and homes whose family could move, when finding a spot for a venue. Say who would move and where they could go. This is still subject to [[bonus-story-welcome]] (changes should make sense) and to her preference for not rebuilding built lots.
