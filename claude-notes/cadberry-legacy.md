The Cadberry households (Ella-Mint in Willow Creek, Leah-Moon in Oasis Springs, Sylvia & Kenny in Newcrest, the Isda Riverfront landlords in Tomarang, and more) are one family: her Not So Berry legacy (confirmed 18 Oct 2026). Her "Spectra" Gallery household is tagged #notsoberry #mint #founder.

She keeps each generation alive when they move out, so they all stay in the world, each with their own household, often in different worlds. She knows this isn't how the game normally goes.

**How to apply:** treat Cadberry households in any world as relatives, not coincidences. When she adds their Sims, link them into one family tree across worlds. Don't flag "lots of Cadberrys" as a problem. Related: [[world-geography]]
