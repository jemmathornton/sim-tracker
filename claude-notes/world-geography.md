Her own map of how the worlds sit together (18 Oct 2026; replaces the 16 Oct notes). Use it when judging whether a world "needs" a lot type, since its Sims can use nearby worlds. It's her picture, not where EA based them.

1. **Two cities on opposite coasts of a thin strip of land, not too far apart:**
   - **San Myshuno** (city), with **Newcrest** as its suburb and **Magnolia Promenade** as a retail park nearish by.
   - **San Sequoia** (city), with **Willow Creek** as its suburb (she moved WC over to SS when SS came out, to spread things out).
2. **Desert:** Oasis Springs, Del Sol Valley, StrangerVille, Chestnut Ridge. Oasis Springs and Del Sol Valley are closer to each other than to the other two.
3. **Cool, spooky places near each other:** Forgotten Hollow, Moonwood Mill, Ravenwood.
4. **Across the world from group 3, same cool climate but not spooky:** Granite Falls, Glimmerbrook, and Innisgreen (closest to Glimmerbrook).
5. **Near each other:** Britechester, Henford-on-Bagley.
6. **Not too far from group 5, away from group 7:** Brindleton Bay, Nordhaven, Windenburg.
7. **Between groups 1 and 5, but not close to each other (a diamond shape):** Copperdale, Evergreen Harbor.
8. **One very big island:** Tartosa and Ondarion on opposite sides; Ciudad Enamorada probably on it too.
9. **Near each other:** Gibbi Point, Tomarang.
10. **Islands, not near much else:** Sulani.
11. **Far north:** Mt. Komorebi (nearest is the desert, group 2, but not really near).
12. **Far south, the opposite way from the desert:** Selvadorada.

Willow Creek is still the quiet family town: no bar or nightclub on purpose (Movers & Shakers became a Recreation Center).
