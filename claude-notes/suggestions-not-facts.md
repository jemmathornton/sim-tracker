My suggestions must always read as suggestions. On 31 Oct I wrote "the Ferraras then need another flat" as if my homes proposal had already put them at 1313 21 Chic Street, which pushed out the Karaoke Legends, her own idea for that flat. She hadn't decided any of it.

**Why:** she can't tell my proposals from her decisions unless I say which is which. A suggestion stated as fact gets past her without being checked.

**How to apply:** say "my suggestion (not decided)" every time, and say where a fact comes from (her call on a given date, her spreadsheet, a save, or me). Never let a proposal stand in for the plan in a question. Related: [[no-generated-text]], [[whole-picture-first]].
