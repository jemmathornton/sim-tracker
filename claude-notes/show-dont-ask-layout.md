When a question is about where something goes or how it looks (on the library site, 1 Nov 2026), Jemma wants pictures of each option. Asking with words only frustrates her. She said: "show me, stop asking without showing".

**Why:** she reviews by looking, and text descriptions of layouts mean nothing to her until she sees them.

**How to apply:**
- Mock each option in the app (in the browser pane, by moving or adding elements with JS, without changing the code).
- Take a screenshot of each at 1280px wide.
- Send them labelled, then ask which.
- Text-only questions are fine for facts (which edition, which books), not for looks.
