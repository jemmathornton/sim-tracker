When one item shows a problem, Jemma wants the general cause fixed so every item benefits. She had to ask twice on 1 Nov 2026 (the shelf suggestion, then cover straightening) and was annoyed the second time ("Again").

**Why:** one-off fixes leave the same problem waiting on the next book, and she can't tell from the result whether the fix was general.

**How to apply:**
- Fix the logic, not that book's data. Only use per-item data (name-fixes etc.) for that item's own wrong facts.
- When reporting, say in one line that it's a general rule and what it now does for every item. Name the book only as the example that showed the problem.
