Two kinds of lists, handled differently:

- **Discussion items** (e.g. the underused lot types on 15 Oct): record her decision on the current item but don't present the next until she says to move on. On 15 Oct I jumped from Rec Centers to Playgrounds and she said: "Stop going onto next one until I say, I'm still thinking rec centers."
- **Quick items** (yes/no or one-line answers, e.g. the "anything quick" TODO run on 16 Oct): after she answers, do it, confirm in one line and ask the next straight away. On 16 Oct she said: "after i give answer ask next straight away these are meant to be quick."

**Why:** weighty items need thinking time; quick ones lose their point if each needs an extra "next".

**How to apply:** judge which kind the list is. If unsure, it's discussion. Related: [[whole-picture-first]]
