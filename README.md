# Jemma's Sim World

**To open it:** double-click **Jemma's Sim World** on your Desktop (also in the Start menu, and in this folder). It opens in its own window. You can pin it to the taskbar.

**What happens by itself:** every Sunday at 8:54 (or as soon as your PC is next on) the Gallery numbers are refreshed: favourites, downloads and comments for all your uploads. Stats page → "On the Gallery" shows how they're growing.

**To change anything:** open Claude Code in this folder and ask. Your World Plans spreadsheet still works too: say "import my spreadsheet".

**Putting it online later:** the `site` folder is the whole app. It can be uploaded as it is to a free static host whenever you want.

What's in this folder:

- `site` – the app itself
- `data` – everything the app shows (worlds, lots, households, Gallery numbers)
- `tools` – the scripts Claude runs (build, Gallery refresh, spreadsheet import, screenshots)
- `for-jemma` – things for you to look at (gridded maps for placing neighbourhoods)
- `TODO.md` – what's still open, including what's waiting for you
- `live-site`, `old-chat-build`, `old-chat-notes`, `research` – from the claude.ai chat, kept for reference
- `part1/2/3.zip` – already unpacked here; you can delete them

Nothing in your spreadsheet or your other folders was changed.
