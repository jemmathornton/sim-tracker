# Jemma's Sim World

A private website about Jemma's Sims 4 save: every world in the game rebuilt lot by lot, with neighbourhoods, lots, households, her screenshots and her Sims 4 Gallery uploads. It started as a single HTML page built in claude.ai chat (Oct 2026); since 13 Oct it's an app on her PC (a static site she opens from a shortcut), built so it can go online later.

Read `HANDOVER.md` once for the full story, then `TODO.md` for everything still open. This file holds the rules that apply every session.

## Working rules (set by Jemma, keep them)

- **Never edit Jemma's own files without asking first** (the World Plans spreadsheet, her Mods, Builds, Pictures, Documents and Downloads folders), even in auto mode. Copy into the project and work on the copy. Changing this project's own code and data is fine.
- **No AI-generated art anywhere.** Maps come from the 20th Century Plumbob map replacement mod; icons are Lucide (ISC licence), plus a few she picked from Lucide Lab, Tabler and Phosphor (MIT; all noted in site/js/LICENSE-lucide.txt), line icons drawn at 1.5; pictures are Jemma's screenshots or her own Gallery pictures. No AI-upscaled or "Sims-style" stock images either.
- **British English** in everything shown (colours, neighbourhood, favourites). Gallery "likes" are called **favourites**. Proper names from the game keep their spelling (Evergreen Harbor, Information Center).
- **Curly apostrophes** in displayed text; ids, links and data keys stay straight.
- **Short, plain answers.** She reviews by looking at the site, not by reading long reports.
- **Obvious bugs** (wrong numbers, things out of sync, broken behaviour) can be fixed straight away. **Anything needing her judgement** (names, wording, lot types, layout choices) goes on `TODO.md` for her to decide.
- **One Gallery upload per lot, the latest.** Exceptions only for versions she keeps on purpose (Community Space variants, rental + regular versions, `galleryVariants` lots). If copies of a build aren't identical, she chooses.
- **Lot names:** her Gallery title wins, then her own name ("My Name", ignored if just "?"), then the in-game name. The in-game name shows only on the lot page and the Stats sheet.
- **Percentages only on the Stats page.** Elsewhere use counts or words.
- **Bedrooms** count only rooms used as bedrooms (not gyms, music or art rooms).
- **Lot types** use the game's official names (she types shorthand in game because of character limits), except her own British replacements: **Thrift and Boba Store** (not "Bubble Tea") and **UoB Commons** (not "UBrite"). Combos (Park/Cemetery) and her notes in brackets ("Restaurant (Seafood)") stay.
- **World colours** come from the world's map and her builds, never from one side of a world (e.g. Britechester must match neither UoB green nor Foxbury red). The "deep" shades drive the fade at the bottom of each map hero and must look the same as that world's neighbourhood cards.
- **Never guess where a neighbourhood is on a map.** Give her a gridded copy (8 columns A–H × 6 rows 1–6) and let her name the squares.
- **Phone layout is parked** until she asks; laptop (1280px) first.
- **Sims and households (18 Oct):** her spreadsheet says which households exist and where they live; it wins over any save. EA’s premade families come from the game’s own files and the Sims Wiki unless she has said otherwise. Her own households may be filled from any of her saves or her Library where one matches, for now, marked for her to double-check (she has many saves with different versions).
- **Her saves:** only the ones called "Building…" are for lot details, and from them only lots (apart from the Sims rule above). Only lots marked shared get anything from a save (unshared ones may not be finished); her one exception (16 Oct): rentals get their unit names and unit traits even before they’re shared. Saves are read from a temporary copy, never opened in place.
- Notion is no longer used for this project.

## Where things are

- `site/` – **the app**: everything needed to show it, and the only folder to upload if it ever goes online. `index.html`, `site.css` (must stay next to index.html: picture paths in it are read relative to its own folder), `js/app.js` (page code), `js/icons.js` (Lucide and her other picks), `js/data.js` (**generated**, never edit), `maps/`, `shots/` (her screenshots), `gallery/` (Gallery pictures: `<world>/<lot>.jpg`; Community Space versions `<world>/<lot>/<version>-<n>.jpg`), `icon.*`.
- `data/` – **the source of truth** for what the site shows:
  - `worlds.json` (world order) and `worlds/<world>.json`: colours, `heroY` (how far down the world banner sits on its map, 0 top to 100 bottom), blurb, `weather` and `highlights` (from the game), neighbourhoods (`desc`, `mapSpot`, `mapBox` (the box she drew; the card zooms to fit it), `mapCentred` (card doesn't slide away from neighbours), `mapZoom` (card zoomed in closer than its box, e.g. 1.25), `bannerZoom` and `bannerShift` [right, down] (her own nudges to the neighbourhood page banner, as fractions of the map; its card on the world page follows them), `cardShift` [right, down] (a little extra for the card only), `bannerOnlyZoom` (the banner alone, the card stays), and from the game `festivals`, `ecoStart`), lots. Lot fields `lot` (in-game name), `myName`, `type`, `size`, `residents`, `built`, `shared`, `screenshots`, `simsMade` are as in her spreadsheet. App-only lot fields: `displayName` (her spelling beats the Gallery title), `gallery` (Gallery ids of every upload of the lot), `galleryLead` (the copy she chose to lead; otherwise the most popular leads), `galleryOld`, `versions` (Community Space tabs), `beds`/`baths` (apartments uploaded as Rooms), `tenants`, `noLandlord` (a rental whose household rents too, so nobody is shown as landlord), `residentsUnit` (the unit a rental's residents live in, when not unit 1), `statsResidents` (Stats text when a household name isn't enough), `traits`/`challenges` (from her save, shared lots only), `units` (a rental's unit names and traits, from her save, shared lots only). World-level `households` (Sim details) and `familyLinks`. A Sim with a save-wide `id` gets its own page (`#s-<id>`; trial 17 Oct: Goth, Landgraab, Zest, Roomies, Villareal, Bjergsen, Best of Friends, Fletcher), with `sex`, `origin` ("Premade" or "Mine"), `parents` [ids], `partners` [[id, "Spouse"/"Partner"/"Engaged"/"Ex-Spouse"/"Late Spouse"]], `dynasty` {name, role: head/heir/member/outcast}, `clubs` [{name, role: leader/member}], `orgs` [{name, role}] (university organisations), `born` (the family a married-in Sim was born into, e.g. Bella "Bachelor"); family words are worked out, never typed. A household can have `portrait` (her household screenshot in `site/shots/`, shown top left of its page; a placeholder until then), `origin`, `played`, `stories` (Neighbourhood Stories).
  - `people.json`: Sims with no household on the site who appear on family trees (late relatives, townies), same fields plus `world`/`where`, `noHome`, `died`, `galleryOnly` (a Maxis Gallery relative in no world, e.g. Daniel and Mary-Sue Pleasant), and `placeholder` (a parent who isn't on the game's family tree, drawn as an empty box). Anyone on a household's tree gets a Sim page too. `groups.json`: each dynasty’s own details (ideals, prestige, allies, rivals); each club’s `place` (lot id where it meets) or `anyType` (meets at any lot of that type, e.g. "Restaurant", with `world` for where it’s based), `activities`, `rules`, and `others` ([name, role, household id] for members not on the site yet; trial 17 Oct: the Villareals’ and Bjergsens’ nine premade clubs); each university organisation’s `uni` (uob, foxbury, or secret: green and red with gold trim from the game’s own university banners, dark purple), `events`, `join` (trial 17 Oct: Best of Friends and Cameron Fletcher in Britechester; premades start in none, so their organisations are test picks). Groups nobody on the site is in get no page.
  - `calendar.json` (19 Oct): the Calendar page: `seasonWeeks` (4, her choice), `seasons`, `holidays` (hers: {name, season, week 1–4, day Mon–Sun}), `events` (festivals, uni and school events from the game's files: world, neighbourhood, days, time, how often), `lotEvents` (lounge and thrift store nights, made from her lots' types and traits), `surprise`. Weeks start on Monday.
  - `gallery/`: `uploads.json` + `comments.json` (refreshed weekly, don't hand-edit), `history.csv` (a row per upload per refresh), `unlinked.json` (uploads deliberately not on a lot, and why), `text-fixes.json` (typo fixes shown on the site; the build also puts her Gallery descriptions into British spelling, `british()` in tools/build.py).
  - `spreadsheet/last-import.json`: her spreadsheet as last imported (the base for the three-way import).
- `tools/` – the scripts (see below). `tools/one-off/` – the 13 Oct move from the chat, kept for the record (they refuse to run again).
- `not-shown-yet/` – pictures kept out of `site/` because nothing shows them yet (full maps of worlds she doesn't own: move one back to `site/maps/` when she buys the pack).
- `for-jemma/` – things for her to look at (e.g. `map-grids/`).
- `live-site/` – snapshot of the old claude.ai page on 13 Oct (reference). `old-chat-build/` – the chat's generator and data (retired; `live_overrides.json` is folded into `data/`).
- `research/`, `old-chat-notes/` – reference only. `research/data/` holds what the `tools/research/` scripts read from the game and her save.
- Jemma's PC folders that matter (read only): Mods\20thCenturyPlumbob Maps (map packages), Documents\Builds (screenshot folders per build), Downloads (`World Plans (fixed).xlsx` is current; `... - backup 12 Oct.xlsx` is from before the Kopf fix), Sims 4 Screenshots, Pictures, Videos, ansel, Documents\Sims4.

## How to work here

- Python 3.13: `C:/Users/Jemma/AppData/Local/Programs/Python/Python313/python.exe` (not on the shell's PATH). Run scripts with `-X utf8`. Pillow and openpyxl are installed in Python's own folder (not `--user`: the weekly task can't see the user folder). numpy is installed too (16 Oct, her OK; only `extract_map.py` needs it). Ask her before installing anything else.
- To change what the site shows: edit `data/worlds/<world>.json` (or the code in `site/`), then run `tools/build.py` (it stops if a picture is missing, and stamps fingerprints on the code files in index.html so browsers never use stale copies).
- To check: start the `app` preview (`.claude/launch.json`, port 8001), run the contents of `tools/check_pages.js` in the page (expect no problems), and look at the changed pages at 1280px. Add a new `?v=` to the address if the browser shows an old copy.
- Gallery: `tools/gallery_refresh.py` (about 7 minutes; rebuilds the site). Runs by itself every Sunday 08:54 as the Windows task "Jemma's Sim World - weekly Gallery refresh"; log in `logs/gallery-refresh.log`. A new upload is linked to a lot only when its title names exactly one lot; anything else goes in `data/gallery/unlinked.json` for her.
- Sims: `tools/research/premades.py` reads EA’s premade households from the game (`research/data/premades.json`, plus `game-names.json` for trait, career and skill names); `tools/premade_import.py` fills the plan households that match (its MATCHES list), then `tools/premade_family_fix.py --apply` adds the “Unknown” parents that join siblings and cousins. Her own households: `tools/research/own_households.py` finds every copy in her saves and Library (`research/data/own-households.json`); `tools/own_import.py` fills them from the newest copy (Sims get `origin` Mine and `from`) and writes `research/own-households-review.md`. Importers never overwrite a household that already has Sims.
- Her spreadsheet: `tools/import_spreadsheet.py` reports (reads a temporary copy, changes nothing); `--apply` copies her spreadsheet edits into `data/` and rebuilds. App-side changes are never overwritten; clashes are listed. Columns J–M (Lot Traits, Lot Challenges, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, added 15 Oct) are filled from the app and not read back.
- Lot traits and lot challenges: `tools/lot_traits.py` (report; `--apply` writes `traits`/`challenges` on shared lots and rebuilds; `--game` re-reads trait names after a game update). It reads her newest "Building" save and the game's own files through `tools/sims4files.py` (package files, save layout, English text); names are cached in `data/game/lot-traits.json`. Lots named differently in her save go in its `SAVE_NAMES` list, only once she has confirmed them.
- Research from the game's own files (report only; the one exception is `world_extras.py --apply`, which copies weather, highlights, festivals and eco start into `data/`): `tools/research/game_lots.py` and `world_files.py` (every lot's name, description, size and area), `game_venues.py` (lot type names), `plan_vs_game.py` (her plan against them, plus lot types from her newest Building save and the Gallery; shared lots only), `world_extras.py` (weather, festivals, starting eco footprint, highlights; `tuning.py` reads the game's tuning), `data_check.py` (plan vs Gallery vs game vs save, gaps), `gallery_deep_dive.py` (what does best on her Gallery), `community_mix.py` (how many of each community lot type, and the everyday places each world is missing), `world_lot_types.py` (which lot types each world allows). Write-ups in `research/*.md`; one note per world in `research/worlds/` (16 Oct; Evergreen Harbor, Tomarang, Nordhaven and Ondarion are in `research/` itself), and `research/not-owned-worlds.md`. Rerun after a game update or a new pack.
- Library pictures (19 Oct): `tools/library_pictures.py` (report; `--apply` writes `site/library/` and `data/library-pictures.json`, then rebuilds) uses her own Library's household and Sim pictures as stand-ins until she has screenshots (`portrait` always wins). `tools/tray_pictures.py` decodes the game's picture files (an XOR'd JPEG plus a PNG mask). Rerun after she saves households to her Library.
- Screenshots: `tools/screenshots.py` (a new Builds folder needs a line in its FOLDERS list). Maps: `tools/extract_map.py`. Neighbourhood map spots: she answers on `tools/map-squares.html` (clickable, opens straight from the folder) or from the pictures `tools/map_grids.py` makes in `for-jemma/map-grids/`; `tools/set_map_spots.py` puts her answers into `data/` as `mapSpot`. App icon: `tools/make_icons.py`; shortcuts: `tools/make_shortcuts.ps1`.
- The project is a git repo: commit after each piece of work (`data.js` included, so the folder always works as it is).
