## How things work

- **Gallery numbers and comments** are a snapshot from 11 Oct. Refreshing them needs you signed in to EA in the Claude desktop app's browser.
- **Screenshots**: add them to your build folders; Claude copies them in. The site holds at most 511 files, so new pictures go into the site's picture storage (up to 5,000).
- **Maps** come from your 20th Century Plumbob files in the Mods folder.
- **Edits to your own files** only happen after you say yes.
- **The site is private**: only you can open it unless you share it.
