# Who lives where: a proposal (28–29 Oct 2026)

Suggestions only. Nothing on the site has changed.

**Already decided, left alone:**
- Kenji and Kumiko at 2-4-3 Wakabamori
- Kamilla and Chris at Rippling Flats after their degrees
- the Everyday Sims 4 elders at Rio Verde
- the Cadberrys and Rochanas (later)
- your Sulani split
- every home that already has someone named

**How this was checked:**
- **Packs:** you own every expansion except Lovestruck, Enchanted by Nature and Werewolves; no Vampires or My Wedding Stories game packs; and only some kits. Sims needing a pack you lack can't come with their occult or pack items.
- **Bedrooms:** from your own Gallery uploads (“beds”). Unbuilt homes can be built to fit.
- **Lore:** from the Sims Wiki and Maxis's Gallery descriptions.

**The squeeze:** about 45 empty homes in worlds you own, plus about 15 free rental units. That's enough for all your own Sims, the lore families and the best story Sims, but not for every trailer family. Each home has one pick, with alternatives after it.
