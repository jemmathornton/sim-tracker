Lot prototypes

Two page ideas drawn with the site’s own look and your real lots. Each page has lettered options to pick from, and a short list of questions at the bottom. Nothing here changes the site.

Empty homes and free units Every empty home, apartment and free rental unit in your owned worlds, by world and neighbourhood. Option A a compact table, option B cards, side by side.Open Filters A world’s lots under chips for pack, lot type, lot trait, built and shared, and empty or lived in, with a count on every chip. Option A chips always showing, option B one folded bar. The same chips on a Stats-style sheet.Open

Both pages read the site’s own data (lots as of the last build, Gallery numbers as of the last refresh), so they stay in step with the site until it changes. Lot names, types and households follow the site’s rules.
