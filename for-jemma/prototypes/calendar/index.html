What’s on

Every festival, holiday and regular event in your owned worlds, read from the game’s own files. Three ways to show it, side by side. Nothing here changes the site.

Lots and neighbourhoods from the site’s data, as of the last build.
Where this comes from. Days, times and how often from the game’s own settings (tuning) and the times it shows on festival signs and notice boards, checked against the Sims Wiki. Lot nights are worked out from your plan’s lot types and lot traits, so they follow the plan. Holidays assume the game’s default one-week seasons. Full write-up: research/festivals-calendar.md.
