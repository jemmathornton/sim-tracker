    Tips

    1. The title is what name search reads. Titles that say the place and what it is (Oasis Springs Library, Anchorpoint Rec Center) come up in the top five.
    2. Core hashtags first, extras after. The description stops at 500 characters. Core: the lot’s name, the game’s lot name, its neighbourhood, its world, what it is (#library, #communityspace, #vacationrental…), #nocc, #playtested.
    3. Small, specific tags are where you can be near the top: the neighbourhood and the game’s lot name. Busy ones (#library, #oasissprings) mostly help in the first days.
    4. Drop the rare twins: #playtested, #moo and #under20k are far busier than #playertested, #moveobjectson and #under20000.
    5. #cosy and #cozy reach different searches; #cozy is the busier one.
    6. #basegame only when no pack is needed; a “packonly” tag (#seasonsonly) when just one pack is.
    7. Holiday lets: add #vacationrental. The Gallery shows them as Residential.
    8. Set the lot type and bedrooms and bathrooms before saving. That’s what the filters and the Gallery page show.
    9. Daytime picture, with the build in view. Pause the game when the light is right, then save.
    10. Maxis picks go to builds with no custom content that look right without move objects.
